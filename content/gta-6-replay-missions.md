# Can You Replay Missions in GTA 6? No, Save First

> - **Quick answer:** **No.** You cannot replay a finished story mission from the GTA 6 menu, not even after the story ends (GameSpot, IGN).
> - **The way back:** load an older **manual save**. GTA 6 has **15 save slots** (GameSpot, IGN). GameSpot says the autosave uses one of them; IGN and PowerPyx describe 15 manual slots plus the autosave.
> - **Best tip:** make a new manual save before every big mission. You cannot save during a mission (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** A *mission* is a story job with a goal. *Replay* means playing it again. A *save slot* is one space where the game keeps your progress. *Autosave* means the game saves by itself. A *manual save* is one you make yourself.

*This page has no story spoilers. It explains menus and saves only.*

## How to go back to a mission

1. Make a **manual save** before the mission starts. GameSpot says you can save at any time from the options menu, but not during a mission.
2. Pick an **empty slot**, or one you do not need.
3. Play the mission.
4. Want to play it again later? Open the menu.
5. Load the save you made in step 1.

The exact PS5 and Xbox buttons for the save menu are **not announced** in the sources we checked.

IGN's tip: make a new save about every **7%** of the story. Then you are never far from a point you may want back.

## Mission going badly? Retry, quit or restart

1. Open the menu **during** the mission.
2. Choose one of the three options GameSpot lists: **retry**, **quit** or **restart**.

Need a break? GameSpot says opening the menu or the map **pauses** the mission.

## Save-slot tips

- 15 slots is not a lot for a long game. IGN says the main story takes **50 to 100 hours**.
- Do not save over your only save from before a big choice.
- Keep the autosave in mind. It always replaces the last autosave.

## FAQ

### Can you replay missions in GTA 6?
No. Not from the menu. Load an older manual save to play a mission again (GameSpot, IGN).

### How many save slots does GTA 6 have?
15 (GameSpot, IGN). GameSpot says the autosave uses one of them; IGN and PowerPyx describe 15 manual slots plus the autosave.

### Can I save during a mission?
No. GameSpot says you can only save when you are not on a mission.

### Can I keep playing after the story?
Yes. GameSpot says you can keep playing after the last story mission.

### Will Rockstar add mission replay?
Not announced. IGN says it does not know if it will come after launch.
