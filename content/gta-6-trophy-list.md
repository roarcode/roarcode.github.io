# GTA 6 Trophy List: All 55 Trophies (Spoiler Guide)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has **55 trophies**: **1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze**. Xbox has the same 55 as achievements (Insider Gaming).
> - **28 of them are hidden**, and many hidden ones are tied to the story. So every trophy name on this page sits inside a closed box.
> - **Best tip:** there is no mission replay. Make manual saves as you go, or you may need a second playthrough.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** this guide lists trophy names that can give away story moments. Tap a box only if you want to know.

**Words used on this page.** A *trophy* (PS5) or *achievement* (Xbox) is a reward badge the game gives you for doing something. The *Platinum* is the PS5 badge for getting all the others. A *hidden* trophy keeps its name secret until you earn it. *Missable* means you can lose the chance to get it. *100% completion* means finishing the game's full checklist. *Manual save* means a save you make yourself, not the automatic one.

Want the big rewards without any story spoilers? Read the tips first and leave the boxes closed. The count, the rarity mix and the save advice are all safe.

## Save first: there is no mission replay

1. **Know the rule.** You cannot replay a finished mission from the mission list. IGN's review and GameSpot both say so (18 Nov).
2. **Make a manual save before big missions.** GameSpot says you cannot save while a mission is running.
3. **Use more than one slot.** GameSpot says there are **15 save slots**. The autosave uses one slot and writes over itself.
4. **If a mission goes wrong, use the pause menu.** GameSpot says it offers **retry, quit or restart** during a mission.
5. **Do not turn on cheats on your main save.** PowerPyx says cheats turn off trophies and saving.

The exact button to open the save screen is **not announced** on official pages yet. We will add the PS5 and Xbox buttons once we can confirm them.

## FAQ

### How many trophies are in GTA 6?
55: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. 28 are hidden.

### Is the GTA 6 Platinum hard?
PowerPyx rates it 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours. Most of the time goes into the 100% completion trophy.

### Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?
Possibly one, according to PowerPyx. Open the "one trophy you might miss" box above. Saving often also helps, because missions cannot be replayed.

### Do cheats disable trophies in GTA 6?
Yes, PowerPyx says cheats turn off trophies and saving.

### Are there online trophies?
No. PowerPyx lists 0 online trophies.
