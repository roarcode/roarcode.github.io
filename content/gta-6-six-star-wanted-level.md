# GTA 6 Six-Star Wanted Level: How to Get It (and Survive)

> - **Quick answer:** six stars needs mass destruction and a long chase. PC Gamer got it by **stealing a tank and blowing things up** (PC Gamer). GameSpot got it by **evading police for about 20 minutes** (GameSpot).
> - **Warning:** at six stars you cannot lose your wanted level. PC Gamer says the law will never stop hunting you.
> - **Save first.** Make a manual save before you try.

**Words used on this page.** *Wanted level* is the star rating that shows how much the police want you. *Evade* means to stay away from someone chasing you. *Criminal profile* is the game's record of how violent you are. *Armoured vehicle* is a heavy police truck. *Manual save* is a save you make yourself.

*This page has no story spoilers. It is a bad-idea guide, so use it at your own risk.*

## Why this is hard

GameSpot says that even a lot of chaos in a short time gets you only four or five stars. It says you cannot start at six stars unless the story does it, so you must climb the levels yourself.

## Route 1: the chase (GameSpot)

1. **Get to three stars.** GameSpot says the quickest way is to kill a police officer. Three stars show up when the police scanner says an officer is down.
2. **Stay close** to where it happened so the cops can describe you and keep chasing (GameSpot).
3. **Keep the chase going.** GameSpot says you should be at or near five stars after about **10 minutes** of being chased.
4. **Keep going for 10 to 15 more minutes.** GameSpot says a notice tells you that you will soon earn a sixth star if you keep escaping.
5. GameSpot says the whole chase is nearly **20 minutes**.

GameSpot's tips for surviving:

- Bring both characters. It gives better odds and more options (GameSpot).
- Use a tough vehicle and automatic weapons, such as an assault rifle or an SMG (GameSpot).
- Leave downtown Vice City. Narrow roads make roadblocks easy for the police. GameSpot says the highway north or west is better.
- Watch out for armoured vehicles that drive head-on at you (GameSpot).

## Route 2: the military route (PC Gamer)

PC Gamer says the only way its team reached six stars was by **stealing a tank from a military base and blowing up other tanks and vehicles**. It says you need mass destruction and loss of life.

GameSpot also says that breaking into Vice City Airport or an air station west of the starting area raises your wanted level to four or five stars straight away. It warns that those places are very hard to enter and have air support and tanks, so your chances of surviving are low.

PC Gamer says the highest star levels are for stealing a passenger jet at an airport or hijacking an APC at a military base, as in earlier games. It adds that five stars brings a tank.

The two outlets do not agree on one exact method. Both agree it needs a lot of destruction.

## What happens at six stars

- You can no longer lose heat or your wanted level. Police and the military will not stop hunting you (PC Gamer).
- PC Gamer says your only way out is to die.
- Killing that many cops can hurt your criminal profile badly (PC Gamer).
- GameSpot says the areas involved stay closely watched until you are caught or killed. You need to change your clothes, look and vehicle to go back.

## FAQ

### How do you get six stars in GTA 6?
It takes mass destruction. PC Gamer used a stolen tank. GameSpot evaded police for about 20 minutes.

### Can you lose six stars?
PC Gamer says no.

### Is it worth it?
That is up to you. Both outlets call it very hard, and the cost is high.
