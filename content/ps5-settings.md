# GTA 6 on PS5 and PS5 Pro: What Is Confirmed (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 targets **30 FPS** (30 pictures per second) on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. **No 60 FPS or performance mode is announced.**
> - **PS5 Pro adds two things:** **PSSR** (a sharper picture) and **ray-traced shadows** (more realistic shadows). Both are PS5 Pro only.
> - **Set up now:** keep **at least 212 GB** free, run **HDR setup**, and use **Game Mode** on your TV. In-game options are **not announced**. We will update this page after launch.

**Words used on this page.** *FPS* means frames per second, how smooth the picture is. *HDR* means brighter lights and deeper darks on your TV. *Haptics* means the controller buzzing to match the action. *VRR* is a TV setting that smooths the picture when the speed changes. *SSD* is the fast storage inside the PS5. *Tempest 3D Audio* is the PS5's way of making sound come from all around you.

## Step 1: Check your free space (at least 212 GB)

1. Press the **PS button**, then open **Settings**.
2. Select **Storage**.
3. Check you have **at least 212 GB** free.

![PS5 storage screen](TODO: image:ps5-settings-step-2 — menu screen, PS5: Settings > Storage with free space visible)
*Caption: Diagram. The PS5 storage screen showing free space.*

## Step 2: Set up HDR (general PS5 tip, not a GTA 6 setting)

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Select **Screen and Video**.
3. Select **Video Output**.
4. Select **Adjust HDR**.
5. Follow the on-screen steps.

## Step 3: Pick the right TV settings

1. Open your TV's picture menu.
2. Turn on **Game Mode** (name varies by TV). This lowers delay between button press and picture.
3. Our reading: 120 Hz will **not** make GTA 6 faster, because the game targets 30 FPS.
4. VRR (a setting that smooths the picture on some TVs): GTA 6 support is not announced. Our reading: leave it on **Automatic** for now.

## Step 4: Change the controller buzz

1. Start GTA 6 once it unlocks.
2. Open the game's **Settings** and look for **Accessibility**.
3. Change the haptics (buzz) and adaptive triggers (stiff buttons) there. Rockstar says they are adjustable in accessibility settings. The exact menu names are not announced yet.

## Xbox Series X|S: what is known

- Xbox Series X and Series S run it natively. Rockstar gave no Xbox-only graphics options.
- **Resolution (Rockstar via Wccftech, 18 Nov):** Series X starts at **1440p** and goes up to 4K. Series S starts at **720p** and goes up to **1080p** with AMD FSR 4. Both target 30 FPS. More in [GTA 6 performance mode](/launch-hub/gta-6-performance-mode/).
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming** (streaming) uses **Xbox Series S graphics settings**, per Rockstar and Xbox Wire.
