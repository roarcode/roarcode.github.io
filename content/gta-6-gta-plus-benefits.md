# GTA 6 GTA+ Benefits: Free Car, 10% Off and How to Turn Off

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 GTA+ benefits in story mode are a **free car and garage**, **10% off** in many shops, **free rides**, and **10% more cash** when you sell some cars (GameSpot, IGN).
> - **Price:** **$7.99 a month** in the US (Polygon). Pre-order buyers get **1 month free**, then it renews unless you cancel (GameSpot, IGN).
> - **Catch:** the perks only work **while you pay**. Cancel and you lose them, car included (GameSpot, Polygon).

**Words used on this page.** *GTA+* is Rockstar's monthly club that you pay for. A *perk* is a bonus you get for being in the club. A *garage* is a place in the game where you keep a car. *Fence* means selling stolen things. *Auto-renew* means the club keeps charging you every month until you stop it.

## The 4 GTA+ perks in GTA 6 story mode

| Perk | What you get | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Car and garage** | A '90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy with a special wide body, plus a one-car garage with a weapon locker. You can also sell stolen things there | GameSpot, IGN |
| **Getting around** | Scooter Bros Premium, premium RydeMe (a ride app in the game), free scooter, bike and Seashark rentals, and free public transport | GameSpot, Polygon |
| **Shop discount** | **10% off** at some weapon shops, clothes shops, hairdressers, tattoo shops and car mod shops | GameSpot, Polygon |
| **Sell cars for more** | **10% more cash** when you sell an eligible car you own | GameSpot, Polygon |

## How to turn the GTA+ boosts off

Want to play the game without the extra help? Polygon says you can switch the boosts off:

1. Press **Options** on PS5, or **Menu** on Xbox, to open the pause menu (general controller button).
2. Go to the **Account** tab.
3. Find **GTA Plus Boosts**.
4. Turn it **off**.

## Price and the free month

- **Price:** **$7.99 a month** in the US (Polygon). GameSpot says about $8.
- **Free month:** digital pre-order buyers get **1 month of GTA+ free**. On PS5 you must claim it by **31 March 2027**, and it then renews at the normal price ([Rockstar Report](https://rockstarreport.com/news/gta-6-gta-plus-story-mode-benefits), [IGN](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/277365/gta-6-how-microtransactions-and-gta-benefits-work)). See [the Vintage Vice City Pack page](/launch-hub/vintage-vice-city-pack/) for how to claim it.
- **If you stop paying:** you lose every perk, including the car and the garage (GameSpot, Polygon).

## How to stop GTA+ before you are charged

Set a reminder a few days before your free month ends. Then:

1. **PS5:** go to **Settings > Users and Accounts > Account > Payment and Subscriptions > Subscriptions**, pick **GTA+** and turn off auto-renew (general PS5 menu path, check on your console).
2. **Xbox:** go to **account.microsoft.com/services** in a web browser, find **GTA+** and select **Cancel** or turn off recurring billing (general Microsoft account path).

## Is GTA+ worth it in GTA 6?

It is your choice. The perks are small helpers, not a big change (IGN). Some players do not like a paid club in a single-player game. A popular Reddit post about it got thousands of upvotes, Polygon reports. If you only want the free month, enjoy it and set that reminder.

## FAQ

### What do you get with GTA+ in GTA 6?
A free car and garage, travel perks, 10% off in many shops and 10% more cash for some car sales (GameSpot).

### How much is GTA+?
$7.99 a month in the US (Polygon).

### Do you keep the GTA+ car if you cancel?
No. GameSpot and Polygon say the perks, car included, stop when your membership ends.

Yes. Pause menu > Account > GTA Plus Boosts (Polygon).
