# GTA 6 Error Codes: What Each One Means and How to Fix It

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 error code? **Write down the whole code, then find it in the tables below.** No GTA 6 error codes are confirmed yet. We add each one when Rockstar, PlayStation or Xbox confirms it.
> - **First check:** [Rockstar Service Status](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus), [PlayStation status](https://status.playstation.com) and [Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status).
> - **Do not guess:** a code from another game does not always mean the same thing here.

**Words used on this page.** An *error code* is a short mix of letters and numbers that names a problem. *Platform code* means a code made by PlayStation or Xbox, not by the game.

## How to read a code

1. Write down the **full code**, including the letters. Example shape: two letters, a dash, then numbers.
2. Note **where** you saw it: store, download, start-up or in-game.
3. Note the **time** and your platform. PlayStation Support asks for any error code, a description and a timestamp.

## Platform codes (PlayStation and Xbox)

Codes that start with **CE-** or **NP-** on PS5 come from PlayStation, not from GTA 6. PlayStation has a page for each one at playstation.com/en-us/support/error-codes/. For Xbox codes, use Xbox Support.
