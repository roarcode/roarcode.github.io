# GTA 6 Scam Sites: 5 Signs a Page Is Fake (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 scam sites are real. Avast checked **more than 200** new GTA 6 web addresses and rated **about 1 in 10** as medium or high risk (INSERT FUTURE, Eurogamer.de).
> - **Safe places to buy:** only the **PlayStation Store**, the **Microsoft Store on Xbox**, or a shop you already know. GTA 6 is on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** only.
> - **Biggest red flag:** a "free" or "PC" download of GTA 6. Rockstar has not announced a PC version, so it is a trap (Avast, via La Vanguardia).

**Words used on this page.** A *scam site* is a fake web page that wants your money or your password. *Phishing* means a fake page that looks real so you type in your login. A *crypto wallet* is an app that holds digital money. A *domain* is a web address, like `example[.]com`.

## 5 signs a GTA 6 page is fake

1. **It offers GTA 6 for PC.** Rockstar has only announced PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A PC download now is a red flag (Avast's Luis Corrons, via La Vanguardia).
2. **It offers the game free or early.** Pre-load (downloading early from the real store) is not early access. A page that says "play now, free" is lying (La Vanguardia).
3. **It asks you to connect a crypto wallet.** Avast says this is a warning sign. Connecting can lead to losing money if you then approve a payment (Eurogamer.de, INSERT FUTURE).
4. **It asks for your PlayStation, Xbox or Rockstar password.** Never type it into a giveaway or "leak" page (INSERT FUTURE).
5. **The web address looks a little off.** Check the address before you log in or pay. Do not trust ads or social media links (Eurogamer.de).

A real Rockstar logo does not make a page real. Avast says fake shops copy official art (La Vanguardia).

## How to buy GTA 6 safely

**PS5**

1. Press the **PS button**.
2. Open **PlayStation Store** from the home screen.
3. Search for **Grand Theft Auto VI**.

**Xbox Series X|S**

1. Press the **Xbox button**.
2. Open **Store** (general Xbox menu path, check on your console).
3. Search for **Grand Theft Auto VI**.

Buying a box in a real shop is fine too. The box holds a **code**, not a disc (PlayStation's GTA VI support page). Type the code only into your console's store.

## What a fake "GTA 6 PC download" really puts on your computer

Security companies opened these fake downloads to see what is inside. It was never the game. A *stealer* is a program that secretly copies your saved passwords and logins.

| Who checked | What the fake file did | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Malwarebytes (Aug 2026) | Fake "GTA 6 demo" sites gave out a file of only **1.1 MB**. It was a password stealer | [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/threat-intel/2026/08/fake-gta-6-extended-look-and-demo-sites-deliver-an-infostealer) |
| Malwarebytes (Sep 2026) | A fake "leaked copy" shop tried to empty visitors' crypto wallets | [Malwarebytes](https://www.malwarebytes.com/blog/scams/2026/09/fake-gta-6-leaked-copy-drains-your-crypto-wallet) |
| Cynet | A "free PC download" ZIP held a program under **1 MB** that steals passwords, game logins and crypto | [Cynet](https://www.cynet.com/blog/download-gta6-for-free-inside-the-malware-trap/) |
| NordVPN (16 Nov 2026) | Criminals sell a kit with fake PC downloads and a fake "keygen" that steals logins | [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/vpn/vpn-privacy-security/fake-gta-6-pc-downloads-are-spreading-malware-through-dark-web-scam-bundle-nordvpn-warns) |

**Easy test:** a big game like GTA 6 is far, far bigger than a few MB. Malwarebytes says a 1.1 MB file "is nowhere near enough" to hold a modern game, so it cannot be GTA 6.

Never open a GTA 6 installer, crack or keygen on a PC. If you already did, use a different, clean device to change your passwords, then run a virus scan.

## I already typed my password. What now?

1. Change that password on the real site right away.
2. Turn on 2-step verification (a second code when you log in) for your PlayStation, Xbox and Rockstar accounts.
3. If you paid, call your bank.
4. Download or code problems: PlayStation's GTA VI support page sends you to **PlayStation Support** for account, code and download issues.
