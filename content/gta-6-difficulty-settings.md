# GTA 6 Difficulty Settings: Is There Easy Mode? (No, But...)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has **no difficulty options**. GameSpot says it can confirm there is nothing like easy, medium or hard.
> - **What you can change:** the aiming controls in the settings menus (GameSpot).
> - **Also:** an accessibility toggle that cuts enemy health, damage you take and vehicle damage (Can I Play That).

**Words used on this page.** *Difficulty* is how hard a game is. *Accessibility* means options that make a game easier to use for more people. *Assist* is help the game gives you, like aiming help. *QTE* (quick-time event) is a moment where you must press a button fast. *Aim assist* helps your aim stick to targets. *Toggle* is a switch you can turn on or off.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## What is confirmed

GameSpot says that after getting its hands on the game, it can confirm there are **no difficulty options** to choose from. It says this fits Rockstar's habit of one set difficulty for the main campaign.

GameSpot also says you can still change how hard it feels yourself:

1. Open the **settings menus**.
2. Change the **aiming controls** to what suits you (GameSpot).
3. After you finish the campaign once, cheat codes unlock, and they can make the game easier or harder (GameSpot).

## What early accessibility reports list

Can I Play That, a site about accessible gaming, says Rockstar has not given review copies to accessibility-focused outlets. So its post is an early look based on what it could find, and it says it is "both incomplete, and some details might be off". Treat these as early reports, not a full list.

| Area | What Can I Play That says |
|---|---|
| Making fights easier | A toggle to reduce enemy health, the damage you take and the damage your vehicle takes |
| Buttons | Five levels of tap assist for QTEs, hold assists, hold-to-toggle options and hold delay settings |
| Minigames | They can be simplified |
| Driving and shooting | Auto drive, and toggles that help with drive-bys and shooting |
| Aim | Aim assist can be adjusted |
| Sound | Resizable subtitles and captions, a tinnitus-reduction toggle, mono audio and separate volume sliders |
| Sight | Menu narration, audio descriptions, a high contrast mode, a colourblind mode for the focus trail and adjustable text and map icon sizes |
| Comfort | Motion blur and head bob options, and options for blood and gore |

Can I Play That says there are three default controller presets and it could not find confirmation that you can remap buttons. It says sprinting moved to **L3**, which can be changed.

## Steps to make GTA 6 easier

1. Open the settings menus and look for the **accessibility** and **aiming** sections (GameSpot, Can I Play That).
2. Turn on the toggle that reduces damage if you find fights too hard (Can I Play That).
3. Turn on aim assist and drive-by help if you want help shooting (Can I Play That).
4. Turn on tap assists if button mashing is hard for you (Can I Play That).
5. We have not tested these ourselves. Names of menu items may differ.

Change aiming controls (GameSpot) and use the assists that early reports describe (Can I Play That).

### Can I remap buttons?
Can I Play That could not find confirmation that you can.
