# GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist: 35 Tasks (Spoilers)

> - **Quick answer:** Insider Gaming counts **35 tasks** on the 100% checklist, and the Platinum trophy needs it.
> - **You do not have to do everything.** For example 70 challenges of 80, and 100 animals of over 160 (Insider Gaming).
> - **The list opens after the credits** (Insider Gaming), so we keep it in closed boxes.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** the checklist shows up after the story. Insider Gaming says nothing on it gives away the plot, but the list is behind boxes so you can choose.

**Words used on this page.** *100% completion* means finishing the game's full checklist. *Platinum* is the top trophy on PS5. *Fenced* means sold to a fence, a buyer of stolen goods. *Stunt* is a daring move, like a big jump. *Gold medal* is the best grade in a race or at a range. *Drone time trial* is a race flown with a small drone.

## What you need to know first

1. The checklist appears after you roll the credits on the main story, with a tracker for each item (Insider Gaming).
2. The Platinum trophy needs 100% completion (Insider Gaming).
3. Not every list needs to be finished. Some need only part: 70 challenges (of 80 in total) and 100 animals (of over 160) (Insider Gaming).
4. VGC says all the scan and collect challenges, if you did them all, would push the game past 200 hours.

## Tips to get started

1. Play the story first. The checklist only shows up after the credits (Insider Gaming).
