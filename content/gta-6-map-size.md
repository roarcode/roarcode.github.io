# GTA 6 Map Size: What Rockstar Actually Said (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 map size: **Rockstar has not published a number in miles or km.** The two claims we have are "about 2x GTA 5" and "about 3x Red Dead Redemption 2".
> - **Who said it:** 2x GTA 5 is from Aaron Garbut (Rockstar North) in Game Informer's cover story, reported by Forbes and Mapheist. 3x RDR2 is reported by GameSpot.
> - **Careful:** every km or mile figure you see online is a fan estimate. Real measuring starts after launch.

**Words used on this page.** *Map size* is how much land the game world covers. *km²* means square kilometres. *Estimate* means a guess based on maths, not a number from Rockstar.

## What we know about the GTA 6 map size

| Claim | Who said it | How sure |
|---|---|---|
| About **2x GTA 5's map** | Aaron Garbut (Rockstar North) in Game Informer's cover story, 29 Sep 2026. Forbes and Mapheist report it. | A developer statement, reported by outlets |
| About **3x Red Dead Redemption 2's map** | GameSpot reports it. | Reported by an outlet |
| Area in km² or miles | Nobody. | **Not announced** |

The two claims use different games as the yardstick. Do not mix them into one number.

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** a community-made map (YANIS v16) measures Leonida's land at about **85.6 km²**. Notebookcheck relayed it and Mapheist summarised it. It is a fan estimate, not a Rockstar number. Source: https://mapheist.com/en/news/gta-6-game-informer-cover-story/

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** Forbes writes **58.6 square miles**. That is the writer's own sum (twice GTA 5's area), not a Rockstar figure. Source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/brianmazique/2026/10/06/gta-6s-map-is-about-twice-the-size-of-gta-5-with-hurricanes-and-170-animal-species/

The two rumour numbers do not match (58.6 square miles is about 152 km²; the fan map says 85.6 km²). That is why we do not pick one.

## Why a bigger map is not always better

A map can be big and still feel small, or the other way round. Size alone does not tell you how much there is to do. The playtime estimate in GameSpot's recap is about 80 hours for a player focused on the main story and story-based extras (reported, not official). How long it takes to cross the map is **not announced**.
