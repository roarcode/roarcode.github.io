# GTA 6 Bigfoot Location: Where to Find Him (Spoiler-Light)

> - **Quick answer:** GameRant and Insider Gaming both put Bigfoot at **Cottonmouth Lake**. Insider Gaming says it is just below Hamlet. You can go after your first mission (GameRant).
> - **Clue:** you hear a **deep, guttural growl** when you are close (Insider Gaming).
> - **The outlets do not all agree on the exact spot**, so read the list below.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** the area is safe to read. What happens when you meet Bigfoot is inside a closed box. Tap it only if you want to know.

**Words used on this page.** *Easter egg* is a hidden surprise in a game. *Bigfoot* (also called the *Skunk Ape*) is a tall, hairy creature from legends. *Effigy* is a model of a person. *Cutscene* is a short movie inside the game. *Marsh* is wet, low land with reeds.

## Where to go

Several outlets say where to look. They are close, but not the same:

| Outlet | Where it says to look |
|---|---|
| GameRant | South of Cottonmouth Lake, across from your starting area in Chapter 1 |
| Insider Gaming | Cottonmouth Lake, just below Hamlet |
| GameSpot | Gulley Marsh |
| GamerHour | South of Grassrivers, near the bridge to Cottonmouth |
| Balls.ie | In the south, in and around Grassrivers. It says one player gave a spot near Watson Bay |

So the common idea is the **south marsh and lake area**. If you do not find him at the first place, try the next one.

## When can you go?

GameRant says you can go as soon as your first mission is done, with no time lock. Insider Gaming says you do not need to progress far through the main story.

## How to know you are close

Insider Gaming says you will know you are in the right area when you hear a deep, guttural growl that is "truly hard to miss". Balls.ie says a streamer heard roars when getting into a car.

1. Drive to the lake and marsh area.
2. Listen for the growl.
3. Follow the sound.
4. Go on foot when the road ends.

:::spoiler What happens when you find him? (reveals an easter egg encounter)
GameRant says Bigfoot is eating a deer carcass up a footpath, and the encounter forces you off your vehicle to chase him into the woods. It says you cannot catch him and your bullets will not hurt him.

Insider Gaming found him on the road standing over an animal and says he quickly runs off into the marshland. Insider Gaming says a strange effigy is tied to a nearby tree. GameRant says that further into the woods, at a marked spot, there is what seems to be a human effigy, and that inspecting it makes you fall unconscious and Bigfoot takes you back to your vehicle. Insider Gaming blames flowers nearby and says you see his big hairy feet in a short cutscene.

Both GameRant and Insider Gaming say there may be more to this. GameSpot's easter egg list says Bigfoot is unmissable because of a particular mission, so GameSpot sees it as a mission moment, not only a secret.
:::
