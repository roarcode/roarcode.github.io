# GTA 6 Weather Not Changing? PS5 Cache Fix and Patch Status

> - **Quick answer:** If the GTA 6 weather never changes on **PS5**, players say **clearing the system software cache in Safe Mode** brings rain and storms back (IGN, Insider Gaming).
> - **Affects:** PS5 and PS5 Pro reports after update **01.002.002**. Rockstar's patch notes list a weather fix. The cache trick is **not** an official fix.
> - **Status: unconfirmed workaround**, checked 2026-11-26. Many players report it works; IGN says it could be a placebo. Not tested by us.

**Words used on this page.** A *glitch* is a mistake in the game. A *patch* is an update that fixes mistakes. *Safe Mode* is a special start-up menu on the PS5 for fixing problems. *Cache* is a store of temporary files the console can throw away and make again.

*Story mode, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No mods or PC tools.*

## Symptoms

- The sky stays clear and sunny for many in-game days.
- You almost never see rain, fog or a storm, even after many hours.

## First check: is it really a bug?

1. **Update the game.** Rockstar's [23 Nov patch notes](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/grand-theft-auto-vi-launch-updates-ps5-xbox-series-x-or-s) list "an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods" as fixed. That is update 01.002.002.
2. **Check your chapter.** Rockstar's Owen Shepherd told [IGN](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/278006/gta-6-weather-still-not-varied-even-after-the-patch-ps5-players-are-sharing-a-method-that-may-help-r) the weather is mostly good in the first chapter and gets more stormy later.
3. **Look around.** Rain is local. IGN quotes Rockstar that you can miss the early pockets of rain.

## Fixes, fastest first

### Fix 1: Clear the PS5 system software cache (about 2 minutes)

This trick was shared by [GameRoll on X](https://x.com/GameRoll_/status/2125764422371029015) on 26 Nov. [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/fix-weather-bug-gta-6-rain-storms/) credits GameRoll and the X user mauricioce85796, who first raised it.

1. In GTA 6, **save your game**. Then close the game.
2. **Turn the PS5 fully off.** Do not use Rest Mode.
3. **Press and hold the power button.** Let go after the **second beep**, about 7 seconds (Insider Gaming).
4. **Plug in your controller with a USB cable.** Press the **PS button**.
5. Pick **Clear Cache and Rebuild Database** (option 6).
6. Pick **Clear System Software Cache**. Select **OK**.
7. Wait for the PS5 to restart. Start GTA 6 and load your save.

**Do not pick any reset option** lower in the Safe Mode menu. Those can wipe the console. Clearing the cache does not delete your saves or games (IGN, [PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/hardware/safe-mode-playstation/)).

The weather may not change at once. Play for a while and drive to a new area (Insider Gaming).

### Fix 2: Fully close the game and restart the console (about 3 minutes)

1. Save your game.
2. **PS5:** press the **Options button** on GTA 6 in the home screen, then **Close Game**. **Xbox:** press the **Xbox button**, highlight GTA 6, press the **Menu button**, then **Quit**.
3. Restart the console. Load your save.

[PlayStationInfo](https://www.playstationinfo.de/en/2026/11/26/gta-6-only-shows-sunshine-for-many-players-and-a-simple-ps5-trick-could-fix-the-problem/) suggests this for Xbox players, where the cache trick is not tested.

## FAQ

### Does clearing the PS5 cache delete my GTA 6 save?
No. IGN says clearing the system software cache does not delete saves or games. Do not pick the reset options lower in the Safe Mode menu.

### Did Rockstar fix the GTA 6 weather bug?
Rockstar's 23 Nov patch notes list a fix for weather not changing for extended periods. Some PS5 players still report the problem after it.

### Why is it always sunny in Chapter 1?
Rockstar told IGN the first chapter has mostly good weather and storms grow later. Rain is local, so you can miss it.

### Does the cache trick work on Xbox?
No outlet has tested it on Xbox yet. Update the game, fully quit it, and restart the console.
