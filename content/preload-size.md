# GTA 6 Pre-Load: 212 GB Size, Start Times, Fixes (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** The GTA 6 pre-load is **up to 212 GB**. PlayStation says to keep **at least 212 GB free** on PS5.
> - **Pre-load (downloading the game early) opened on 10 Nov 2026.** Boxed copies have a **download code, not a disc**, and reached shops on **12 Nov**.
> - **It stays locked until midnight in your time zone on 19 Nov.** Pre-loading does not let you play early. See the [release time table](/launch-hub/release-time/).

**Words used on this page.** *GB* means gigabytes, the size of a file. *Storage* is the space on your console. *Rest Mode* (PS5) and *Sleep mode* (Xbox) keep the console on at low power so it can download.

## Step 1: Check how much space you have

**PS5**
1. Go to the home screen.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Select **Storage**.
4. Read the free space. Keep **at least 212 GB free** (PlayStation's advice). Take extra for patches.

**Xbox Series X|S** *(general Xbox menu path, check the names on your console)*
1. Press the **Xbox button** on your controller.
2. Go to **Profile & system**, then **Settings**.
3. Select **System**, then **Storage devices**.
4. Read the free space. Keep **at least 212 GB free**.

## Step 2: Make room if you are short

1. Pick one old game you do not play.
2. Delete it, or move it to another storage device.
3. Check your free space again.
4. Repeat until you have **at least 212 GB** free.

PlayStation's own advice is to delete data or move it to a different storage device.

## Step 3: Start the download

**PS5**
1. Open **Game Library**.
2. Select **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. Select **Download**.

**Xbox Series X|S**
1. Press the **Xbox button**.
2. Open **My games & apps**.
3. Select **Full library**.
4. Select **Grand Theft Auto VI**, then **Install**.

Bought the boxed version? There is a code inside, not a disc. Enter it first:
- **PS5:** sign in, select your avatar at the top of the screen, select **Redeem Code**, then enter the code.
- **Xbox:** redeem the code in the Microsoft Store (menu names on your console may differ).

## Step 4: Let it finish while you sleep

**PS5** (official PlayStation steps)
1. Open **Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates**.
2. Turn on **Auto-Install in Rest Mode**.
3. Open **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode**.
4. Turn on **Stay Connected to the Internet**.
5. Open the Control Center, select **Power**, then **Enter Rest Mode**.

**Xbox Series X|S** *(general Xbox menu path, check on your console)*
1. Press the **Xbox button**, then open **Profile & system > Settings**.
2. Go to **System > Updates** and turn on the options to keep your console and your games up to date.
3. Keep the console **on or in Sleep mode**, not fully off, so the download can continue.

## How long will it take?

There are no official times. One real example from New Zealand provider Chorus (via 1News, 18 Nov). Your speed will be different. Our method check: 212 GB ÷ your speed (calculated). 212 GB at 500 Mbps is about 57 minutes. Real speeds vary.

| Internet | About how long |
|---|---|
| 500 Mbps fibre | 57 minutes |
| 100 Mbps | 4.5 hours |
| 4G fixed wireless | More than 10 hours |

Use a cable (Ethernet) if you can. It is more stable than Wi-Fi.

## Download stuck? Fix it in this order

**PS5** (official PlayStation steps)
1. Open **Downloads/Uploads** in the Control Center.
2. Select the GTA VI download.
3. Select **Pause**, then select **Resume**.
4. Still stuck? Select the download and choose **Cancel and Delete**.
5. Go back to **Game Library** and select **Download** again.
6. Still stuck? Check four things: you are on the account that bought the game, the console is online, you have enough space, and your system software is up to date.

**Xbox Series X|S** *(general Xbox steps, check on your console)*
1. Press the **Xbox button** and open **My games & apps**.
2. Open the **Queue** and highlight GTA VI.
3. Press the **Menu button** (the three-lines button) and pause, then resume.
4. Still stuck? Cancel it and install again from **Full library**.
5. Check account, internet and space, as above.

Who to ask: PlayStation Support or Xbox Support for downloads and codes. Rockstar Support for gameplay problems, patches and bugs.

## FAQ

### How big is the GTA 6 download?
Up to 212 GB. PlayStation says keep at least 212 GB free on PS5.

### When did GTA 6 pre-load start?
10 November 2026. PS5 at midnight local time. Xbox at midnight GMT.

### Can I pre-load the boxed version?
Yes. Enter the code from the box on your console store. There is no disc.

### Can I play early if the pre-load is done?
No. The game unlocks at midnight in your time zone on 19 November.

### My download will not start. What now?
Pause and resume. Then cancel and download again. Then check account, internet, space and updates.
