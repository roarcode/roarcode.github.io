# GTA 6 Pre-Load Not Starting? 7 Fixes for PS5 and Xbox

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 pre-load not starting? **Check you are signed in to the account that bought the game, then open Downloads/Uploads (PS5) or the Queue (Xbox).** The pre-load opened on 10 Nov 2026.
> - **Boxed copy?** The box has a **download code and no disc**. Redeem the code first. On PS5: avatar, then **Redeem Code**.
> - **Need space:** keep **at least 212 GB** free. Pre-load does not unlock the game early.

**Words used on this page.** *Pre-load* means downloading the game before it unlocks. *Redeem a code* means typing the code from the box into the store. *Code-in-box* means a box with a code and no disc.

## The 7 fixes

1. **Check your order went through (PS5).**
   1. Sign in on the PlayStation Store website.
   2. Select your **avatar** at the top of the screen.
   3. Select **Order History**.
   4. Check that the GTA VI order says it was successful (PlayStation support).
2. **Redeem your box code (PS5).**
   1. Sign in on the PlayStation Store.
   2. Select your **avatar**, then **Redeem Code**.
   3. Enter the code from the box. If the code is missing or unreadable, contact the shop where you bought it (PlayStation support). See [GTA 6 code-in-box: how to redeem and fix code problems](/glitches/gta-6-physical-edition-code-in-box/).
3. **Redeem your box code (Xbox).** Redeem it in the Microsoft Store on the account you play on (general Xbox path, check on your console).
4. **Find the download.**
   - PS5: press the **PS button**, then open **Downloads/Uploads** and select GTA VI. If it is there but not moving: **Pause**, then **Resume**.
   - Xbox: press the **Xbox button**, open **My games & apps > Full library**, select **Grand Theft Auto VI**, then **Install**.
5. **Free 212 GB.** PS5: **Settings > Storage**. Xbox: **Profile & system > Settings > System > Storage devices**. Delete or move an old game.
6. **Use the right account.** PlayStation says you must be signed in to the account that ordered the game or entered the code.
7. **Update the console.** PS5: **Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings**. Also check the [PlayStation status page](https://status.playstation.com) or the [Xbox status page](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status).
   - **Required for GTA 6:** PlayStation says a PS5 system update must be installed before you play. See [GTA 6 PS5 update required (14.23.00): how to install](/glitches/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/).

## Make it download on its own (PS5)

1. Go to **Settings > Saved Data and Game/App Settings > Automatic Updates**.
2. Turn on **Auto-Install in Rest Mode**.
3. Go to **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode**.
4. Turn on **Stay Connected to the Internet**.
5. If you share the console, go to **Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play** and select **Enable**.

## What changed on launch day

The pre-load window was 10 Nov onward: PS5 at midnight local time, Xbox at midnight GMT (VGC, USA Today). Boxed copies reached shops on 12 Nov. If you did not pre-load, you can still download now as a normal download. See [GTA 6 download stuck](/glitches/gta-6-download-stuck/) if it stalls. Xbox says Series X and Series S could pre-install, and Xbox One streams through Xbox Cloud Gaming instead of downloading (Xbox Wire).

## FAQ

### Why is my GTA 6 pre-load not showing?
You may be on the wrong account, the order may not have gone through or the box code may not be redeemed. Check those three first.

### Can I pre-load GTA 6 on Xbox One?
No. Xbox One streams it with Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1:00 AM ET on 19 Nov (07:00 CET), so there is nothing to download (Xbox Wire).

### Does the boxed version have a disc?
No. It has a code only (PlayStation support, VGC).

### Can I play early if I pre-loaded?
No. It unlocks at midnight in your time zone on 19 Nov 2026.
