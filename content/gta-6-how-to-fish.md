# How to Fish in GTA 6: Buttons, Lures and Reeling

> - **Quick answer:** fishing is **unlocked from the start**. As soon as you can walk around as Jason, you can go to a dock (Shacknews).
> - **Cast:** hold **L2** (Xbox: **Left Trigger**), then press **R2** (Xbox: **Right Trigger**) (Shacknews).
> - **Bonus:** GameSpot says fishing raises your **Focus** stat.

**Words used on this page.** *Lure* is the fake bait on the fishing line. *Cast* means to throw the line into the water. *Hook* means to catch the fish on the line. *Reel in* means to wind the line back. *Weapon wheel* is the circle menu that holds weapons and items. *Focus* is a stat that lets you slow time when you aim.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## How to start fishing

1. Walk to the water. Shacknews says to go to a dock.
2. **Hold L1** (Xbox: **Left Bumper**) to open the weapon wheel.
3. Use the **right stick** to pick the **Equipment** tab.
4. Use **L2 / R2** (Xbox: **Left / Right Trigger**) to choose the **Fishing Rod**.
5. Follow the on-screen prompts (Shacknews).

## How to cast, hook and reel

1. **Cast:** hold **L2** (Left Trigger), then press **R2** (Right Trigger) to throw the line in (Shacknews).
2. **Wait.** Shacknews says to let the fish take the bait when you feel a bite.
3. **Hook:** press **R2** (Right Trigger).
4. **Tire the fish:** use the **left stick** to pull the opposite way to where it swims (Shacknews).
5. **Reel in:** when the fish takes a break, **rotate the right stick** (Shacknews).

Shacknews says you can click **L3 or R3** (Left or Right Stick) for Focus to spot places with fish. It says it did not find it hard to hook fish without that tool.

## Lures

Shacknews says you start with the **Small Saltwater Lure** and **Small Freshwater Lure**. You can buy a **Medium Saltwater Lure** at any Ammu-Nation that sells fishing supplies, straight away. It says the shop near your starting safehouse sells lures too.

## Why fish?

GameSpot says "mindfulness" activities such as fishing and playing pool raise your **Focus**, and a Focus gain is permanent.

## FAQ

### Can you fish at the start of GTA 6?
Yes. Shacknews says fishing is unlocked immediately.

### What buttons do you use?
Cast with L2 then R2, hook with R2, pull with the left stick and reel with the right stick. Xbox uses the triggers and sticks in the same way (Shacknews).

### What lures can I buy?
Shacknews says a Medium Saltwater Lure is on sale at Ammu-Nation shops that sell fishing supplies.
