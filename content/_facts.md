**Checked:** Wed 18 Nov 2026, ~11:30–11:45 CET (Europe/Ljubljana = CET, UTC+1 on these dates). Every source was opened this session unless noted.
**Site is an unofficial fan site, not affiliated with Rockstar Games.** Pre-launch rule from BRIEF: publish only Official / Reputable-preview facts; keep Rumors out of body copy.

**Labels:** `OFFICIAL` = Rockstar site/newswire/support/trailers, or platform-holder store/support pages (PlayStation, Xbox) · `PREVIEW` = reputable outlet (IGN, GameSpot, VGC, Polygon, PC Gamer, Verge, Game Informer, GamesRadar, Engadget…) · `SECONDARY` = fan/aggregator or Wikipedia that cites the above; use only with a second source or a hedge · `RUMOR` = see last section.
