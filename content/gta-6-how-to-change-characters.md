# How to Switch Between Jason and Lucia in GTA 6

> - **Quick answer:** once both characters are available, press **right on the D-pad** to swap (VGC).
> - **Greyed out?** That character is busy or unavailable for a reason in the story (VGC).
> - **You cannot swap at the very start.** It unlocks later. The details are in closed boxes below.

**Words used on this page.** *Playable* means you can control that character. *D-pad* is the plus-shaped button pad. *Open world* means the big map you can roam freely. *Parole* means a prisoner is allowed out under rules. *Safehouse* is the home where a character stays.

*The boxes on this page tell you when swapping unlocks, which hints at the story. Leave them closed if you want to be surprised.*

## How to swap

1. Make sure swapping is unlocked (see the boxes below).
2. Press **right on the D-pad** (VGC).
3. Choose the character you want.

VGC names only the D-pad direction and gives no separate Xbox button. The D-pad is the same on both consoles.

## When does swapping unlock?

:::spoiler When can I swap in the first hours? (reveals the order of the early game)
VGC says the game starts with you playing as Lucia, who is in jail, so you cannot explore the open world with her yet. After the first mission with Jason you can explore, but you cannot swap yet.

The first time you play as Lucia alongside Jason is in the mission **Day Release**. When it ends you go back to Jason, because Lucia is on parole. Later missions in Chapter 1 switch you to Lucia, for example **Tourist Trap**, but they are part of a run of missions at the end of the chapter, so you do not get Lucia in the open world yet.

The first time you play Lucia in the open world is after the end of Chapter 1. Keep playing Chapter 2 and it becomes obvious when you can swap (VGC).
:::

## Why is a character greyed out?

VGC says a grey name means that character is unavailable. This only happens with an in-game reason, such as them being on another mission. VGC also says that after you swap, the character you left sometimes calls a RydeMe and leaves, and that can briefly grey out the swap menu.

## Swapping as a shortcut

GameSpot says swapping is the quickest and cheapest way to cross the map. The character you are not playing usually waits near your safehouse.

GameSpot also says that if you go to a gym together, both characters work out no matter who you control.

## FAQ

### How do you switch characters in GTA 6?
Press right on the D-pad once both are available (VGC).

### Why can I not swap characters?
It is locked early in the game. Later it can be greyed out if the other person is busy (VGC).

### Do I need to unlock Lucia?
You play as her in the prologue, but swapping freely unlocks later (VGC).
