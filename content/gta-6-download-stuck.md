# GTA 6 Download Stuck? 8 Fixes for PS5 and Xbox (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 download stuck? **Pause it, then resume it.** If the bar still does not move, cancel the download and start it again from your library.
> - **The game is big:** up to **212 GB**. On 100 Mbps internet it takes about 4.5 hours, so a slow bar is not always a stuck bar.
> - **Warning:** if the PlayStation or Xbox status page shows a problem, wait. Fixes on your console will not help.

**Words used on this page.** *Download* means copying the game to your console over the internet. *Queue* is the list of downloads waiting on your Xbox. *Mbps* is internet speed: more is faster.

## First: is it stuck or just slow?

Watch the number for 10 minutes. If the percent or the GB number goes up, even slowly, it is working. Real examples from New Zealand provider Chorus (via 1News, 18 Nov 2026): about 57 minutes on 500 Mbps fibre, about 4.5 hours on 100 Mbps and more than 10 hours on 4G fixed wireless. Rockstar says the size is up to 212 GB (VGC). If the number has not moved for 10 minutes, use the fixes below.

**Busy internet is real.** UK provider Virgin Media O2 says pre-loads on **10 Nov 2026** gave it record traffic: its peak was **3.8%** above the old record, and traffic in the first hour after midnight was **51.6%** higher than a week before (Virgin Media O2 press release, 11 Nov). The London Internet Exchange told the BBC that UK providers such as BT and Virgin Media added capacity before launch. So at unlock time, downloads and updates may be slower than usual. Wait a little before you cancel anything.

## PS5: 4 fixes from PlayStation's own steps

1. **Pause and resume.**
   1. Press the **PS button** to open the Control Center.
   2. Select **Downloads/Uploads**.
   3. Select the **GTA VI** download.
   4. Select **Pause**, then select **Resume**.
2. **Cancel and download again.**
   1. In **Downloads/Uploads**, select the GTA VI download.
   2. Select **Cancel and Delete**.
   3. Go to **Game Library**, select **Grand Theft Auto VI**, then select **Download**.
3. **Test the internet.** Go to **Settings > Network > Connection Status**, then select **Test Internet Connection**. Use a network cable if you can.
4. **Rebuild the database in Safe Mode.** This does not delete your games or saves. See [GTA 6 PS5 fixes](/glitches/gta-6-ps5-fixes/) for the exact steps.

## Xbox Series X|S: 4 fixes

*General Xbox menu paths, check the names on your console.*

1. **Look at the Queue.**
   1. Press the **Xbox button**.
   2. Open **My games & apps**, then **See all**.
   3. Select **Manage**, then **Queue**.
   4. Highlight **Grand Theft Auto VI**. If it says Paused or Queued, press the **Menu button** (three lines) and select **Resume installation**.
2. **Cancel and install again.** In the Queue, highlight GTA VI, press the **Menu button**, select **Cancel**, then install it again from **Full library**.
3. **Restart the console.** Hold the **Xbox button** on the controller, select **Restart console**, then **Restart**. If the console is frozen, hold the **Xbox button on the console** for about 10 seconds, then turn it back on.
4. **Test the network.** Press the **Xbox button**, go to **Profile & system > Settings > General > Network settings**, then select **Test network connection**.

## Check these on both consoles

- **Free space.** Keep at least **212 GB** free (PlayStation's advice). PS5: **Settings > Storage**. Xbox: **Profile & system > Settings > System > Storage devices**.
- **Right account.** Use the account that bought the game or entered the box code.
- **Status pages.** [PlayStation Network status](https://status.playstation.com) and [Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status). If you see an alert, wait.
- **System software.** PS5: **Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings**.
  - **Required for GTA 6:** PlayStation says a PS5 system update must be installed before you play. See [GTA 6 PS5 update required (14.23.00): how to install](/glitches/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/).

## FAQ

### Why is my GTA 6 download stuck at 0%?
Common causes are a service problem, a full drive and the wrong account. Check the status pages, free 212 GB and use the buying account. Then pause and resume.

### How long does the GTA 6 download take?
It depends on your speed. About 57 minutes at 500 Mbps and about 4.5 hours at 100 Mbps, from one NZ provider's figures (1News).

### Will cancelling lose my progress?
You may start again from the beginning. Try pause and resume first.

### Does Rest Mode keep downloading?
On PS5, yes, if **Stay Connected to the Internet** is on under **Settings > System > Power Saving > Features Available in Rest Mode** (PlayStation support).
