# GTA 6 Military Base: Where Is Mile Zero Air Station?

> - **Quick answer:** Mile Zero Air Station is on **Goose Key** in Mariana County, in the **far southwest corner** of the map (PC Gamer).
> - **Inside:** jets, tanks, armoured transports, helicopters and a truck (PC Gamer).
> - **Warning:** you get a wanted level and a gunfight if you are spotted on the base (PC Gamer).

**Words used on this page.** *Air station* is a military airfield. *APC* is an armoured truck for carrying soldiers. *Stunt jump* is a ramp or spot that gives you a prize for a big jump. *Wanted level* is the star rating that shows how much the police want you. *Southwest* is the bottom-left of the map.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## Where is it?

PC Gamer says Mile Zero Air Station is in **Mariana County, on Goose Key**, in the **southwest corner** of the map. It is on the big bridges to the southern keys. PC Gamer says it is a bit of a trip by car, boat or aircraft, but not very far from the first house you can use in Key Lento.

PC Gamer says this is the easiest way to steal a tank or get a combat jet.

## How risky is it?

- PC Gamer says you get a wanted level if you are seen on the base, and a quick gunfight.
- GameSpot says breaking into this kind of place raises you to **four or five stars** at once, with air support and tanks, so the chance of survival is low.
- PC Gamer's six-star page says a tank from a military base is how it reached six stars, and that at six stars you cannot lose your wanted level.

Think before you go. Save first.

## PC Gamer's easy way in

PC Gamer says the easiest way to get in without a full gunfight is the **Mile Zero Air Station Southeast** stunt jump. It is on the southeast corner of the base on the southern access road. You weave past a few lightly manned checkpoints and take some gunfire. Then you take a vehicle at speed over the big dirt mound by the barbed-wire fence for the stunt jump, and you land inside.

PC Gamer's tip is a vehicle at speed. We have not tested it ourselves.

1. Drive to the southern access road.
2. Pass the checkpoints.
3. Aim at the dirt mound by the fence.
4. Go over it at speed.
