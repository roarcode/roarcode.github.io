# GTA 6 Xbox Fixes: 8 Steps for Series X|S and Xbox One

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 Xbox fixes start with **the Xbox status page, then a restart.** Hold the **Xbox button** on the controller, select **Restart console**, then **Restart**.
> - **Series X|S play it on the console. Xbox One streams it** through Xbox Cloud Gaming from 1:00 AM ET on 19 Nov (07:00 CET), at Xbox Series S graphics settings (Xbox Wire).
> - **Not a bug:** Rockstar targets 30 frames per second on every console (Game Informer).

**Words used on this page.** *Queue* is the list of games your Xbox is downloading. *Power cycle* means a full turn off and on. *Cloud gaming* means the game runs on a faraway computer and streams to your screen.

*Menu paths on this page are general Xbox paths. Check the names on your console.*

## The 8 steps

1. **Check Xbox status.** Open [support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status). If Store or downloads show a problem, wait.
2. **Check the unlock time.** The game is locked until midnight local time on 19 Nov 2026. See [is GTA 6 out](/launch-hub/is-gta-6-out/).
3. **Restart the console.** Hold the **Xbox button** on the controller. Select **Restart console**, then **Restart**.
4. **Power cycle if it is frozen.** Hold the **Xbox button on the console** for about 10 seconds until it turns off. Touch it again to turn it on.
5. **Look at the Queue.** Press the **Xbox button**, open **My games & apps > See all > Manage > Queue**. Highlight GTA VI. If it says Paused or Queued, press the **Menu button** (three lines) and select **Resume installation**.
6. **Test the network.** Press the **Xbox button**, go to **Profile & system > Settings > General > Network settings**, then select **Test network connection**.
7. **Check storage.** Go to **Profile & system > Settings > System > Storage devices**. Keep at least **212 GB** free.
8. **Check the account.** Use the account that bought the game. Install it from **My games & apps > Full library**.

## Xbox One: cloud streaming tips

- Xbox One cannot run GTA 6 on the console. It streams it. You need a good internet connection. Use a network cable if you can.
- From 11 Nov, in supported markets, you can buy cloud playtime without Game Pass (Xbox Wire). Availability and features vary by market.
- Cloud streaming uses Xbox Series S graphics settings (Xbox Wire).

## The Blu-ray "persistent storage" fix: skip it

You may see advice to clear **persistent storage**. That fix is for **disc** installs. The boxed GTA 6 has a download code and **no disc**, so it is not needed.
