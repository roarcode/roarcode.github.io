# GTA 6 New Zealand Trick: Does It Work? Real Risks

> - **Quick answer:** The GTA 6 New Zealand trick means telling your console you are in New Zealand, so the game unlocks at **12:00 CET on Wed 18 Nov** instead of **00:00 CET on Thu 19 Nov**.
> - **Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony and Microsoft have not commented** on it (BBC). It is not approved.
> - **Risk:** an analyst told the BBC it may break PlayStation or Xbox rules, which could mean **account limits or losing your digital games**. We do not recommend it.

**Words used on this page.** *Region* is the country your console or account is set to. *Rolling launch* means each country unlocks at its own midnight. *Terms* are the rules you agree to when you make an account. *Ban* means the company blocks your account.

## Why New Zealand?

1. GTA 6 unlocks at **midnight local time** in each country. Sony calls it a "rolling midnight launch", **starting in New Zealand** (PlayStation Blog).
2. New Zealand reaches midnight first. That was **12:00 CET** on Wed 18 Nov.
3. Slovenia reaches midnight **12 hours later**, at 00:00 CET on Thu 19 Nov.
4. So some players try to make their console think it is in New Zealand.

## What people say they do

*This is a short description of what is being reported. It is not a how-to, and we do not recommend it.*

**Xbox (reported by RNZ, 13 Nov, and Windows Central, 8 Sep):**
- Players change the **location setting on the console** to New Zealand. The console then shows the New Zealand unlock time.
- RNZ says the account's billing region is left as it is. It also says you would want to have **pre-ordered the game first**, because buying in another region's store needs a local billing address and a matching payment method.
- Windows Central says it is easy to **set the console back** afterwards.
- RNZ says Xbox has not said the trick is allowed. RNZ calls it "internet folklore" that Xbox does not seem to mind.

**PS5 (reported by Windows Central, Polygon, NZ Herald):**
- The country that counts is the **PlayStation account's**, not the console's. PlayStation says an account's country cannot be changed after it is created.
- So the trick would need a **separate account registered in New Zealand**, and a way to pay in **NZ dollars**.
- That account would be set up under a country the player does not live in. See the PlayStation terms below.

**Travel:** the NZ Herald reports some streamers flew to New Zealand to play first.

## What the companies' own rules say

- **Microsoft / Xbox:** RNZ quotes Xbox's rules: attempting to "circumvent regional content restrictions" is prohibited. Xbox says to change your country or region only if you have moved, or if you are already somewhere other than the region that was set. RNZ says the consequences could range from a warning or suspension to a ban if Xbox decides you broke its rules. (Quoted from RNZ, which quotes Microsoft's terms.)
- **Sony / PlayStation:** the PSN Terms of Service, section 3.2, say you must choose the country or region **where you live**, and you cannot change it later. Section 3.1 says Sony may suspend, terminate or restrict an account that uses or was created with false information, or used for a purpose that breaks the terms. ([PSN terms](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/legal/psn-terms-of-service.html/))
- **Store and payment:** PlayStation's support page says your account's country should match your billing information, so you can buy and download. ([PlayStation: account country](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/account/check-account-country-region/))
- **Money side effects (Xbox):** Windows Central, quoting Xbox's messaging, says local currency tied to your account will not move to the new region. If you plan to buy Ultimate Edition add-ons, buy them before any region change.
- **Codes:** PlayStation says voucher codes must match the account's country to activate. Physical code-in-box copies are covered on [our code-in-box page](/glitches/gta-6-physical-edition-code-in-box/).

## The 4 risks

1. **Rules:** Ampere Analysis told the BBC that Sony and Microsoft both have rules on regional accounts and purchases. It said Sony's are a bit stricter.
2. **Your account:** breaking those rules could bring **account limits or loss of your digital games** (BBC).
3. **No promise it works:** Rockstar, Take-Two, Sony and Microsoft have said nothing (BBC). PC Gamer was not sure it works (PC Gamer).
4. **Spoilers:** if you wait, people in New Zealand play 12 hours before you. A games journalist told the BBC that story surprises can spread online in that time.

## Already changed your Xbox region?

You can set it back to your real country.

1. Press the **Xbox button** on the controller.
2. Go to **Profile & system > Settings**.
3. Select **System**, then **Language & location**.
4. Set **Location** to your real country.
5. Restart the console when it asks.

## FAQ

### Does the GTA 6 New Zealand trick work?
Some Xbox players say it worked for them. No company has confirmed it. On PS5 it needs a separate New Zealand account (Polygon).

### Can I get banned for the New Zealand trick?
It can happen. No company has confirmed a ban for this trick. But news sites report that one streamer got a temporary ban on Xbox while trying it on 18 Nov, and Xbox's rules prohibit getting around region limits (RNZ). An analyst told the BBC it may break PlayStation or Xbox rules. That can lead to account limits or losing digital games.
