# GTA 6 A Night in Vice City: Heat Game Time in CET

> - **Quick answer:** "A Night in Vice City" is the **Miami Heat vs Milwaukee Bucks** basketball game on **Wed 18 Nov 2026 at 7:00 PM ET**. In Slovenia that is **01:00 CET on Thu 19 Nov**.
> - **It is on ESPN** in the US. Rockstar and the Heat made it a GTA 6 night, the eve of the game's launch.
> - **Tip:** in central Europe GTA 6 unlocks at 00:00 CET, one hour **before** tip-off.

**Words used on this page.** *Tip-off* is the moment a basketball game starts. *ESPN* is a US sports TV channel. *Vice City* is the game's Miami-style city. *Eve* means the day before.

Rockstar posted that you can watch the Heat "trade Miami for Vice City" on ESPN (Rockstar on X and YouTube, 9 Nov).

## What is special about the night

- **A one-night court:** pink, white and blue, with "Vice City" in the middle (ESPN).
- **A new Heat uniform** made for the night (ESPN).
- **The arena roof** reads "Welcome to Vice City" (ESPN).
- **Music from GTA 6** plays in the arena (ESPN, NBC 6).
- **A halftime show and a drone show** over Biscayne Bay (NBC 6). *Halftime* is the break in the middle of the game.
- **A Vice City jersey** is sold at the game in limited numbers (ESPN).
