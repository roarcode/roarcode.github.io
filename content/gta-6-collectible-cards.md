# GTA 6 Collectible Cards: 480 Cards, 18 Sets, 15 to Finish

> - **Quick answer:** there are **480 collectible cards in 18 sets** (Bloxodes). You need **15 full sets** for the 100% checklist (PowerPyx, Insider Gaming).
> - **Track them:** **Progress menu, Collections, Collectible Cards** (PowerPyx).
> - **Not all spots are known yet.** Only part of the 480 locations had been found on 18 Nov. Our page lists the rules, not guesses.

**Words used on this page.** *Collectible card* is a small card of a character that you pick up in the world. *Set* is a group of cards that belong together. *Missable* means you can lose the chance to get it. *100% completion* means finishing the game's big checklist. *Colour version* means the same card in gold, silver or bronze.

*A few card tips mention story chapters. Those are in a closed box.*

## How sets and colours work

Bloxodes says each colour version of a card counts as its own card toward its set. A second copy of a card you already have does not count twice. Sets are different sizes. PowerPyx gives these set sizes:

| Set | Cards | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Backyard Wrestling | 12 | PowerPyx |
| Delights Dancers | 18 | PowerPyx |
| Ed Comics Universe | 36 | PowerPyx |
| Liberty City Wrath | 24 | PowerPyx |

## How to find them

1. Explore buildings. Bloxodes says indoors, cards usually sit on a surface or stuck to a wall.
2. Outside, look for **yellow phone boxes** on walls and **tool boxes** on surfaces (Bloxodes).
3. Open **Progress, then Collections, then Collectible Cards** to see which you have (PowerPyx).
4. You cannot buy cards and there are no packs (Bloxodes).
5. Some cards can be found in more than one place (Bloxodes).

## Missable cards

PowerPyx marks one Delights Dancers card as **missable**: it is in a motel office and it says that spot is not reachable after Chapter 2. PowerPyx says a different card in the same set can be got later by going back into a club.

:::spoiler Why might a card be missable? (hints at how chapters work)
PowerPyx says some locations close once the story moves on. GameSpot's tips page says each chapter centres on a different safehouse, and that you cannot go back "unless you have a hard save". So make a manual save at the end of each chapter if you want to be safe. See [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/beginner-tips/gta-6-replay-missions/).
:::
