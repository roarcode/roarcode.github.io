# GTA 6 Collectible Cards: Sets, Trophy and 100% Needs

> - **Quick answer:** you need **15 full card sets** for the 100% checklist (Insider Gaming). The **Don't Touch My Deck** trophy or achievement needs **3 full sets** (PC Gamer, GamesRadar).
> - **Sets count, not single cards.** The trophy and the checklist both ask for sets (PC Gamer, Insider Gaming).
> - **The total count and where to look are unconfirmed.** PowerPyx and Bloxodes give numbers and tips, but no major outlet has confirmed them yet. See the section below.

**Words used on this page.** *Collectible card* is a small card that you pick up in the world. *Set* is a group of cards that belong together. *Missable* means you can lose the chance to get it. *100% completion* means finishing the game's big checklist. *Unconfirmed* means only small sites report it so far.

*A few card tips mention story chapters. Those are in a closed box.*

## What major outlets confirm

| Fact | Number | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Full sets for the Don't Touch My Deck trophy or achievement | 3 | PC Gamer, GamesRadar |
| Full sets on the 100% checklist ("Collectible Card Sets Found") | 15 | Insider Gaming |
| Tasks on the whole 100% checklist | 35 | Insider Gaming |

GamesRadar lists Don't Touch My Deck as Silver / 30G. Neither outlet gives the total number of cards or sets.

## Early reports: not yet confirmed by a major outlet

- **480 cards in 18 sets.** Reported by PowerPyx and Bloxodes. No major outlet has given a total yet. Bloxodes updated its list of 125 found cards on 18 Nov 2026, so many spots are still missing.
- **Set sizes.** PowerPyx gives Backyard Wrestling 12 cards, Delights Dancers 18, Liberty City Wrath 24 and Ed Comics Universe 36.
- **Colours.** Bloxodes says each colour version of a card counts as its own card toward its set, and a second copy does not count twice.
- **Where to look.** Bloxodes says indoors, cards usually sit on a surface or stuck to a wall. Outside, it says to look for yellow phone boxes on walls and tool boxes on surfaces.
- **Menu path.** PowerPyx says to open Progress, then Collections, then Collectible Cards to see what you have.
- **No packs.** Bloxodes says you cannot buy cards. Some can be found in more than one place.
- **A missable card.** PowerPyx marks one Delights Dancers card as missable, in a motel office that it says is not reachable after Chapter 2.

We list these as reports, not facts. We will move them up when a major outlet or Rockstar confirms them.

:::spoiler Why might a card be missable? (hints at how chapters work)
PowerPyx says some locations close once the story moves on. GameSpot's tips page says each chapter centres on a different safehouse, and that you cannot go back "unless you have a hard save". So make a manual save at the end of each chapter if you want to be safe. See [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/beginner-tips/gta-6-replay-missions/).
