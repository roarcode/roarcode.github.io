# GTA 6 Prologue Walkthrough: Buttons and Tips (Spoilers)

> - **Quick answer:** the **Prologue** is the very first mission of GTA 6. You start it as **Lucia** (VGC).
> - **Buttons to know:** hold **L2** to talk, **L2** to aim and **X** to jump (VGC).
> - **Every step is in a closed box** so you only read what you want to know.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** this is the opening of the story. The steps are inside closed boxes. Tap a box only if you want to know.

**Words used on this page.** *Prologue* is the first part of a story. *Mission* is a task with a goal. *Cutscene* is a short movie inside the game. *Flashback* is a scene that shows something that happened before. *Cashier* is the person at the till.

VGC says its walkthroughs try to avoid cutscene spoilers and only describe what you play. We follow the same rule here.

## Step by step

:::spoiler Part 1: the cell and the hall (the opening playable scene)
VGC says you start in a prison as Lucia. Walk around your cell and speak to your cellmate. To talk to a character, **hold L2**. You usually get these choices: **Greet, Provoke, Taunt or Defuse**. When a character has special words, one of them replaces the usual choices.

A guard then asks Lucia to walk down the hall. VGC says you can talk to other prisoners on the way, for example the ones arguing over the TV, but your goal is the end of the hall and the office.
:::

:::spoiler Part 2: the flashback job (reveals what the game shows you)
VGC says the next part is a flashback that explains why Lucia was arrested. You try to rob a payday loan building. **Aim at the cashier with L2.** Press **Square** to threaten them. Once they are scared they start giving you money from the register. Press **Triangle** to pick it up.

While that happens someone tackles you. VGC says you can fight them off with **Square**.
:::

:::spoiler Part 3: running from the police (can end early)
VGC says you then run from the cops. After a short cutscene you can jump onto the roofs next door and go through a car garage. Press **X** to jump and climb.

VGC says this part can go several ways. If the cops catch you, the mission ends with cutscenes. If you get away, on foot or in a vehicle, you get a second part where you head back to the coffee shop.
:::

:::spoiler Part 4: the way back (the end of the playable part)
VGC says that in the second part you head to the map marker. At some point dye packs explode and cover Lucia in dye. After that she is arrested, and the playable part of the mission ends.
:::

## FAQ

### Who do you play in the GTA 6 Prologue?
Lucia (VGC).

### Can you win or lose the Prologue?
VGC says it ends either way. Getting caught ends it early, and getting away gives you a second section before it ends.

### How do I talk to someone?
Hold L2 (VGC).
