# How to Steal Cars in GTA 6: Slim Jim, Key Cloner, Trackers

> - **Quick answer:** walk to the door and press the interact button. It tells you if the car is **locked, alarmed or keyless** (PC Gamer).
> - **Smash the window and hotwire** some cars. **Keyless cars** need a tool called a key cloner (PC Gamer).
> - **Trackers can bring the police.** Check the icons above the minimap (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Hotwire* means starting a car without its key. *Alarm* is a loud noise that tells people a car is being stolen. *Keyless car* is a car with no physical key. *Tracker* is a device that tells the police where a car is. *Slim jim* is a thin tool that opens a locked car door. *Register* means paying to make a stolen car your own.

*The tools unlock during the story. The mission names are in closed boxes.*

## How to take a car, step by step

1. Walk up to a car door and press the **interact button**. PC Gamer says this shows if the car is locked (most are), alarmed (nearly all are) and keyless.
2. If it is locked, **smash the window**. GameSpot says a red shield icon next to the text means an alarm will go off.
3. Climb in and **hotwire** it. PC Gamer says this takes a second or two. Use the control sticks to find the "sweet spot".
4. Drive away. If the alarm is on, GameSpot says it keeps going while you drive.

PC Gamer says you can still pull a driver out of a car, but many fight back and you must press a button fast to win. Many drivers are armed.

## The tools

| Tool | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Slim jim | Opens a locked car quietly, with no broken window and no alarm. Takes a bit longer than smashing | PC Gamer, GameSpot |
| Key cloner | Opens a **keyless** car and starts it, with no hotwiring | PC Gamer, GameSpot |
| WAiNK app | Scan a car to see locks, alarms, keyless status, trackers, value and registration cost | PC Gamer |

PC Gamer says you cannot use a slim jim on a car someone is sitting in. It also says a slim jim cannot start a keyless car.

:::spoiler When do you get the slim jim and key cloner? (reveals two mission names and the names of two characters)
PC Gamer says you get the **slim jim** in Chapter 2, in the fifth mission, **Moses**, where a mechanic named Moses teaches you to use it. GameSpot says you meet Moses roughly 30% of the way through the story.

PC Gamer says you get the **key cloner** in Chapter 3, in mission 16, **Steal the Zorruso for Randy**. GameSpot says you meet Randy around the halfway point.

PC Gamer says the two fences are Moses, who only buys older, low-end cars, and Randy, who only buys certain flashy, expensive cars.

The two outlets describe the timing in different ways, but both agree on the order: slim jim first, key cloner later. PC Gamer says Cal helps you install the scanning app in Chapter 1.
:::

## Trackers: yellow and red

The two outlets describe trackers a little differently, so here are both:

| | PC Gamer | GameSpot |
|---|---|---|
| Standard or yellow tracker | You cannot modify the car at a mod shop unless you register it. PC Gamer did not worry about them | Police can trace it, but only when you come to a full stop and wait a few seconds. It stays on until you register the car |
| Advanced or red tracker | Tells the police the car is stolen and where you are. Drive fast enough that it cannot send a signal. If it beeps, speed up within seconds | Pings the police if you slow below a certain speed for about 5 seconds. Pay 'n' Spray will not take the car and you cannot store it in a garage |

GameSpot says you can see which tracker you have by the vehicle icons above the minimap.

GameSpot also says that if you leave a tracked stolen car near a mission start, the ping goes out when you leave it. That makes the area a crime zone until your heat is cleared. Ditch it far from where you are going.

## Selling and storing

PC Gamer says you can sell stolen cars at a Pay 'n' Spray, up to **three cars a day**. It says you also have two car fences you meet later in the story, one for older cheap cars and one for flashy expensive ones, and that the WAiNK app tells you which one fits a car. It says each garage holds **one car** and you can also use parking spots outside your safehouse.

GameSpot's tips page says that to keep a car you take it to a Pay 'n' Spray and pay the registration fee. The worse the car, the cheaper the fee.

## FAQ

### How do I steal a car in GTA 6?
Press interact at the door, smash the window, hotwire and go (PC Gamer).

### Why can I not drive a car?
It may be keyless. You need a key cloner (PC Gamer, GameSpot).

### How do I get rid of a tracker?
GameSpot says the yellow one comes off if you register the car. The red one cannot be removed or registered.
