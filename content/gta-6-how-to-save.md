# How to Save in GTA 6: Manual Save, Autosave, Slots

> - **Quick answer:** open the pause menu, choose **Game**, then **Save Game**, then pick a slot (PC Gamer, GameSpot).
> - **There are 15 save slots** (PC Gamer, GameSpot). They disagree on whether autosave is one of the 15. Details below.
> - **You cannot save during a mission** (GameSpot). **Using a cheat turns saving off** until you quit and come back (PC Gamer).

**Words used on this page.** *Manual save* means a save you make yourself. *Autosave* means the game saves for you. *Slot* is one place to keep a save. *Free roam* means you are walking or driving around with no mission running. *Pause menu* is the menu you open in the middle of the game. *Unconfirmed* means only small sites report it so far.

*This page has one small spoiler box about when saving turns on.*

## How to save your game

1. Finish or leave your current mission. GameSpot says you cannot save while you are actively on a mission.
2. Open the pause menu. PC Gamer calls it the main menu. **Unconfirmed:** KeenGamer, PowerPyx and Beebom say the button is **Options** on PS5 and **Menu** on Xbox Series X|S. No major outlet has said which button yet.
3. Select **Game** (PC Gamer).
4. Select **Save Game** (PC Gamer). Beebom and KeenGamer call it **Save**.
5. Pick a slot. Choose an empty one, or one you are happy to overwrite.
6. Wait until the save finishes before you quit.

PC Gamer says you can load a save from the same menu.

## Slots and autosave

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| How many slots? | 15 manual slots | PC Gamer |
| How many slots? | 15 in total, and the autosave uses one of them | GameSpot |
| How many slots? | 15 manual plus 1 autosave | KeenGamer, PowerPyx (small sites) |
| Does autosave overwrite itself? | Yes, each new autosave replaces the last | GameSpot |
| When does it autosave? | After missions and open-world events (unconfirmed) | KeenGamer |

Sources disagree on whether the autosave takes one of the 15 slots, so plan for 15 and keep a spare. The danger is the overwrite. If the autosave happens right after something you wish had gone differently, the older state is gone unless you made a manual save (GameSpot).

## When to save

PC Gamer suggests keeping one save at the start of each chapter if you want to be able to go back. GameSpot says to save before a big heist, and before tasks you might miss, because you cannot always go back to them.

GameSpot's tips page says the story is split into chapters, and each chapter centres on a different safehouse. Once you move on there is no going back, "unless you have a hard save". GameSpot recommends saving in each chapter if you want to revisit old safehouses or catch conversations you missed.

KeenGamer also suggests (unconfirmed advice, not a fact):

- After the prologue
- At the start and end of each chapter
- Before big story choices
- Two or three rotating slots for free roam

There is no mission replay in GTA 6. Read [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/beginner-tips/gta-6-replay-missions/) for what that means.

## Saving not working? Check these

- **You are in a mission.** Finish or leave it first (GameSpot).
- **You used a cheat.** PC Gamer says cheats disable saving entirely. You have to quit out and come back to save again. KeenGamer says even a screen filter does it, and that trophies and achievements are turned off too (unconfirmed).
- **You are still in the prologue.** KeenGamer says manual saving is off until it ends (unconfirmed).
- **Autosave overwrote what you wanted.** KeenGamer says to open **Game**, then **Load Game**, and pick your newest manual save (unconfirmed). PC Gamer confirms you can load from the Game menu.
- **Cloud backup.** KeenGamer says PS5 saves can back up to PlayStation Plus cloud storage and Xbox Series X|S saves sync to the cloud on their own. Unconfirmed. We have not checked this ourselves.

## Lost progress after a crash?

Some players on PS5 and Xbox report crashes on launch day that sent them back hours. These are player reports, and Rockstar has not explained the cause ([Notebookcheck](https://www.notebookcheck.net/GTA-6-PS5-and-Xbox-crashes-are-losing-hours-of-progress-despite-autosave-system.1453724.0.html)).

1. Do not start a new game yet.
2. Open the pause menu and choose **Game**, then **Load Game**. Notebookcheck says the **Save Game** menu does not show autosaves, so look in **Load Game** instead.
3. Pick the newest save you can see.

To lose less next time:

1. Make a **manual save** after each mission. Notebookcheck says Rockstar has not confirmed how often the game autosaves, and that the prologue and some mission checkpoints do not trigger an autosave.
2. Use more than one slot. Each autosave replaces the last one (GameSpot), so a spare manual save is your backup.

## FAQ

### How do you save in GTA 6?
Pause menu, Game, Save Game, pick a slot (PC Gamer).

### Can you save anywhere?
In free roam, yes. During a mission, no (GameSpot).

### How many save slots are there?
15 (PC Gamer, GameSpot). GameSpot says autosave uses one of them. KeenGamer and PowerPyx say 15 manual plus 1 autosave.

### Does GTA 6 autosave?
Yes. Each autosave overwrites the previous one (GameSpot). Exactly when it triggers is unconfirmed.
