# How to Save in GTA 6: Manual Save, Autosave, Slots

> - **Quick answer:** open the pause menu (**Options** on PS5, **Menu** on Xbox), choose **Game**, then **Save**, then pick a slot (KeenGamer).
> - **You get 15 manual slots plus one autosave slot.** Autosave is overwritten every time (KeenGamer).
> - **You cannot save during a mission.** Save before big jobs, because missions cannot be replayed.

**Words used on this page.** *Manual save* means a save you make yourself. *Autosave* means the game saves for you. *Slot* is one place to keep a save. *Free roam* means you are walking or driving around with no mission running. *Pause menu* is the menu you open in the middle of the game.

*This page has one small spoiler box about when saving turns on.*

## How to save your game

1. Finish or leave your current mission. The save option stays locked while a mission is running (KeenGamer).
2. Open the pause menu. On PS5 press **Options**. On Xbox Series X|S press **Menu** (KeenGamer).
3. Select **Game**.
4. Select **Save**.
5. Pick a slot. Choose an empty one, or one you are happy to overwrite.
6. Confirm, then wait until the save finishes before you quit.

KeenGamer says you can do this anywhere in free roam. You do not have to drive home first.

:::spoiler When does saving turn on? (reveals an early story point)
KeenGamer says manual saving is turned off for the whole prologue. It turns on when the opening sequence ends and Jason gets to his house at the marina, right after the first job for Brian. Until then the game saves for you.
:::

## Autosave and slots

| Question | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| How many manual slots? | 15 | KeenGamer |
| How many autosave slots? | 1, and each new autosave overwrites the last | KeenGamer |
| When does it autosave? | After missions and open-world events | KeenGamer |

The danger is the overwrite. If the autosave happens right after something you wish had gone differently, the older state is gone unless you made a manual save (KeenGamer).

## When to save

GameSpot says the story is split into chapters, and each chapter centres on a different safehouse. Once you move on there is no going back, "unless you have a hard save". GameSpot recommends saving in each chapter if you want to revisit old safehouses or catch conversations you missed.

KeenGamer suggests these save points:

- After the prologue
- At the start and end of each chapter
- Before big story choices
- Two or three rotating slots for free roam

There is no mission replay in GTA 6. Read [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/beginner-tips/gta-6-replay-missions/) for what that means.

## Saving not working? Check these

- **You are in a mission.** Finish or leave it first (KeenGamer).
- **You are still in the prologue.** Manual saving is off until it ends (KeenGamer).
- **You used a cheat.** KeenGamer says any cheat, even a screen filter, turns off saving, trophies and achievements until you close the game and start it again.
- **Autosave overwrote what you wanted.** Open **Game**, then **Load Game**, and pick your newest manual save (KeenGamer).
- **Cloud backup.** KeenGamer says PS5 saves can back up to PlayStation Plus cloud storage and Xbox Series X|S saves sync to the cloud on their own. We have not checked this ourselves.

## FAQ

### How do you save in GTA 6?
Pause menu, Game, Save, pick a slot.

### Can you save anywhere?
In free roam, yes (KeenGamer). During a mission, no.

### How many save slots are there?
15 manual slots and 1 autosave slot (KeenGamer).

### Does GTA 6 autosave?
Yes, after missions and open-world events, into one slot that is overwritten (KeenGamer).
