# GTA 6 Festival Miami: Date, Lineup, Tickets (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** The GTA 6 Festival is a live music show on **Saturday 21 Nov 2026** at **Mana Wynwood in Miami**. Doors open at **4:00 PM ET (22:00 CET)**.
> - **Tickets were only for lottery winners.** The lottery closed on 10 Nov at 4 PM ET. Each code allowed **2 tickets**.
> - **You must be 17 or older** with a photo ID. Tickets cannot be passed on to someone else.

**Words used on this page.** *Festival* means a big music show with many artists. *Lottery* means names are picked at random. *Lineup* is the list of artists who play. *ET* is US East Coast time. *CET* is the time in Slovenia.

Rockstar calls it "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival". It celebrates the game's launch. Rockstar says the artists come from the game and its official soundtrack (Rockstar on X, 9 Nov).

## How the tickets worked

1. Fans signed up for a free lottery with a **DICE** account (DICE is a ticket app).
2. The sign-up closed on **Tue 10 Nov at 4:00 PM ET** (22:00 CET).
3. Winners got a code by **7:00 PM ET** that evening (Rockstar YouTube).
4. Winners could buy tickets from **Wed 11 Nov at 12:00 PM ET** (18:00 CET), first come, first served (DICE).
5. One code let you buy up to **2 tickets** (Rockstar YouTube).

**Can I still get a ticket?** The lottery is closed. DICE says tickets were offered only to lottery winners. We found no public sale. Do not buy tickets from strangers: tickets are not transferable, so a resold ticket may not get you in (Miami and Beaches).
