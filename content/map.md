# GTA 6 Map: Leonida's Size and 6 Regions (Updated)

> - **Quick answer:** The map is a US state called **Leonida**. Rockstar North's Aaron Garbut says it is about **twice the size of GTA 5's map**. The exact size is **not announced**.
> - **It has 6 regions.** The names are hidden below in a spoiler block, if you want to stay surprised.
> - **Free printable map:** Rockstar says a printable poster and Leonida map go on its GTA VI website on launch day (19 Nov 2026). Check it is live before you link to it.

**Words used on this page.** *Region* is a big area of the map. *Spoiler block* is a box you open to read. Open it only if you want that information.

## How big is it?

| Claim | Who said it | Label |
|---|---|---|
| About **2x GTA 5's map** | Aaron Garbut (Rockstar North) in Game Informer's cover story (29 Sep 2026). Forbes and Mapheist report it. Mapheist adds that Rob Nelson gave the same ratio in August, (we did not check the original report). | Developer statement, reported |
| About **3x Red Dead Redemption 2** | GameSpot reports the map is about three times the size of Red Dead Redemption 2's. Rockstar has not given a figure in square kilometres. | Reported by an outlet |
| Exact area in km² or miles | Nobody. Rockstar has published no figure. | Not announced |

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** A community-made map (YANIS v16) measures Leonida's land at about **85.6 km²**, a figure relayed by Notebookcheck and summarised by Mapheist. This is a fan estimate, not a Rockstar number. Source: https://mapheist.com/en/news/gta-6-game-informer-cover-story/

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** Forbes' "58.6 square miles" is the writer's own sum (twice GTA 5's area). It is not from Rockstar. Source: https://www.forbes.com/sites/brianmazique/2026/10/06/gta-6s-map-is-about-twice-the-size-of-gta-5-with-hurricanes-and-170-animal-species/

## Weather and wildlife

- **Weather** is local and keeps going. It can rain in one place while it is sunny next to it. **Hurricanes** are in the game. How often they happen is not announced. (Game Informer cover story via Mapheist; Forbes)
- **Wildlife:** **170+** animal species, sorted as **Rare, Discoverable and Legendary** (Game Informer cover story via Mapheist). Forbes alone reports **60+** bird types and **30+** land mammals. We could not re-open that article, so treat the split as reported, not confirmed.

## Regions of Leonida

Leonida has **6** regions. Their names are shown in Rockstar's official material. Open the box to see them.

:::spoiler The 6 region names (no story, names only)
- Vice City
- Leonida Keys
- Grassrivers
- Port Gellhorn
- Ambrosia
- Mount Kalaga National Park

Sources: Rockstar's GTA VI character page links to some of these regions. Forbes and PC Gamer list all six. For what each region is like, see the [Leonida hub](/launch-hub/leonida/).
