# GTA 6 Leonida Guide: All 6 Regions Explained (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** Leonida is the US state where GTA 6 is set. It has **6 regions**, **170+ animal species** and **hurricanes**.
> - **Each region has its own look**, from city streets to swamps to a national park. The names and details are hidden in spoiler boxes below.
> - **Rockstar has not announced** region sizes, borders or fast travel.

**Words used on this page.** *Region* is a large area of the map. *Spoiler box* is a box you open if you want that detail.

## What is Leonida?

Leonida is a made-up US state. USA Today calls it the series' take on Florida. Rockstar's tagline is "Vice City, USA. The darkest side of the sunniest place in America." Its nickname is "the Goodtime State". For size, see the [map page](map.md).

## Named places

:::spoiler Places named in official store text (no story)
- **Gambit Bay**: a place for fishing.
- **Washington Beach**: where the Shitzu Squalo boat is docked.
- **Watson Bay**: has the Paradise Garage, with a weapon locker and a place to drop off stolen goods.
- **Mount Kalaga**: backwoods for off-road driving.
- **Southside Vice City**: home to the PTT Youngin$ Compound.
- **Stockyard**: home to Stock 305 and Electric Fang Tattoo.
- **Lake Leonida**: home to One-Eyed Willie's mod shop.
- **Ocean Beach** and **Shore Drive**: named in the Vintage Vice City Pack and '95 Grotti Cheetah text (per Polygon).

The store text does not always say which region a place is in, so we have not guessed.
:::

## Weather, animals and activities

- **Weather:** rain, sun and wind are local and keep going. Hurricanes are in the game. (Game Informer, via Forbes)
- **Animals:** 170+ species, sorted as Rare, Discoverable and Legendary. 60+ are birds. 30+ are land animals. (Forbes)
- **Activities named in previews:** base jumping, scuba diving, kayaking, airboats, jet skis, racing on and off road, billiards, hiking dates, gyms, zoos, mini golf. (Forbes)
- **How often hurricanes hit, where to find Legendary animals, and what rewards you get:** not announced.
