# GTA 6 Snap Gun: How to Get It and How to Use It

> - **Quick answer:** the snap gun is a tool that opens locks faster and more quietly than kicking doors in (PC Gamer).
> - **It is locked behind the story.** You cannot get it early (PC Gamer). The details are in a closed box.
> - **Use it:** equip it, go to a locked door, then follow the prompt (PC Gamer).

**Words used on this page.** *Snap gun* is a lockpicking tool in GTA 6. *Sweet spot* is the right place to hold the stick so the lock opens. *Alarm* is the alert that calls the police. *Back door* is a door at the rear of a building. *Meter* is a bar that fills up.

*One box tells you how the snap gun unlocks. It mentions a Chapter 3 mission, so leave it closed if you like surprises.*

## How to use the snap gun

PC Gamer's steps:

1. Walk to a **locked door** with the snap gun **equipped**.
2. The prompt **"Use snap gun"** appears.
3. Use the **left stick** to find the lock's **sweet spot**.
4. Press the **right trigger** until the meter fills (R2 on PS5, RT on Xbox).

## When to use it

GameSpot says the snap gun lets Jason and Lucia break locks without triggering the alarm, so they can sneak in through a back door. PC Gamer says using it on a back door is a great way to reach the office and switch the alarm off completely.

But PC Gamer warns that the alarm is still active. It can still be set off by kicking open other doors, breaking display cases or a store worker.

## Why not earlier?

PC Gamer says the snap gun is locked behind story progress like the slim jim and the key cloner, so you cannot use it to make earlier missions easier.

## FAQ

### What does the snap gun do in GTA 6?
It opens locks faster and quieter than kicking doors in (PC Gamer).

### Does it turn off alarms?
No. It lets you open a door without setting the alarm off, but the alarm can still be triggered by other things (PC Gamer).

### Can I get it at the start?
No. It is a story unlock (PC Gamer).
