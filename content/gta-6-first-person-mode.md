# Does GTA 6 Have First-Person Mode? Not Fully: 3 Ways

> - **Quick answer:** **No full first-person mode.** GTA 6 does not have the GTA 5 mode where you play the whole game through your character's eyes (VGC, PowerPyx, Insider Gaming).
> - **What works:** a first-person look while **standing still**, while **aiming** some weapons, and while **driving with your phone out** (PowerPyx; VGC says some vehicles switch to first-person).
> - **Watch out:** as soon as you walk, the standing first-person view switches off (VGC, PowerPyx).

**Words used on this page.** *First-person* means you see through your character's eyes. *Third-person* means the camera is behind your character, so you see them. *L2 / LT* is the left trigger. *R3* means pressing the right stick down like a button (on Xbox you press the right stick too). *L3* means pressing the left stick down. *Focus* is a meter in GTA 6 that a special mode uses up.

*This page has no story spoilers. It is about the camera only.*

## Way 1: Look around in first person while standing still

The two guides describe the buttons a little differently. Try PowerPyx's way first. If it does not work, try VGC's way.

**PowerPyx's way**

1. Stand still.
2. Hold **L2** (Xbox: **LT**).
3. While holding it, press **R3** (Xbox: press the **right stick**).

**VGC's way**

1. Stand still.
2. Hold **L2** to zoom in a little.
3. While holding it, press **L3 and R3** together (both sticks). On Xbox that is **LT**, then both sticks. This Xbox naming is general Xbox naming, not a VGC line.

Now you can turn the camera and look around. Walk and the view goes back to normal.

**Careful:** VGC says pressing L3 and R3 **without** holding L2 first turns on Focus mode instead, and that uses up your Focus meter.

## Way 2: First-person while aiming

1. Equip a gun that has **sights** (a scope or optic on top).
2. Aim with **L2** (Xbox: **LT**).
3. While aiming, press **R3** (Xbox: press the **right stick**).

PowerPyx says you can add optics to your gun at any **gun store** by talking to the clerk.

## Way 3: First-person while driving (phone out)

1. Get in a car and drive.
2. Press **up on the D-pad** to take out your phone.
3. Keep driving with the phone out. PowerPyx says the camera is first-person **only while the phone is out**.

A player on Reddit reported the same thing: "No first person confirmed except when your on your phone while driving" ([r/GTA6](https://www.reddit.com/r/GTA6/comments/1xzdvfr/no_first_person_confirmed_except_when_your_on/)). Do not text and drive in real life.

Insider Gaming adds that the phone view itself can be changed in the **settings menu**, under **Camera**. It also says you can switch between three phone camera modes by holding **down on the D-pad**. We have not tested this.

## Will Rockstar add a full first-person mode?

**Not announced.** PowerPyx thinks it could come in a later patch because parts of it already exist. That is a guess, not news. GTA 5 got its first-person mode about a year after launch, in the 2014 PS4 and Xbox One version (VGC).

## FAQ

### Does GTA 6 have first-person mode?
Not a full one. You can only use first-person while standing still, while aiming some guns, or while driving with your phone out.

### How do I go first-person in GTA 6 on PS5?
Stand still, hold L2, then press R3 (PowerPyx). VGC says hold L2, then press L3 and R3.

### How do I go first-person in GTA 6 on Xbox?
Stand still, hold LT, then press the right stick (PowerPyx).

### Can I drive in first person in GTA 6?
Only with your phone out, says PowerPyx. Press up on the D-pad to take it out.

### Will GTA 6 get a full first-person mode later?
Not announced.
