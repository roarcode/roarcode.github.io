# GTA 6 Fast Travel: How It Works (RydeMe, Trains, Swap)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has no "tap the map and teleport". The main way is the **RydeMe** phone app: open your phone with **up on the D-pad** (VGC).
> - **Skipping the ride costs $100** on top of the normal fare (VGC and GameSpot).
> - **Cheaper:** buses and trains, or swap to your other character (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Fast travel* means getting to a far place quickly without driving the whole way. *RydeMe* is the taxi app in GTA 6. *D-pad* is the plus-shaped button pad. *Safehouse* is the home where you sleep and keep your things. *Bank account* is where your money is kept digitally.

*This page has one small box about when the app unlocks and one about swapping characters.*

## How to fast travel with RydeMe

1. Press **up on the D-pad** to open your phone (VGC).
2. Go to the **RydeMe** app.
3. Pick a place: your current safehouse, your map marker, or **New Destination** (VGC).
4. If you picked New Destination, the map opens. Choose where to go.
5. Wait for the car to arrive. If you cannot see it, look at the minimap (VGC).
6. Get in. Ride, pay to go faster, or pay to skip the whole ride.

VGC says skipping the whole ride costs **$100** on top of the original fee. GameSpot says the same $100 figure and adds that RydeMe feels expensive early on.

VGC also says that if you are somewhere a taxi cannot reach, the ride is cancelled.

:::spoiler When do you get RydeMe? (reveals an early mission name)
VGC says the app unlocks in the mission **Buck Me**, because it needs a bank account. Once you have the account, the app is added to your phone by itself. Before that you cannot use it.
:::

## Cheaper ways to get around

GameSpot lists two other ways:

| Way | How it works | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Buses and trains | Dozens of stops. They run on a schedule, so you wait. A train system reaches across the whole of Leonida. Public transport is more handy in Vice City than outside it. | GameSpot |
| Swap characters | Fast and free. The character you are not playing usually waits near your safehouse. | GameSpot |

:::spoiler When can you swap characters? (reveals when the second character becomes playable)
VGC says you cannot swap right after the first mission. The first time you play as the second character in the open world is after the end of Chapter 1. VGC says it will become obvious in Chapter 2 when you can swap. Then press **right on the D-pad** to open the swap menu. A greyed-out character is unavailable for a story reason.
:::

## Why you cannot warp from the map

GameSpot says you cannot "open your map and immediately warp to a point". That is why the three ways above exist.

## FAQ

### Is there fast travel in GTA 6?
Yes, but not from the map. Use RydeMe, public transport or a character swap.

### How much does it cost to skip a RydeMe ride?
$100 on top of the fare (VGC, GameSpot).

### Why can I not use RydeMe?
VGC says it unlocks after you get a bank account.

### What buttons on Xbox?
Phone: up on the D-pad, the same as PS5 (VGC). Other Xbox buttons are not named in the sources we checked.
