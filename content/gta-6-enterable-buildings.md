# GTA 6 Enterable Buildings: Dark Blue Means Enter

> - **Quick answer:** on the world map, **dark blue** buildings can be entered. **Light blue** ones cannot (VGC).
> - **Doors:** get close and the minimap shows the way in, usually marked with a **white line** (VGC).
> - **Focus Mode:** a **white** door can be opened. A **red** door is alarmed (VGC).

**Words used on this page.** *Interior* means the inside of a building. *Minimap* is the small map in the corner. *Focus Mode* is the mode you switch on by clicking both thumbsticks. It highlights things around you. *Alarmed* means a door will set off an alarm if you open it. *Floor plan* is a drawing of the inside of a building.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## Step 1: read the map colours

VGC says that on the world map most buildings are blue, in two shades:

| Colour | Meaning | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Darker blue | The building has an entrance or an interior | VGC |
| Lighter blue | There is no way in | VGC |

VGC says you can use this to explore the hundreds of interiors in Leonida.

## Step 2: look at the minimap

1. Walk up to a building that has an inside.
2. Look at the **minimap**. VGC says it shows where the enterable door is, usually with a white line.
3. When you are inside a shop, go near the doors at the back. VGC says the minimap then shows whether there is more to find.
4. VGC says hidden rooms and hidden staircases also show on the minimap if you get close enough.

## Step 3: use Focus Mode on doors

1. Click **both thumbsticks** to turn on Focus Mode (GameSpot).
2. Look at the door.
3. **White** means you can enter. **Red** means it is alarmed (VGC).

## Floor plans

GameSpot says that if you open your map while you are inside a building you can see the whole floor plan, including back doors and side entrances. It says most places have a back door, but not all.

## FAQ

### How do I know if I can enter a building in GTA 6?
Dark blue on the world map means it has an inside. Light blue does not (VGC).

### What do white and red doors mean?
White can be entered. Red is alarmed (VGC).

### How do I find hidden rooms?
Get close and watch the minimap (VGC).
