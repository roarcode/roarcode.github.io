# GTA 6 Trailers: All Official Videos and Dates (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has **4 big official videos**: Trailer 1 (5 Dec 2023), Trailer 2 (6 May 2025), An Extended Look (27 Aug 2026) and the Launch Trailer (15 Oct 2026).
> - **Watch them on Rockstar's own pages:** the Rockstar Games YouTube channel and [rockstargames.com/VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI).
> - **Spoiler tip:** this page lists dates and links only. The videos themselves show story scenes, so watch only if you are fine with that.

**Words used on this page.** *Trailer* is a short video that shows off a game. *Gameplay* means the game being played, not a movie scene. *Captured in-game* means filmed inside the real game, not made separately.

## An Extended Look (27 Aug 2026)

- It was the **first gameplay video** (VGC, GameSpot).
- It premiered on **Netflix at 3:00 PM ET** on 27 Aug. It came to YouTube and Rockstar's site at **9:00 PM ET** the same day (Rockstar YouTube).
- Rockstar says it was **captured in-game on PlayStation 5** (Rockstar YouTube).
- Rockstar also posted **6 short "Now Playing" clips** on YouTube the same night, and **2 "Coming August 27"** teasers on 6 Aug.

## Launch Trailer (15 Oct 2026)

- Rockstar says it was **captured in-game on PlayStation 5** (Rockstar YouTube).
- Its song is **"Flower in a Hurricane"** by Justice, featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard (Rockstar YouTube).
- The song is on the official album. See [GTA 6 radio stations and album](/launch-hub/radio-stations/).
- Rockstar uploaded it **twice** on YouTube, a few seconds apart ([version 1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=icy-lhqJhMQ), [version 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rz1B4B2yBZY)).
