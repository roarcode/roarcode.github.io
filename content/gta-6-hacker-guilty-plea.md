# GTA 6 Hacker Pleads Guilty: Sentence Due 23 November

> - **Quick answer:** The GTA 6 hacker from the 2022 video leak, **Arion Kurtaj**, pleaded guilty on **16 November 2026**.
> - **Next:** a judge is due to give his **sentence on 23 November 2026** at Woolwich Crown Court in London.
> - **Launch:** GTA 6 still comes out on **19 November**. Nothing about the game changed.

**Words used on this page.** A *hacker* is someone who breaks into computers. To *plead guilty* means to tell the court "I did it". A *sentence* is the punishment a judge gives. A *leak* is when secret things get out before they should.

## What happened

1. In **September 2022**, someone broke into Rockstar's computers.
2. They took **90 test videos** of GTA 6 and posted them online.
3. On **16 November 2026**, Arion Kurtaj, now 21, told a London court he did it.

Oxford Mail reports he pleaded guilty to **6 charges**. The Crown Prosecution Service (the people who take cases to court in England) says he admitted hacking **Rockstar, BT/EE, Revolut and Uber**.
