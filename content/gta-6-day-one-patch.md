# GTA 6 Day-One Patch: 01.002.001 Size and How to Check

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has **two day-one patches**: **01.002.000** (13 Nov, about 1 GB on PS5) and **01.002.001** (16 Nov, 602.5 MB on PS5, about 1.6 GB on Xbox).
> - **PS5 check:** highlight GTA VI on the home screen, press the **Options button**, select **Check for Update** (Vice).
> - **No patch notes yet.** Rockstar has not said what the patches change. Do not download from other sites.

**Words used on this page.** A *patch* is a small update that fixes or changes the game. *Day-one patch* means an update that is ready before or at launch. *Rollout in waves* means the update reaches players in groups, not all at once.

## The two patches

| Patch | Date | Size | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01.002.000 | 13 Nov 2026 | about 1 GB on PS5 | GTASeries on X; Vice |
| 01.002.001 | from 16 Nov 2026 | 602.5 MB on PS5, about 1.6 GB on Xbox (dataminers, per Vice) | Vice; Kotaku |

- Vice says 01.002.001 reached PS5 in three waves, 12 hours apart, and was due for everyone by 2 AM PT (11:00 CET) on Tue 17 Nov.
- Kotaku says anyone who has not done so also needs the 602 MB patch.
- Rockstar has not published patch notes for either patch (Vice).

## How to check on PS5

1. Go to the PS5 **home screen**.
2. Highlight **GTA VI**. Do not open it.
3. Press the **Options button** on the controller.
4. Select **Check for Update**.
5. If it says up to date but you are still on 01.002.000, check again later. The rollout came in waves (Vice).

## How to check on Xbox

Xbox Series X|S: press the **Xbox button**, open **My games & apps > See all > Manage > Queue**. If GTA VI is waiting, select it and choose **Resume installation** (general Xbox menu path, check on your console). No Xbox source gives the exact "check for update" button for GTA VI yet.

## Is this the same as the PS5 system update?

No. The patches are for the game. PS5 also needs a separate system update. See [the PS5 system update page](/glitches/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/).
