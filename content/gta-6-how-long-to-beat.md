# How Long Is GTA 6? Story and 100% Time (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** the story alone takes about **60 hours** (VGC) to **65 hours or more** (GameSpot) if you stick to the main missions.
> - **With side jobs:** about **90 to 100 hours** (GameSpot). VGC reached the credits at **100 hours** with a lot of side content done.
> - **Everything:** over **200 hours** (VGC and GameSpot). Nobody at GameSpot has finished 100% yet.

**Words used on this page.** *Main story* means the missions that move the plot along. *Side content* means the extra jobs you can skip, like races or gun ranges. *100% completion* means finishing the game's big checklist. *Hours* here are real-world hours of play.

*This page has no story spoilers. It only talks about how long things take.*

## Why the numbers are different

Every player is different. VGC says the time you spend is "likely not going to be measurable by a raw number", and its estimate comes from one playthrough. GameSpot gives a range because some of its staff explored more than others.

Polygon reports that Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, told the New York Times he played the whole game in February and it took him **80 hours**. He mostly followed the main story but also did "some optional goals". Polygon adds that new players will probably need more time than someone who has worked on the game.

So 60 hours (VGC) and 80 hours (Rockstar's Nelson, as reported by Polygon) are not wrong. They count different things.

## How many missions are there?

PC Gamer lists **five chapters and 91 story missions**.

## What counts as "everything"?

VGC says the traditional side missions, races and gun ranges come first. Then come the challenges that ask you to scan animals and landmarks and to find collectibles. Those are what push the game past 200 hours.

Insider Gaming says you do **not** have to do everything to reach 100%. For example, the checklist asks for 70 challenges, but there are 80 in total.

## How to plan your time

1. Pick your goal first: story only, story plus side jobs, or the full checklist.
2. If you want the story only, you can follow the main missions and skip the rest.
3. If you want side jobs, plan for 90 to 100 hours (GameSpot's range).
4. If you want 100%, plan for 200 hours or more.
5. Save in more than one slot as you go. There is no mission replay: see [Can you replay missions in GTA 6?](/beginner-tips/gta-6-replay-missions/).
