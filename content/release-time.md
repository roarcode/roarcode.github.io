# GTA 6 Release Time: Unlock Times by Time Zone (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 release time: it unlocks at **12:00 AM (midnight) on Thursday 19 November 2026 in your own time zone**, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
> - **Europe (CET, incl. Slovenia):** 00:00 CET on Thu 19 Nov. **UK:** 00:00 GMT. **US East:** 12:00 AM ET (= 06:00 CET). **New Zealand went first:** 12:00 CET on Wed 18 Nov 2026.
> - **US warning:** the US PlayStation Store showed **one Eastern moment for the whole country (12:00 AM ET = 9:00 PM Pacific)**. Xbox timing for the US is unconfirmed, so check your own countdown.

**Words used on this page.** A *rolling launch* means the game unlocks at midnight in each time zone, one after another, not all at once. *Pre-load* means downloading the game early so it is ready when it unlocks. *CET* is central European time, the time in Slovenia, Germany and Italy.

**Europe:** central Europe (Germany, Austria, Italy, France, Slovenia and more) unlocks at **00:00 CET on Thursday 19 November**. That is 23:00 UTC on Wednesday 18 November. It is *not* 06:00 CET: 06:00 CET is the US East Coast unlock. Slovenia details are in the table notes below.

**First unlock in the world:** New Zealand, at 12:00 CET on Wed 18 Nov 2026.

> **Do this first:** your console knows your real time. On PS5 open **Game Library**, then **Grand Theft Auto VI**. On Xbox open **My games & apps > Full library**, then **Grand Theft Auto VI**. The US caveat is explained under "Is the unlock midnight-local or global?".

## Is the unlock midnight-local or global?

- **Midnight local, region by region.** Rockstar's support article, as quoted by PC Gamer, says the game releases "midnight local time on November 19, 2026, in all regions" on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. PlayStation's own support page says the same. Sony also described a "rolling midnight launch by country, beginning at 12:00AM NZDT in New Zealand."
- **US exception in practice.** GamesRadar and PC Gamer report that US PlayStation unlock screens use midnight Eastern for everyone in the US. PC Gamer notes the same may be true on Xbox, but that is not confirmed. Rockstar's wording remains "midnight local time" (see the conflict table below).
- **Xbox store note.** GamesRadar reports Xbox runs a single global store, so time-zone tricks may behave differently there. Rockstar has not said anything about such tricks. We don't recommend them: changing your region or buying through a second account means you cannot move save data back to your main account (Push Square) and may break platform terms.

## Preload info

- **PlayStation 5:** preload opened at midnight local time on **10 November 2026** for digital pre-orders. (PlayStation support)
- **Xbox Series X|S:** preload opened at **midnight GMT on 10 November**. (USA Today, citing Rockstar's announcement)
- **Code-in-box (physical):** the box holds a download code, with no disc. Redeem it to start preloading. Boxed copies were slated for retail from **12 November**. (Rockstar announcement via USA Today and VGC; PlayStation support)
- **Size:** PlayStation tells players to clear **at least 212 GB** on PS5. Rockstar's figure is "up to 212 GB depending on console." (PlayStation, USA Today)
- Preloading does **not** unlock the game early. It only means you can press play the second your region hits midnight.

## FAQ

### What time does GTA 6 unlock in the US?
At midnight Eastern Time on Thursday 19 November, which is 9:00 PM Pacific on Wednesday 18 November, based on US PlayStation unlock screens seen by GamesRadar and PC Gamer.

### What time does GTA 6 release in the UK and Europe?
Midnight local time: 00:00 GMT in the UK and 00:00 CET in central Europe (including Slovenia), both on Thursday 19 November.

### Does GTA 6 unlock at 06:00 CET?
No. 06:00 CET is the US Eastern unlock. Central Europe unlocks at 00:00 CET.

### Which country gets GTA 6 first?
New Zealand, at 12:00 AM NZDT on 19 November (11:00 UTC on 18 November).

### Can I play GTA 6 early by preloading?
No. Preloading only downloads the game. It stays locked until your region's launch time.

### Is GTA 6 coming to PC?
No PC version has been announced on any official page we checked. The game is listed for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

### How big is the GTA 6 download?
Up to 212 GB depending on console. PlayStation recommends clearing at least 212 GB.
