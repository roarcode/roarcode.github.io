# GTA 6 Radio Stations: 6 Previewed Stations (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** Rockstar previewed **6 radio stations** on 8 Oct 2026: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics.
> - **Also confirmed:** on-demand **podcasts**, and listening **on foot** with in-game headphones or earbuds linked to your phone.
> - **Album:** "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album", **34 tracks, due 19 Nov**. Full in-game station lists are **not announced**.

**Words used on this page.** *Station* is a radio channel in the game. *DJ* is the host. *Podcast* is a talk show you pick to play.

## The 6 previewed stations (announced 8 Oct 2026)

| Station | Music | DJs or hosts named |
|---|---|---|
| **Cocoteo FM** | Modern Latin | El Martillo and La Gata |
| **Back Country Radio** | Renegade country | MW and Delta Dawne |
| **AfroBank FM** | African music | Burna and Palmsy |
| **The Chamber** | Classic metal | DJ Kerry and DJ Tom |
| **Flash FM** | Pop | Robyn and Alex |
| **Dirty South Classics** | Rap and hip-hop | Trick and Trina |

Source: Game Informer's report on Rockstar's Newswire post, 8 Oct 2026.

## Podcasts and listening on foot

- **Podcasts:** on-demand shows are part of the audio setup.
- **On foot:** you can listen with in-game headphones or earbuds that connect to your phone.
- **Exactly how to switch stations or start a podcast** (buttons and menus): not announced. We will add the exact PS5 and Xbox buttons after launch.
