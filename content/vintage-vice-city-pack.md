# GTA 6 Vintage Vice City Pack: How to Get It Before 20 Nov

> - **Quick answer:** The Vintage Vice City Pack is a **free bonus** with **every GTA 6 purchase or pre-order made before 20 November 2026**, Standard or Ultimate.
> - **Inside (per Polygon and The Verge):** a '55 Vapid Stanier car, a garage, outfits and hairstyles, and a tropical weapon pattern.
> - **One deadline:** Rockstar says before 20 Nov, the Xbox Store says before 19 Nov 23:59:59, PlayStation says by 19 Nov. **Buy by the end of 19 Nov.**

**Words used on this page.** *Bonus* is an extra you get for free. *GTA+* is Rockstar's monthly club. The free month is a different bonus.

## What is in the pack

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| '55 Vapid Stanier | A car |
| Garage | Has a weapon locker and a place to drop off stolen goods |
| Outfits and hairstyles | Per Polygon: a linen suit and a red sequin dress, one outfit for each lead, plus hairstyles |
| Weapon pattern | A tropical pattern for weapons |

Rockstar describes the pack as items "that flash back to when the neon burned brightest". How to open each item in the game is **not announced**. We will add the steps after launch.

## Who gets it

- **Digital Standard and Ultimate** bought or pre-ordered before 20 Nov 2026: yes.
- **Boxed (code-in-box) Standard:** yes. The Verge says anyone who pre-orders or buys before 20 Nov gets it, digital or physical.
- **Ultimate Edition Upgrade bought alone:** the pack is part of the **game** offer, not the upgrade. The PlayStation page lists the upgrade as not eligible for the GTA+ offer. Check the store text before you buy.

## How to buy before the deadline

**PS5**
1. Open **PlayStation Store**.
2. Search **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. Choose **Standard** or **Ultimate**.
4. Select the **pre-order** or **buy** button.
5. Check for a confirmation email. It has GTA+ steps.

**Xbox Series X|S**
1. Open the **Microsoft Store**.
2. Search **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. Choose **Standard** or **Ultimate**.
4. Select **Pre-order** or **Buy**.

## The free GTA+ month (separate bonus)

- **Who:** digital buyers before 20 Nov 2026. Boxed copies do not get it.
- **PS5:** after buying, open the **GTA+** page on PlayStation Store and follow the steps. **Claim by 31 March 2027.**
- **Xbox claim window:** redeem within 180 days of your pre-order (Xbox Store listing). One redemption per platform account.
- **Xbox:** open the Store, select your **profile icon** at the top left, select **Offers & credits**.
- **Cost:** free for one month, then it renews at the normal price until you cancel.
