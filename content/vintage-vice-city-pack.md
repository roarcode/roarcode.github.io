# GTA 6 Vintage Vice City Pack: How to Get It Before 20 Nov

> - **Quick answer:** The Vintage Vice City Pack is a **free bonus** with **every GTA 6 purchase or pre-order made before 20 November 2026**, Standard or Ultimate.
> - **Inside (per Polygon and The Verge):** a '55 Vapid Stanier car, a garage, outfits and hairstyles, and a tropical weapon pattern.
> - **One deadline:** Rockstar says before 20 Nov, the Xbox Store says before 19 Nov 23:59:59, PlayStation says by 19 Nov. **Buy by the end of 19 Nov.**

**Words used on this page.** *Bonus* is an extra you get for free. *GTA+* is Rockstar's monthly club. The free month is a different bonus.

## What is in the pack

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| '55 Vapid Stanier | A car |
| Garage | Has a weapon locker and a place to drop off stolen goods |
| Outfits and hairstyles | Per Polygon: a linen suit and a red sequin dress, one outfit for each lead, plus hairstyles |
| Weapon pattern | A tropical pattern for weapons |

Rockstar describes the pack as items "that flash back to when the neon burned brightest". How to open each item in the game is **not announced**. We will add the steps after launch.

## Who gets it

- **Digital Standard and Ultimate** bought or pre-ordered before 20 Nov 2026: yes.
- **Boxed (code-in-box) Standard:** yes. The Verge says anyone who pre-orders or buys before 20 Nov gets it, digital or physical.
- **Ultimate Edition Upgrade bought alone:** the pack is part of the **game** offer, not the upgrade. The PlayStation page lists the upgrade as not eligible for the GTA+ offer. Check the store text before you buy.

## How to buy before the deadline

**PS5**
1. Open **PlayStation Store**.
2. Search **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. Choose **Standard** or **Ultimate**.
4. Select the **pre-order** or **buy** button.
5. Check for a confirmation email. It has GTA+ steps.

**Xbox Series X|S**
1. Open the **Microsoft Store**.
2. Search **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. Choose **Standard** or **Ultimate**.
4. Select **Pre-order** or **Buy**.

## How to claim the pack in the game

The pack is **not ready the moment you start**. PC Gamer says you unlock it by playing the story first (PC Gamer, 18 Nov 2026).

**Spoiler-free tip:** if you cannot see the pack yet, you have not done anything wrong. Keep playing the story.

:::spoiler When the pack shows up, and where each item is (names an early mission and a story chapter)
- **See the pack:** the **store** in the main menu opens after mission 11, **"Coming Home"**, in Chapter 1. Open the main menu, select the store, then scroll down past GTA+ to the Vintage Vice City Pack (PC Gamer).
- **Claim it:** the items only work from **Chapter 3** (PC Gamer).
- **The car:** a new task appears in the **Exploration** part of the map index. It sends you to the **Ocean View Hotel** to collect the '55 Vapid Stanier and the garage (PC Gamer).
- **Outfits:** in your **wardrobe**, after you collect the car (PC Gamer).
- **Hairstyles:** in the **Special** menu at any **salon** (PC Gamer).
- **Weapon pattern:** at any **Ammu-Nation** (the gun store), open the **modify** menu, then **Cosmetics/Wraps** (PC Gamer).
:::

> **Report, not confirmed by Rockstar on its own pages:** RockstarINTEL says there are also **two tropical weapon patterns** you get by **linking your Rockstar Games account** to your PlayStation or Xbox account. Its steps: on the Rockstar Games website, sign in, then **Account > Manage Account > Connections**, link your console account, then restart the game. The patterns then show in **Cosmetics / Wraps** at Ammu-Nation (RockstarINTEL, 18 Nov 2026). We have not tested this.

## The free GTA+ month (separate bonus)

- **Who:** digital buyers before 20 Nov 2026. Boxed copies do not get it.
- **PS5:** after buying, open the **GTA+** page on PlayStation Store and follow the steps. **Claim by 31 March 2027.**
- **Xbox claim window:** redeem within 180 days of your pre-order (Xbox Store listing). One redemption per platform account.
- **Xbox:** open the Store, select your **profile icon** at the top left, select **Offers & credits**.
- **Cost:** free for one month, then it renews at the normal price until you cancel.
- **What GTA+ gives you in the game:** [GTA 6 GTA+ benefits](/beginner-tips/gta-6-gta-plus-benefits/).

## FAQ

### What is the deadline for the Vintage Vice City Pack?
Buy by the end of 19 November 2026. Rockstar says before 20 November, the Xbox Store says before 19 November 23:59:59, and PlayStation says by 19 November. None names a time zone, so buy early.

### Is the pack free?
Yes. It comes with the game purchase.

### Does the Standard Edition get it?
Yes. Both editions do.

### Does the boxed version get it?
Yes, per The Verge (digital or physical, before 20 November). The free GTA+ month is digital only.

### I bought the game. Why can't I see my pack?
PC Gamer says the pack appears in the main menu store only after an early story mission, and the items work later in the story. Keep playing. The details are in the spoiler box above.
