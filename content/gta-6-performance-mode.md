# GTA 6 Performance Mode: Is There 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

> - **Quick answer:** **No performance mode or 60 FPS.** Rockstar targets 30 FPS on every console, and TechRadar says there are no graphics modes on PS5 or Xbox.
> - **PS5 Pro gets:** a sharper picture and ray-traced shadows. Digital Foundry's review (18 Nov) measured a steady 30 FPS on Pro and dips to the mid-20s on base PS5.
> - **Careful with tools** that promise 60 or 120 FPS. They are unofficial. We do not link them.

**Words used on this page.** *FPS* means frames per second, how many pictures the game draws each second. *Performance mode* is a setting that trades looks for more FPS. *PSSR* is PS5 Pro's way of making the picture sharper. *Ray-traced shadows* are shadows drawn with light-ray maths. *Frame generation* means a tool invents extra pictures.

## What Rockstar said

- Rockstar says GTA VI "has been fully developed from the ground up" for current consoles, "targeting 30 frames per second" (via Game Informer, 10 Nov).
- **PS5 Pro:** "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro."
- **PS5 and PS5 Pro:** high-fidelity haptic feedback on the DualSense, adjustable in accessibility settings, plus Tempest 3D audio and better load times.
- **Xbox:** Rockstar's 10 Nov post covers Xbox Cloud Gaming on Series S graphics settings (Game Informer). On 18 Nov, Rockstar gave Wccftech the base resolution for every console (see the table below).
- **No 60 FPS or performance mode is announced.** See [platforms](/launch-hub/platforms/) and [PS5 settings](/launch-hub/ps5-settings/).

## Confirmed facts and analysis, side by side

**Confirmed by Rockstar**
- Rockstar's statement on 10 Nov: GTA VI is "targeting 30 frames per second" on current consoles (via Game Informer). No performance, quality or 40 FPS mode is announced.
- On 18 Nov, Rockstar answered questions from TechRadar. Jonathon Ashcroft, Rockstar's senior vice president of technology, said: "The complexity of simulating and rendering our world, with so many systems that need to weave together dynamically, requires us to carefully balance how we spend every millisecond of both the central processor and the graphics processor." He added: "It's a tremendous amount of work to do and only 33ms to do it. The frame rate we've chosen gives us the right balance of presentation, simulation, and gameplay." ([TechRadar, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/why-no-40fps-mode-for-gta-6-rockstar-says-the-complexity-of-simulating-and-rendering-our-world-requires-it-to-carefully-balance-how-we-spend-every-millisecond-of-both-the-central-processor-and-the-graphics-processor))
- PS5 Pro: "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro" (Rockstar, via Game Informer and The Verge).
- **No 60 or 40 FPS mode later on current consoles, not even PS5 Pro.** Kotaku asked Rockstar about a future 60 or 40 FPS mode, and Kotaku says Rockstar confirmed "this isn't going to happen, even on PS5 Pro." Marc Chambers, Rockstar's technology production director, told Kotaku the PS5 Pro's processor "is not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates." ([Kotaku, 18 Nov 2026](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-wont-get-a-60-fps-mode-even-on-ps5-pro-because-its-not-powerful-enough-2000755979))

**Analysis by Digital Foundry (their opinion, not Rockstar's)**
- Digital Foundry's article on the Extended Look gameplay video, dated **Fri 28 Aug 2026**, says the game itself "targets 30fps" and that this is now "100 percent confirmed". It also says a 60 FPS version looks "extremely unlikely on console hardware" because the frame time would drop from 33.3 ms to 16.7 ms. ([Digital Foundry, 28 Aug 2026](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-an-extended-look-analysis-perhaps-the-most-impressive-showcase-weve-ever-seen-in-real-time-rendering))
- That analysis was made on footage from the base PS5. It was not made from a finished game.
- On 17 Nov, Dexerto reported Digital Foundry's Richard Leadbetter saying a 40 FPS mode would still need a "huge amount of optimization or cutbacks" to reach 25 ms per frame, and that "30 FPS makes sense". ([Dexerto, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-running-at-30-fps-makes-sense-experts-say-after-rockstars-confirmation-3428936/))

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** Digital Foundry wrote that, "according to reports from those speaking to Rockstar", higher frame-rate modes "will be considered for post-launch updates". Rockstar has not said this itself. Source: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-an-extended-look-analysis-perhaps-the-most-impressive-showcase-weve-ever-seen-in-real-time-rendering

**Update 18 Nov:** Kotaku now reports that Rockstar ruled out a 60 or 40 FPS mode on current consoles, PS5 Pro included (see above). So do not wait for one on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S.

## Digital Foundry's tech review (18 Nov 2026)

Digital Foundry (DF) tests how games run. Its review by John Linneman went up on 18 Nov. It covers **PS5 and PS5 Pro**. It does not cover Xbox yet. ([Digital Foundry review](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/reviews/grand-theft-auto-6-a-landmark-achievement-that-redefines-a-console-generation))

| What DF checked | PS5 | PS5 Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Frame rate | Mostly **30 FPS**. Small dips in fast driving. Drops to the **mid-20s** are possible in long fights with explosions. | A locked **30 FPS** with perfect frame pacing (every frame arrives on time). |
| Base resolution | **1440p** (2560x1440) | **1440p** (2560x1440) |
| Final picture | Normal upscaling. Softer, with less sharp detail far away. | **PSSR** upscaling. DF says it looks a lot like 4K. |
| Ray tracing | Ray-traced lighting (global illumination) and reflections | The same, **plus ray-traced shadows** |

- DF calls 30 FPS "a deliberate design decision" so the game can simulate a busy world.
- DF says HDR highlights go above **1500 nits** (a measure of brightness). That matters only if your TV supports HDR.
- **Xbox:** not in this review. We will add DF's Xbox numbers when they publish them.

**Words in the table.** *Resolution* is how many dots make up the picture. *1440p* is fewer dots than 4K. *Upscaling* means the console makes a smaller picture bigger to fit your TV. *Frame pacing* means the frames arrive at even gaps, so motion looks smooth.

## Every console: Rockstar's own resolution numbers (18 Nov 2026)

Rockstar answered written questions from Wccftech during the review period. These numbers are Rockstar's words, not a test ([Wccftech, 18 Nov 2026](https://wccftech.com/grand-theft-auto-vi-amd-fsr-4-xbox-series-s-ray-tracing/)).

| Console | Base resolution | Made bigger to | How |
|---|---|---|---|
| PS5 | 1440p | 4K | Rockstar's own TAA |
| Xbox Series X | 1440p | 4K | Rockstar's own TAA |
| PS5 Pro | 1440p | 4K | PSSR2 |
| Xbox Series S | **720p** | **1080p** | AMD FSR 4 |

- Every console targets **30 FPS** (Rockstar).
- Rockstar says ray-traced lighting is on **every** console, including Series S, but it is tuned differently on each one.
- The menus and on-screen text are drawn at 4K on all consoles.
- **Why Series S looks softer:** it starts from 720p, half the height of 1440p. So the Series S picture is softer. That is how the game is made, not a fault with your console.

**More words.** *TAA* and *FSR 4* are ways to make a small picture bigger and smoother. *720p* and *1080p* are smaller pictures than 1440p and 4K.

## FAQ

### Does GTA 6 have a performance mode?
No. TechRadar says GTA 6 has no graphics modes on PS5 or Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar targets 30 FPS on every console.

### Why does GTA 6 drop below 30 FPS on my PS5?
Digital Foundry saw small dips on base PS5 during fast driving, and drops to the mid-20s in long fights with explosions. It is the game, not your console. No fix is announced.

### Does PS5 Pro run GTA 6 at 60 FPS?
No. PS5 Pro runs at 30 FPS. Digital Foundry measured a locked 30 FPS on Pro, with a sharper picture and ray-traced shadows.

### Will GTA 6 get a 60 FPS mode in a later update?
Not on current consoles. Kotaku says Rockstar confirmed it will not happen, even on PS5 Pro, because the processor is not fast enough for the game's world.

### Can I get 60 FPS on Xbox?
No. Rockstar targets 30 FPS on Xbox Series X and Series S too. Xbox Cloud Gaming uses Series S graphics settings.

### What resolution does GTA 6 run at on Xbox Series S?
Rockstar told Wccftech that Series S starts at 720p and is made bigger to 1080p with AMD FSR 4. Series X starts at 1440p and goes up to 4K.

### Should I cancel my pre-order over 30 FPS?
That is your choice. This page only reports what is confirmed.
