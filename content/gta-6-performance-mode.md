# GTA 6 Performance Mode: Is There 60 FPS on PS5 Pro?

> - **Quick answer:** **No performance mode or 60 FPS is announced.** Rockstar says GTA VI is "targeting 30 frames per second" on every console (Game Informer).
> - **PS5 Pro gets:** sharper picture with PSSR and ray-traced shadows. Not more frames.
> - **Careful with tools** that promise 60 or 120 FPS. They are unofficial. We do not link them.

**Words used on this page.** *FPS* means frames per second, how many pictures the game draws each second. *Performance mode* is a setting that trades looks for more FPS. *PSSR* is PS5 Pro's way of making the picture sharper. *Ray-traced shadows* are shadows drawn with light-ray maths. *Frame generation* means a tool invents extra pictures.

## What Rockstar said

- Rockstar says GTA VI "has been fully developed from the ground up" for current consoles, "targeting 30 frames per second" (via Game Informer, 10 Nov).
- **PS5 Pro:** "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro."
- **PS5 and PS5 Pro:** high-fidelity haptic feedback on the DualSense, adjustable in accessibility settings, plus Tempest 3D audio and better load times.
- **Xbox:** Rockstar's post covers Xbox Cloud Gaming on Series S graphics settings and gives no performance numbers for Xbox consoles (Game Informer).
- **No 60 FPS or performance mode is announced.** See [platforms](/launch-hub/platforms/) and [PS5 settings](/launch-hub/ps5-settings/).

## Confirmed facts and analysis, side by side

**Confirmed by Rockstar**
- Rockstar's statement on 10 Nov: GTA VI is "targeting 30 frames per second" on current consoles (via Game Informer). No performance, quality or 40 FPS mode is announced.
- On 18 Nov, Rockstar answered questions from TechRadar. Jonathon Ashcroft, Rockstar's senior vice president of technology, said: "The complexity of simulating and rendering our world, with so many systems that need to weave together dynamically, requires us to carefully balance how we spend every millisecond of both the central processor and the graphics processor." He added: "It's a tremendous amount of work to do and only 33ms to do it. The frame rate we've chosen gives us the right balance of presentation, simulation, and gameplay." ([TechRadar, 18 Nov 2026](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/why-no-40fps-mode-for-gta-6-rockstar-says-the-complexity-of-simulating-and-rendering-our-world-requires-it-to-carefully-balance-how-we-spend-every-millisecond-of-both-the-central-processor-and-the-graphics-processor))
- PS5 Pro: "improved clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows only available on PlayStation 5 Pro" (Rockstar, via Game Informer and The Verge).

**Analysis by Digital Foundry (their opinion, not Rockstar's)**
- Digital Foundry's article on the Extended Look gameplay video, dated **Fri 28 Aug 2026**, says the game itself "targets 30fps" and that this is now "100 percent confirmed". It also says a 60 FPS version looks "extremely unlikely on console hardware" because the frame time would drop from 33.3 ms to 16.7 ms. ([Digital Foundry, 28 Aug 2026](https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-an-extended-look-analysis-perhaps-the-most-impressive-showcase-weve-ever-seen-in-real-time-rendering))
- That analysis was made on footage from the base PS5. It was not made from a finished game.
- On 17 Nov, Dexerto reported Digital Foundry's Richard Leadbetter saying a 40 FPS mode would still need a "huge amount of optimization or cutbacks" to reach 25 ms per frame, and that "30 FPS makes sense". ([Dexerto, 17 Nov 2026](https://www.dexerto.com/gta/gta-6-running-at-30-fps-makes-sense-experts-say-after-rockstars-confirmation-3428936/))

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** Digital Foundry wrote that, "according to reports from those speaking to Rockstar", higher frame-rate modes "will be considered for post-launch updates". Rockstar has not said this itself. Source: https://www.digitalfoundry.net/features/gta-6-an-extended-look-analysis-perhaps-the-most-impressive-showcase-weve-ever-seen-in-real-time-rendering
