# GTA 6 Robberies: Safe Codes, Alarms and Clean Getaways

> - **Quick answer:** for a clean robbery do this in order: control the crowd, switch off the alarm, loot, destroy the CCTV evidence, escape (GameSpot).
> - **Safe codes are random.** A code list from the internet will not work, and the code changes even if you rob the same shop twice (GameSpot).
> - **Check the map first** for the store's security level: Low, Medium or High (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Robbery* means taking money or goods from a place. *Security level* tells you how hard a place is to rob. *Crowd control* means keeping workers and customers calm and out of your way. *Alarm* is the alert that calls the police. *CCTV* means security cameras. *Safe* is a locked box for valuables. *Fence* is someone who buys stolen goods.

*Some tools and places in this guide unlock later in the story. Those details are in closed boxes.*

## Before you go in

GameSpot says to open the map and look at the store's **security level** first. Many things decide it, but nearly every place has an employee, an alarm and security cameras.

GameSpot says that in the starting area, apart from the bank, every shop has a **low** security level. That means no armed guards and few cameras or alarms. Low shops usually need only one character. For Medium or High, robbing as a pair is much easier. GameSpot says you can invite your partner by pressing **Triangle** (PS5) or **Y** (Xbox) on the map icon.

## The five steps, in order (GameSpot)

1. **Control the crowd.** Aim at the worker so they empty the register, or knock them out and take the cash yourself. In bigger places, rob as a pair and the other character handles the crowd.
2. **Switch off the alarm.** Smashing displays or breaking doors can set it off. Look for a **white panel** on the wall, usually in a back room. Focus Mode highlights it. Press the interact button on the panel.
3. **Loot.** Empty the register and the back room. Once the alarm is off you can smash displays safely.
4. **Destroy the CCTV evidence.** Find the security room. GameSpot says you can also shoot cameras first in Focus Mode (click both thumbsticks), but only if you have a silenced weapon.
5. **Escape.** The cops will be alerted when you leave, but if you destroyed the evidence they have nothing to go on.

GameSpot adds that if a camera spots you and you do not destroy the evidence, the police can identify you when you leave. Being seen does not alert them at once.

## Safe codes

GameSpot says that in its playthroughs the codes were **random**. They are not the same for different players, and they change if you rob the same shop again. GameSpot adds that codes can be found:

- on a guard you loot
- on notes or computer screens nearby (Focus Mode helps)

If you cannot find a code, GameSpot says small safes can be picked and lockboxes can be snapped open. Almost all safes can also be opened by force, but GameSpot calls that Plan B.

## Back doors

GameSpot says to open the map inside a building to see its floor plan, including back doors and side doors.

:::spoiler Which tool opens back doors quietly? (reveals a story unlock)
GameSpot says the **Snap Gun** is a key item you unlock by progressing the story. It lets Jason and Lucia break locks without setting off the alarm, so they can sneak in through the back door.
:::

## Banks, loan stores and vaults

GameSpot says banks and loan stores hold **marked bills**. You must sell them to a fence before you can use them. They also carry **dye packs** that explode when they get too far from the building. If one goes off, the money is useless.

:::spoiler How do you beat dye packs? (reveals a story unlock in Chapter 3)
GameSpot says the only way to disable dye packs is a **dye pack jammer**, which you do not get until Chapter 3. GameSpot's tips page says not to rob banks or loan stores before then.
:::

GameSpot's other bank tips:

- High-security places have armed guards. Deal with them first. Keep threatening until they kneel and drop the gun. If you look away early, they will shoot.
- A camera with a **cloud icon** means the footage is stored online, so you cannot destroy it. Destroy cameras as you go instead.
- Most banks have a back door and a side door. One is usually reinforced and needs an explosive. The other can be opened with the Snap Gun.
- Bank safe codes can be on sticky notes, computer screens and guards.

:::spoiler How do you get into a bank vault? (reveals a Chapter 3 contact)
GameSpot says vaults cannot be picked or blown open. You wait for an armoured cash truck. When a worker leaves the truck, the vault is open for a short time. GameSpot says to follow a truck you see, or get tips on truck locations from a fence you meet in Chapter 3.
:::

## Getting away

GameSpot says to change your look or clothes as soon as you are out, then put distance between you and the place. If you went in loud, park near the exit. If you went in quiet, GameSpot suggests keeping your car in a nearby garage. If you were a pair, split up.

## FAQ

### Are GTA 6 safe codes the same for everyone?
No. GameSpot says they are random and change each time.

### How do I turn off a store alarm?
Find the white panel on the wall, often in the back room, and interact with it (GameSpot).

### Can I rob a bank at the start?
GameSpot says not to rob banks or loan stores until Chapter 3, because of dye packs.
