# GTA 6 Not Working? Servers Down Check and 7 Fixes

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 not working? **Open the 3 status pages below first.** If one shows a problem, wait. If all are green, restart your console and check that the download is 100% done.
> - **Status pages:** [Rockstar Service Status](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus), [PlayStation Network status](https://status.playstation.com), [Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status).
> - **Not unlocked yet?** The game stays locked until midnight in your time zone on 19 Nov 2026. See [is GTA 6 out](/launch-hub/is-gta-6-out/).

**Words used on this page.** A *server* is a computer far away that your game talks to. *Status page* means a web page where the company says if its servers work. *Restart* means turn the console off and on again.

## Step 1: Check if the servers are down

1. Open the **[Rockstar Service Status](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus)** page. Rockstar says to check it for outages and maintenance.
2. On PS5, open **[status.playstation.com](https://status.playstation.com)**. If it shows an alert, the problem is on Sony's side, so wait.
3. On Xbox, open **[Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status)**.
4. If any page shows a problem, stop. Nothing you change on your console fixes a server problem. Try again later.

## Step 2: The 7 fixes, fastest first

1. **Check the clock.** Is it past midnight in your time zone on 19 Nov 2026? If not, the game is locked on purpose. See the [release time table](/launch-hub/release-time/).
2. **Check the download.** It must be 100% done. See [GTA 6 download stuck](/glitches/gta-6-download-stuck/).
3. **Restart the console.**
   - PS5: press the **PS button**, select **Power**, then **Restart PS5** (PlayStation support).
   - Xbox: hold the **Xbox button** on the controller, select **Restart console**, then **Restart** (general Xbox menu path, check on your console).
4. **Check you are on the right account.** Use the account that bought the game or entered the box code.
5. **PS5 only: turn on Console Sharing and Offline Play** if someone else on your PS5 owns the game. Go to **Settings > Users and Accounts > Other > Console Sharing and Offline Play**, then select **Enable** (PlayStation support).
6. **Test your internet.**
   - PS5: **Settings > Network > Connection Status > Test Internet Connection** (PlayStation support).
   - Xbox: press the **Xbox button**, then **Profile & system > Settings > General > Network settings > Test network connection** (general Xbox menu path, check on your console).
7. **Install updates.** PS5: **Settings > System > System Software > System Software Update and Settings**. Xbox: let the console finish any update it asks for.
   - **Required for GTA 6:** PlayStation says a PS5 system update must be installed before you play. See [GTA 6 PS5 update required (14.23.00): how to install](/glitches/gta-6-ps5-system-update-required/).

## FAQ

### Are the GTA 6 servers down?
Check the three status pages above. We do not guess. We will post confirmed outages here when Rockstar, PlayStation or Xbox say so.

### Why is GTA 6 not working on my PS5?
The most common causes are a locked game (before unlock time), an unfinished download, the wrong account and a service outage. Work through the 7 fixes.

### Why is GTA 6 not working on Xbox One?
Xbox One streams GTA 6 through Xbox Cloud Gaming. It starts at 1:00 AM ET on 19 Nov (07:00 CET) and needs a good internet connection (Xbox Wire).

### Does GTA 6 need to be online?
Not announced on any page we checked. The PlayStation Store lists it as a single-player game. We will update this when Rockstar confirms.
