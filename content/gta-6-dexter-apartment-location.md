# GTA 6 Dexter's Apartment Location: Washington Island

> - **Quick answer:** GameSpot says it is on **Washington Island** in the east of Vice City, between the **Pay 'n' Spray** and **Vice Beach Top Fun Drones** icons.
> - **You cannot go inside.** The apartment is locked (GameSpot).
> - **Nothing else Dexter-related** was found there, apart from a boat that looks familiar (GameSpot).

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** this is a map secret, not a story twist. The step-by-step location is in a closed box.

**Words used on this page.** *Easter egg* is a hidden nod in a game. *Subregion* is a smaller area of the map. *Apartment complex* is a building with many flats. *Center console boat* is a small open motor boat.

## What is it?

GameSpot says Rockstar closely recreated Dexter Morgan's Miami apartment from the TV show *Dexter*, which ran from 2006 to 2013. Fans spotted it first in a pre-launch gameplay showcase. GameSpot says the in-game location is hard to find and not where real Miami would put it.

## How to find it

:::spoiler Where is the apartment? (gives the exact place)
GameSpot's steps:

1. Open the large map and go to the **Washington Island** subregion, in the east of Vice City. It is east by northeast of Penmarch Isle.
2. Go to where the **Pay 'n' Spray** and **Vice Beach Top Fun Drones** icons are.
3. Between them, look for a small **light-blue building** with a **cylinder shape on its south side**.
4. The cylinder is a **staircase**. Take it to the **second floor**.
5. Head **west** along the outside hallway, with the flats on your left.
6. The apartment is the **last room**, a corner room with a water view that looks slightly bigger than the others.

GameSpot says the outdoor hallway matches the end of the TV show's intro.
:::

## What you can do there

- Walk up the stairs and along the hallway (GameSpot).
- You cannot get in. GameSpot says the apartment is locked and it saw no Dexter objects nearby.
- GameSpot says a center console boat docked just west of the building looks like Dexter's boat, but it does not have the name from the show.

GameSpot says it saw trash bags by the docks but does not think they are an easter egg.
