**Unofficial fan site. Not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.**

Leonida Guides is an independent, fan-made site with guides for Grand Theft Auto VI. Grand Theft Auto, GTA and related names and marks are trademarks of their respective owners. We use no Rockstar logos or ripped assets.

## How we work

- **Every guide credits its sources.** Sources are listed at the bottom of each page and linked.
- **Before launch we publish only confirmed facts** from official Rockstar, PlayStation and Xbox material or reputable previews.
- **Rumours are always labelled.** Anything unconfirmed carries a clear "Rumour / unconfirmed" callout with a source.
- **Every page shows when it was last updated.** Guides change as patches land and as we learn more.
- **Written with AI help and fact-checked against the cited sources.** Each page lists its sources and an updated date, and carries the team byline rather than a named author.
- **Spoilers are labelled.** Each guide shows a spoiler level, and story details sit behind click-to-reveal boxes.

## Corrections

When we find an error, we fix it and update the page's "last updated" date.
