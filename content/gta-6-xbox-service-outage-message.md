# GTA 6 Xbox Service Outage Message: What to Do

> - **Quick answer:** If Xbox shows a **service outage** message for GTA 6, open the **Xbox status page**. If a service has an alert, wait and try again.
> - **Single player?** Xbox Support says you can play offline: **Xbox button > Profile & system > Settings > General > Network settings > Go offline**.
> - **Not confirmed as a big outage.** Two players posted about it at the New Zealand unlock. Xbox has not said what caused it.

**Words used on this page.** A *service outage* means an online service is down. *Xbox status page* is a web page that shows which Xbox services work. *Offline mode* lets you play without a connection to Xbox servers.

## What Xbox Support says to do

This message can appear when you try to play a game. Xbox says it is probably linked to network maintenance or a service outage.

1. Open the [Xbox status page](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status).
2. Look for any service with an alert.
3. Expand that service and scroll to **Notifications**.
4. Sign in to get a message when the service is working again.
5. Wait a little and try GTA VI again.

## Play offline while you wait

Only for single-player play. GTA VI is single-player at launch (see [GTA 6 online](/launch-hub/gta-6-online/)).

1. Press the **Xbox button** on your controller.
2. Go to **Profile & system**.
3. Select **Settings**.
4. Select **General**.
5. Select **Network settings**.
6. Select **Go offline**.

To go back online, return to the same menu and choose the option to connect again.

![Xbox Network settings with Go offline highlighted](TODO: image:gta-6-xbox-service-outage-message-step-2 — menu screen, Xbox: Settings > General > Network settings with Go offline highlighted)

A guide on axeetech.com warns that offline play may fail for a game that has not checked its licence on your console yet. This is not confirmed for GTA VI.

- Check [Rockstar Service Status](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus).

## FAQ

### Why does Xbox say service outage for GTA 6?
Xbox Support says it is probably network maintenance or an outage. Check the status page.

### Can I play GTA 6 offline on Xbox?
Xbox Support says single-player games can be played offline. Use Go offline in Network settings.

### Is the Xbox down for everyone?
Check the status page. At the New Zealand unlock two players posted about the message. That is not proof of a big outage.
