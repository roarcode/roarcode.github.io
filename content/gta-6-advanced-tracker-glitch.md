# GTA 6 Advanced Tracker Glitch: Deliver Supercars, No Cops

> - **Quick answer:** clone the key, tell your **partner to wait** in the stolen car, get in **another vehicle**, then tell them to **follow you**. While they drive, the red tracker does not call the police (Insider Gaming, Polygon, VICE).
> - **What it gets you:** the car arrives with no chase and often no damage. VICE got **$30,000** for one delivery with the perfect bonus. Game Rant lists **$9,650 plus $5,000** for a perfect Ocelot Jugular.
> - **Status: working in reports up to 26 Nov.** No newer patch as of 27 Nov. Four outlets describe it. Insider Gaming thinks it is probably a bug. Not tested by us.

**Words used on this page.** A *glitch* is a bug you can use on purpose. A *tracker* is a device in a car that tells the police where it is. An *advanced tracker* calls the police if you drive too slowly. GameSpot calls this the *red tracker*. Your *partner* is the main character you are not playing as (Jason or Lucia). The *partner menu* is the list of orders you give your partner. The *key cloner* is a phone tool that opens keyless cars.

*Story mode, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. No mods or PC tools. The buyer's name and the unlock missions are in a closed box.*

## What the advanced tracker does

- GameSpot says a car with a red tracker pings the police if you drop below a certain speed for about **5 seconds**.
- Polygon says the tracker stays on until the car is registered in your name. Insider Gaming says the buyer tells you there is no real way to switch it off.
- So the normal way is a fast, nervous drive across the map. One crash or traffic jam can bring the police.

## Before you start

1. **You need both characters together.** Your partner must be with you (Polygon). It does not matter if you play as Jason or Lucia (Polygon). VICE shows it with Jason driving the stolen car.
2. **You need the key cloner.** You get it partway through the story (see the box below).
3. **Bring a second vehicle,** or plan to get one. Polygon says you can call one with the **Scooter Bros** app or steal any car nearby. Game Rant uses a **RydeMe** taxi instead.

:::spoiler Who buys these cars and when the tracker unlocks (names a character and two missions)
The buyer is **Randy**. He has a **Wish List** of high-end cars. Polygon says you unlock the key cloner after stealing a **Zorusso** for Randy at the start of **Chapter 3**.

VICE says the glitch works before Randy's official tracker removal. VICE says that service unlocks after the Chapter 4 mission **Photo/Shoot**, delivering the **Declasse Mamba GT** and a follow-up call from Randy. GameSpot says red-tracker removal at a Pay 'n' Spray comes only after you finish all of the buyer's car jobs. Polygon says that once you have delivered most cars on the Wish List, you can sell any advanced-tracker car to Randy or a Pay 'n' Spray.
:::

## How to do the advanced tracker glitch

1. Find the car you want. Open it with the **key cloner** (VICE).
2. Let **both** characters get in. **Do not drive off.** Polygon says the tracker does not start if you do not start driving.
3. Hold **Right on the D-pad** to open the partner menu (Polygon, VICE, Game Rant).
4. Pick **Wait In**, so your partner stays in the car (VICE). Game Rant says to press **Square or X**.
5. Get out. Get into your **second vehicle** (Polygon, VICE).
6. Hold **Right on the D-pad** again. Pick **Follow Me** (VICE, Polygon).
7. Drive to the buyer at a normal speed. Your partner drives the stolen car behind you. The tracker does not go off, even when they drive slowly (Insider Gaming).
8. When your partner arrives, get into the stolen car and deliver it (VICE). Insider Gaming says your partner can even roll it through the garage door for you.

## The fastest version: take a taxi (Game Rant)

Game Rant's way skips the drive.

1. Do steps 1 to 4 above.
2. Book a **RydeMe** taxi to the buyer.
3. Once you are in the taxi, pick **Follow Me** in the partner menu.
4. **Skip the journey.** Game Rant says your partner pulls up behind you shortly after you arrive.

Game Rant says the car gets there in one piece and the police never come.

## Selling at a Pay 'n' Spray instead

Polygon says this also works when you take a tracked car to a Pay 'n' Spray, once those shops can take it.

1. Park just outside the shop.
2. Walk to your partner in the stolen car. Tell them to **regroup** (Polygon).
3. Drive the stolen car straight into the shop yourself.

Polygon says the tracker will go off at that moment, but it does not matter if you drive in at once.

## Tips and problems

- **The car gets damaged.** Polygon says your partner tends to copy your driving. Drive carefully and the stolen car usually arrives in good shape too.
- **Some spots are harder.** Insider Gaming says it is harder in some spots, for example when the car is shut in somewhere.
- **It does not remove the tracker.** If you drive the car yourself, the tracker works as normal (Polygon).
- **Other way: tow it.** Insider Gaming says a car with a winch can tow the stolen car, but it is slower and clumsy in city streets.

## FAQ

### Does the GTA 6 advanced tracker glitch remove the tracker?
No. It only stops the tracker calling the police while your partner drives. The tracker stays on the car (Polygon).

### Does it matter if I play as Jason or Lucia?
No. Polygon says either character can lead while the other drives the stolen car.

### How much does a high-end car job pay?
It depends on the car. VICE got $30,000 for one delivery with the perfect bonus. Game Rant lists $9,650 plus $5,000 for a perfect Ocelot Jugular.

### Is the advanced tracker glitch patched?
Not in reports up to 26 Nov. No newer patch as of 27 Nov. Not tested by us. The 23 Nov Launch Updates do not mention trackers, and Polygon, Game Rant and VICE describe it working after that update. Insider Gaming thinks it is probably a bug.

### Do I need mods or a PC?
No. It uses normal partner orders in story mode on PS5 or Xbox.
