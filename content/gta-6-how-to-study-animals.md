# GTA 6 How to Study Animals: Binoculars and the Logbook

> - **Quick answer:** equip the **binoculars** from the weapon wheel, zoom with **D-pad up**, then **hold R1** (Xbox: **RB**) to study the animal (PowerPyx).
> - **You only need 100 animals** for the 100% checklist (PowerPyx, Insider Gaming).
> - **Check progress:** **Progress, then Animals** (PowerPyx).

**Words used on this page.** *Study* means looking at an animal through binoculars until the game records it. *Binoculars* are a tool for looking far away. *Weapon wheel* is the circle menu with your weapons and tools. *100% completion* means finishing the game's big checklist. *Breed* is a type of dog or cat.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## How to study an animal

1. **Hold L1** to open the weapon wheel. Beebom says to pick the binoculars with the **right stick**, then let go of L1.
2. Look at the animal.
3. **Zoom in** with **D-pad up** (PowerPyx).
4. **Hold R1** (Xbox: **RB**) until it is studied (PowerPyx).

PowerPyx says to study every new animal you meet.

## How many do you need?

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| PowerPyx | Any **100 of 255** animals counts for the trophies and the 100% checklist. It says that is about 40%, a lenient requirement |
| Insider Gaming | 100 animals on the checklist, out of "over 160" it says are in the game |

The two outlets give different totals for how many animals there are. Both agree on **100** being the number you need.

PowerPyx says that unlike Red Dead Redemption 2, you do **not** have to hunt or skin animals. Studying is enough. It also says each breed of dog and cat counts separately.

## Where to see your logbook

1. Open the game menu.
2. Go to **Progress**.
3. Choose **Animals** (PowerPyx).

## Tips

- Start early and it comes naturally while you play (PowerPyx).
- Study every new breed of dog and cat you see, because they count one by one (PowerPyx).
