# GTA 6 How to Study Animals: Binoculars and the Logbook

> - **Quick answer:** you study an animal with the **binoculars** (GameSpot) or by choosing **Study** when you walk up to one. Studying adds it to your compendium (GamesRadar).
> - **You only need 100 animals** for the 100% checklist (Insider Gaming).
> - **Exact buttons and the menu path are unconfirmed.** Only PowerPyx and Beebom give them so far. See the box below.

**Words used on this page.** *Study* means looking at an animal until the game records it. *Binoculars* are a tool for looking far away. *Compendium* (or *logbook*) is the list of animals you have studied. *100% completion* means finishing the game's big checklist. *Breed* is a type of dog or cat. *Unconfirmed* means only small sites report it so far.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## What the big outlets say

- GameSpot says Rockstar told The Guardian that players can use binoculars to "study" birds and animals, like in Red Dead Redemption 2. It says there are 62 bird species and more than 170 animal types in total.
- GamesRadar says that when you walk up to an animal you get a list of choices. For a dog these were Praise, Scold and Pet, and there was also an option to **Study** the animal, which adds it to your compendium. GamesRadar says there are likely to be rewards for filling in the logbook. It says Rockstar has also confirmed Rare, Discoverable and Legendary animals, and has not given details.
- Insider Gaming says the 100% checklist only needs **100 animals**.

## Controls: early reports, not yet confirmed by a major outlet

PowerPyx and Beebom have launch-day steps. No major outlet has confirmed the buttons yet. Treat them as early reports and check in your own game.

1. **Hold L1** to open the weapon wheel. Beebom says to pick the binoculars with the **right stick**, then let go of L1.
2. Look at the animal.
3. **Zoom in** with **D-pad up** (PowerPyx).
4. **Hold R1** (Xbox: **RB**) until it is studied (PowerPyx).

PowerPyx says to study every new animal you meet.

## How many do you need?

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| Insider Gaming | 100 animals on the checklist, out of "over 160" it says are in the game |
| PowerPyx (small site) | Any **100 of 255** animals counts for the trophies and the 100% checklist. It says that is about 40% |
| GamesRadar, GameSpot | More than 170 animal species in total (not the same count as the checklist) |

The outlets give different totals for how many animals there are. They all agree on **100** being the number you need, where they say it (Insider Gaming, PowerPyx).

PowerPyx says that unlike Red Dead Redemption 2, you do **not** have to hunt or skin animals. Studying is enough. It also says each breed of dog and cat counts separately. Both are unconfirmed.

## Where to see your logbook

GamesRadar says studied animals go into your compendium. Where it sits in the menu is **unconfirmed**. PowerPyx says it is **Progress, then Animals**, and we have not seen a major outlet repeat it.

## FAQ

### How do I study animals in GTA 6?
Use the binoculars (GameSpot) or pick the Study option when you walk up to an animal (GamesRadar). The exact buttons are unconfirmed. PowerPyx says hold R1 or RB while zoomed in.

### How many animals do I need?
100 (Insider Gaming).

### Do I need to hunt them?
PowerPyx says no, studying is enough. No major outlet has confirmed this yet.
