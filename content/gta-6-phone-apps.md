# GTA 6 Phone Apps: What Each App Does (WAiNK, RydeMe)

> - **Quick answer:** your phone has apps for scanning cars (**WAiNK**), taxis (**RydeMe**), your money (**BuckMe**), calling your own car (**Scooter Bros**), a social feed (**Snapmatic**) and a fitness app. Major outlets name all of them.
> - **Open the phone:** press **up on the D-pad**, even when driving (GameSpot, VGC). See [how to scan cars in GTA 6](/cars/gta-6-how-to-scan-cars/).
> - **Reminders:** press up on the D-pad, then **Square** (PS5) or **X** (Xbox) (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *App* is a small program on your phone. *Scan* means pointing your phone at something so the game saves its details. *Fast travel* means getting to a far place quickly. *Register* means making a stolen car your own. *Confirmed* here means a major outlet or Rockstar says it. *Unconfirmed* means only small sites report it so far.

*This page has no story spoilers. Some apps unlock during the story.*

## Apps confirmed by major outlets

| App | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| WAiNK | Scans cars. Shows locks, alarms, trackers, value and what it costs to register | GameSpot |
| RydeMe | A taxi app used for fast travel. You can pay to skip the ride | VGC |
| BuckMe | Your BuckMe account. It has a shared savings account, and what you put in affects the story | GameSpot |
| Scooter Bros | Brings a car you have registered to you | GameSpot |
| Snapmatic | A social feed that returns from earlier games. You can browse it, but you cannot post on it, because Jason and Lucia do not want to leave a digital footprint | IGN, as reported by The Verge |
| Messages | Texts. GameSpot lists it among the default apps | GameSpot |
| Fitness app | Tracks workouts. Closing your fitness ring is part of GameSpot's gym advice | GameSpot |

GameSpot says both Jason and Lucia have their own phone, and that the default apps include Messages, Snapmatic and Scooter Bros.

## Shortcuts and tips

1. Press **up on the D-pad** to open the phone (GameSpot, VGC).
2. Press **up on the D-pad**, then **Square or X**, for your reminders list. GameSpot says it shows articles, posts, texts you can answer and people you should call. It says some lead to side missions.
3. Use RydeMe for fast travel. VGC says skipping a ride costs $100 on top of the fare.
4. Registered cars can be called with Scooter Bros (GameSpot).

## Early reports: not yet confirmed by a major outlet

- **Hold up on the D-pad to open WAiNK at once.** Reported by PowerPyx only.
- **On Demand,** an app for podcasts and shows. Reported by Beebom only. GameSpot says there is **no dedicated app for podcasts or radio**. It says you buy headphones, then hold **Left** on the D-pad to pick a station or podcast. So the two sources disagree and we do not list On Demand as an app.
- **More apps and websites.** Beebom lists many more and marks them as unconfirmed. We do not list them as real. When a major outlet shows one working in the game, we will add it.

## Why the phone matters

GameSpot says the phone reminders are worth checking when you do not know what to do next. VGC says RydeMe needs a bank account before it works, so it comes a little later in the story.

## FAQ

### What apps are on the GTA 6 phone?
Major outlets name WAiNK, RydeMe, BuckMe, Scooter Bros, Snapmatic, Messages and a fitness app. An "On Demand" app is an early report (Beebom) that GameSpot's headphones guide contradicts.

### Can I post photos on Snapmatic?
No. IGN says Jason and Lucia cannot post, as reported by The Verge.

### How do I open the phone?
Press up on the D-pad (GameSpot, VGC).
