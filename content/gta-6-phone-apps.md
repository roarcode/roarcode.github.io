# GTA 6 Phone Apps: What Each App Does (WAiNK, RydeMe)

> - **Quick answer:** your phone has apps for scanning cars (**WAiNK**), taxis (**RydeMe**), banking (**BuckMe**), calling your own car (**Scooter Bros**) and more.
> - **Open the phone:** press **up on the D-pad** (VGC). Hold it to open WAiNK straight away (PowerPyx). See [how to scan cars in GTA 6](/cars/gta-6-how-to-scan-cars/).
> - **Reminders:** press up on the D-pad, then **Square** (PS5) or **X** (Xbox) (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *App* is a small program on your phone. *Scan* means pointing your phone at something so the game saves its details. *Fast travel* means getting to a far place quickly. *Register* means making a stolen car your own. *Confirmed* means shown or described in the final game or by Rockstar.

*This page has no story spoilers. Some apps unlock during the story.*

## Apps that are confirmed

| App | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| WAiNK | Scans cars. Shows locks, alarms, trackers, value and what it costs to register | Beebom, GameSpot |
| RydeMe | A taxi app used for fast travel. You can pay to skip the ride | VGC, Beebom |
| BuckMe | The digital bank app. It manages money you deposit | Beebom |
| Scooter Bros | Brings a car you have registered to you | GameSpot, Beebom |
| Snapmatic | A social feed. You can browse and follow, but Jason and Lucia cannot post their own photos | Beebom |
| Fitness app | Tracks workouts and sleep | GameSpot, Beebom |
| On Demand | Podcasts and shows | Beebom |

Beebom says some of these were confirmed through Rockstar previews before launch. Others, like WAiNK and RydeMe, you can see working in the game guides from GameSpot and VGC.

## Shortcuts and tips

1. Press **up on the D-pad** to open the phone (VGC).
2. Press **up on the D-pad**, then **Square or X**, for your reminders list. GameSpot says it shows articles, posts, texts you can answer and people you should call. It says some lead to side missions.
3. PowerPyx says holding up on the D-pad opens WAiNK at once.
4. Use RydeMe for fast travel. VGC says skipping a ride costs $100 on top of the fare.
5. Registered cars can be called with Scooter Bros (GameSpot).
