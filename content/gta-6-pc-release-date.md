# GTA 6 PC Release Date: Is There a PC Version?

> - **Quick answer:** **No PC version or date is announced.** GTA 6 launches on **19 Nov 2026** on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** only.
> - **Rumour:** GamesBeat reported on 13 Oct 2026 a PC launch in **Q1 2027** (Jan to Mar). It uses four unnamed sources. Rockstar has not confirmed it.
> - **Do not buy hardware for it yet.** Wait for Rockstar or Take-Two to say something.

**Words used on this page.** *Announced* means the company itself said it. A *rumour* is a claim nobody official has confirmed. *Anonymous sources* are people the reporter does not name.

## What is official

- Rockstar's GTA VI page lists **PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S**. Just Jared checked this on 15 Nov and found no PC version, date, pre-order or system requirements.
- Take-Two's earnings release lists the game for "PS5, Xbox Series X|S" (Just Jared).
- Xbox One owners can **stream** it with Xbox Cloud Gaming. That is not a PC version. See [platforms](/launch-hub/platforms/).

## The rumour

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** GamesBeat says GTA VI comes to PC in Q1 2027, citing "four industry sources familiar with the studio's plans". Rockstar did not respond. The Verge and PC Gamer both repeated it with caution. Source: https://www.theverge.com/games/1010098/gta-vi-is-reportedly-launching-on-pc-in-q1-of-2027

- That would be much faster than GTA V, which reached PC more than a year and a half after consoles (The Verge).
- PC Gamer had predicted a PC version would not come before November 2027, and called the report a rumour that readers should not get their hopes up for.
- Other sites that repeat the story are still repeating the same one report.
