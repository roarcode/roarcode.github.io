# GTA 6 Health, Stamina and Focus: How to Level Them Up

> - **Quick answer:** Jason and Lucia have three core stats: **Health, Stamina and Focus** (GameSpot).
> - **Raise them:** lift weights for Health, run or bike for Stamina, and fish or play pool for Focus (GameSpot).
> - **Quick buffs:** eat, sleep and shower. These last until your session ends or you die (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Stat* is a number or bar that shows how good a character is at something. *Stamina* is how long you can run or work out before you get tired. *Focus* is how long you can slow time to aim. *Buff* is a short boost. *Session* means one stretch of play until you quit the game.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## What each stat does and what raises it

| Stat | What it does | What raises it | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Health | Your health bar size | Lifting weights | GameSpot |
| Stamina | Your stamina meter | Exercise such as running, biking or kayaking | GameSpot |
| Focus | How long you can slow time to line up shots | Calm activities such as fishing and playing pool | GameSpot |

GameSpot says health and stamina gains fade unless you keep doing the activities. A Focus gain is **permanent**.

## Quick buffs: eat, sleep, shower

GameSpot says you can also give each stat a short boost by eating, sleeping and showering. When something affects a stat you will see an icon: a pink heart for Health, blue lightning for Stamina and a purple eye for Focus.

These buffs last for your play session or until you die (GameSpot). GameSpot's example routine: sleep at night, work out in the morning, eat from the fridge, shower.

## How the gym works

GameSpot describes the strength workouts like this:

1. Use the two **trigger buttons**.
2. Press both in **quickly**.
3. Let go **slowly**.
4. Finish your set before your stamina runs out.

Slow or clumsy timing costs more stamina. GameSpot says it does not matter which exercise you pick, and some sets are shorter. Its team found deadlifts quick and kettlebell swings had a nice rhythm.

GameSpot adds that both characters have a fitness app on their phone with an exercise window. Stop when you have closed your fitness ring, then eat a protein bar or shake to lock in the gains. The window opens again roughly every in-game day. It also says Jason has weights outside his house.

For stamina, GameSpot suggests a gym membership so you can use a treadmill or exercise bike. Keep the cursor in the **pink area**. Its team said there is no gym close to the starting area.

## Focus Mode

GameSpot says to click **both thumbsticks** to turn on Focus Mode. With a gun in hand it slows time for a short while. Away from a fight you can keep it on. It highlights things: enemies in red, things you can use in yellow and cash in green. GameSpot says it can show enemies through walls, notes with safe codes and animal tracks.

## FAQ

### What are the three stats in GTA 6?
Health, Stamina and Focus (GameSpot).

### How do I turn on Focus Mode?
Click both thumbsticks (GameSpot).

### Do I have to work out?
GameSpot says skipping it will not ruin the game, but it puts you at a disadvantage.

### Does low stamina matter?
GameSpot's healing guide says low stamina slows how fast your health refills.
