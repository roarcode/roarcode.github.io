# GTA 6 Cheats: How to Enter Them on PS5 and Xbox (2026)

> - **Quick answer:** **GTA 6 does have cheats.** You open your in-game phone, hold the two shoulder buttons, then enter a button code (PC Gamer, 18 Nov 2026).
> - **They are locked at the start.** Each cheat opens later, with its own challenge. Details are in the hidden box below.
> - **Careful:** PC Gamer says that once you use a cheat, **you cannot save** from that point on.

**Words used on this page.** A *cheat* is a secret code that changes the game, like more money. *D-pad* is the plus-shaped button pad on the controller. *Shoulder buttons* are the buttons on top of the controller. *Trophies* (PlayStation) and *achievements* (Xbox) are prizes for playing well.

*Spoiler note: this page does not describe the story. One short box explains when cheats unlock, because that tells you how far the game goes.*

## How to enter a cheat (PC Gamer's steps)

You must have the cheat unlocked first.

1. Press **up on the D-pad** to open your phone.
2. **Hold L1** and press **R1**. (On Xbox the same two buttons are called **LB** and **RB**. This name swap is general Xbox naming, not a PC Gamer line.)
3. Enter the cheat's button code.

## Saving, trophies and achievements

- **Saving:** PC Gamer says that if you use a cheat, you cannot save your game from that point on. So you cannot use cheats to finish side missions or challenges. Quit without saving to keep your save clean.
- **Trophies and achievements:** PowerPyx's GTA 6 trophy roadmap (18 Nov 2026) says "Cheats disable trophies and saving the game". Our [GTA 6 trophy list](/collectibles/gta-6-trophy-list/) page says the same. Rockstar has not said it, so treat it as one trophy guide's report.
- **Getting trophies back:** Beebom (18 Nov 2026) says reloading a save without cheats brings trophies back. Beebom bases this on GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2, so it is **not confirmed for GTA 6**.

> **Rumour / unconfirmed:** reloading a save without cheats turns trophies and achievements back on, as in earlier Rockstar games. Source: https://beebom.com/do-cheats-disable-trophies-in-gta-6/

Beebom's page and PC Gamer disagree about saving (Beebom says you can save). PC Gamer opened the game and tested it. We follow PC Gamer until Rockstar says more.

## Do GTA 5 codes work?

There is no source that says they do. Do not trust lists that copy GTA 5 phone numbers or buttons.
