# GTA 6 Criminal Profile Explained: All 5 Levels

The GTA 6 criminal profile is the game's record of how violent you are, and it has **5 levels**, from **Professional** to **Irredeemable** ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/)). Kill less to stay near the top; kill a lot and you slide down.

> **Quick answer**
> - **5 levels:** Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho, Irredeemable (PC Gamer, GamesRadar).
> - **Check it:** hold **right on the d-pad**, or open the **Jason & Lucia** menu (GamesRadar).
> - **Careful:** you can move between the first 4, but **Irredeemable is permanent** (PC Gamer, GamesRadar).

**Words used on this page.** A *criminal profile* is the game's score for how you behave. *Non-lethal* means not killing. *Focus meter* is the bar that lets you slow down time (bullet time). *D-pad* is the cross-shaped set of 4 arrow buttons on the left of your controller.

*This page has minor spoilers about how the profile can affect the two main characters. They are hidden in a box below.*

## What the criminal profile is

Jason and Lucia each have **their own** criminal profile ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/)). It watches how you treat people and animals. PC Gamer says it is "almost entirely dependent on how often you kill people".

Rockstar's Rob Nelson says there is no right or wrong way to play. But he says "consistent actions may have an impact in terms of how the world reacts to you and how the narrative plays out" ([IGN India, from an Edge interview](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/276015/gta-6-rockstar-games-rob-nelson-reveals-more-details-about-the-criminal-profile-system)).

## How to check your criminal profile

GamesRadar gives two ways ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profiles/)):

1. **Quick look:** hold **right on the d-pad**.
2. Look at the small icon next to Jason's or Lucia's picture.
3. **Full view:** open the **Jason & Lucia** menu.

## All 5 levels and what each one gives you

PC Gamer lists the levels from best to worst. Each one has its own coloured icon, a face with horns ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/)).

| Level | Icon colour | How you get it | Bonus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Professional** | Yellow | Only use violence when you must. Knock enemies out instead of killing. Stay hidden. | Non-lethal takedowns and kind acts fill your focus meter. |
| **Hardened** | Orange | Kill enemies. Kill people only in self-defence. | Same as Professional. |
| **Violent** | Purple | Skip non-lethal options. Kill enemies, police and people at random. | Headshots fill your focus meter. |
| **Psycho** | Light grey | Kill lots of police, helpless people and pets or defenceless animals. Hunting does not count. | Headshots fill your focus meter, but you **can't surrender** to the police. |
| **Irredeemable** | Dark grey | Stay Psycho for too long. | None listed. It is **permanent**. |

## How to stay Professional

1. Aim at arms and legs, not the head. See [can you play GTA 6 without killing anyone](/beginner-tips/gta-6-non-lethal-playthrough/).
2. Knock enemies out instead of killing them.
3. Sneak past when you can. Being seen is fine on Hardened, not on Professional (PC Gamer).
4. Don't hurt pets or people who can't fight back.
5. Check your icon often with **right on the d-pad**.

## What a bad profile changes

GamesRadar says police **open fire on you much faster** if you are Psycho or Irredeemable ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profiles/)). People on the street also react differently.

:::spoiler What Irredeemable does to the two main characters
GamesRadar says an Irredeemable profile "will drive a wedge between Jason and Lucia", and that it may permanently affect their relationship ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profiles/)). PC Gamer quotes Rockstar: "being irredeemable will have a lasting impact on Jason and Lucia" ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-criminal-profile-honor-system-explained/)).
:::

## FAQ

### How many criminal profile levels are there in GTA 6?
Five: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable (PC Gamer, GamesRadar).

### Can you fix an Irredeemable profile?
No. PC Gamer and GamesRadar both say Irredeemable is permanent. You can move freely between the other four.

### Does hunting hurt my criminal profile?
PC Gamer says killing defenceless animals pushes you toward Psycho, but that **doesn't apply to hunting**.
