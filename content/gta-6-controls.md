# GTA 6 Controls on PS5 and Xbox: Every Default Button

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 controls put **run on a left-stick click** (L3 on PS5, LS on Xbox), not on Cross or A.
> - **Driving:** **R2/RT** to go, **L2/LT** to brake, **Cross/A** for the handbrake, **Triangle/Y** to get out.
> - **Want the old style?** Pick the **Classic** layout. There is also a **Left-Handed** layout.

**Words used on this page.** *Controls* are the buttons you press to move and act. *Layout* is which button does which job. *L3* and *R3* mean pushing the left or right stick down like a button until it clicks (on Xbox these are *LS* and *RS*). *Focus* is a special slow-motion view. *Haptics* means small vibrations you feel in the controller.

**Where the buttons come from.** The PS5 buttons are from the Shacknews controls guide (18 Nov 2026), written from the finished game. The Xbox column shows the button in the **same spot** on an Xbox controller. We have not seen an Xbox controls screen yet, so treat that column as a match by position.

## Driving buttons

1. Hold **R2** (Xbox: **RT**) to drive forward.
2. Hold **L2** (Xbox: **LT**) to brake. Keep holding it to go backwards.
3. Push the **left stick** left or right to steer.
4. Press **Cross** (Xbox: **A**) for the handbrake. This makes the back wheels slide in tight turns.
5. Press **Triangle** (Xbox: **Y**) to get out.

**Driving feels hard?** Many new players are asking the same thing on Reddit. Two buttons help most: the **handbrake** (Cross/A) for sharp corners, and **Focus**, which slows time while you drive (see below).

## On-foot buttons

1. Push the **left stick** to walk.
2. Click **L3** (Xbox: **LS**) to run.
3. Press **Cross** (Xbox: **A**) to jump or climb.
4. Press **Circle** (Xbox: **B**) to crouch. Hold it to rest.
5. Press **R1** (Xbox: **RB**) to hide behind cover.

## Fighting and aiming

- **Aiming:** Hold **R1** (RB) to throw quickly. Press **Triangle** (Y) to swap weapons fast. Hold **D-pad left** to look over your other shoulder.
- **Fist fights:** **Square** (X) punches or kicks, **Cross** (A) blocks or dodges, **R2** (RT) grabs, and holding **L2** (LT) locks on.

## Focus: click both sticks

1. Click **L3 and R3 at the same time** (Xbox: **LS and RS**).
2. Time slows down and targets are easier to see.

Shacknews says Focus helps in gunfights, slows time while driving, spots fish and animals, and shows targets through walls. You can use it for some things right away. Focus for gunfights unlocks a little way into the story. How long it lasts grows as you level up Focus. Food and drinks with the Focus icon also help.

## How to switch to the Classic layout

1. Press the **Options** button (Xbox: **Menu**) to pause.
2. Go to **Settings**, then **Controls**.
3. Pick **Classic** for the older GTA button style, or **Left-Handed** to swap sides.

The three layout names are from Shacknews. The exact menu path is our best guess and will be checked in game.

## Controller vibration on PS5

On PS5 and PS5 Pro, the DualSense has detailed vibration (*haptic feedback*). You can change it in the accessibility settings (Rockstar, via Game Informer).

## FAQ

### What is the run button in GTA 6?
Click the left stick: **L3** on PS5, **LS** on Xbox. Pick the Classic layout if you want the older style.

### What is the handbrake in GTA 6?
**Cross** on PS5, **A** on Xbox.

### How do I use Focus in GTA 6?
Click **both sticks** at the same time.

### Can I change the controls?
Yes. You can pick Default, Classic or Left-Handed. Full button remapping is not confirmed yet.
