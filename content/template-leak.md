## How to check a leak yourself

1. Find the **first public post**. Do not trust a copy of a copy.
2. Look at **who** posted it. Do they have a good record?
3. Look for **two reputable outlets**. One post is not enough.
4. Check the **date**. Old claims get recycled.
5. Wait for **Rockstar**. It is the only final word.
