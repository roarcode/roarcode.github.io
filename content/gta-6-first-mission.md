# GTA 6 Gone Fishing Walkthrough: First Jason Mission

> - **Quick answer:** **Gone Fishing** is the first mission where you play as **Jason**. It is the second mission if you count the Prologue (VGC, PC Gamer).
> - **Best tip:** go **stealth**. You only have one weapon at the start (VGC). Crouch with **Circle** and take enemies down quietly.
> - **Focus Mode** (click **L3 and R3**) highlights what matters, even outside missions (VGC).

> **Spoiler warning:** this page has minor spoilers. The mission steps are inside the boxes below. Open them only if you want to know.

**Words used on this page.** *Mission* is a task with a goal. *Stealth* means sneaking so enemies do not see you. *Focus Mode* is a view that highlights enemies and items. *Takedown* means knocking an enemy out quietly. *Checkpoint* means a place where the game remembers your progress.

## Tips that do not spoil anything

- **Focus Mode:** click **L3 and R3** (VGC). It highlights enemies, special items and more (VGC). Beebom also says to use it to find your next spot.
- **Be quiet:** VGC says to crouch with **Circle**, grab an enemy with **R2**, then press **Square** to take them down. VGC says to aim for knockouts, not loud gunshots.
- **Only one weapon at the start**, so a full gun fight is hard (VGC).
- **Heal:** VGC says you use health items with **L1 and Square**.

PS5 buttons are given in the sources. Xbox buttons are **not stated** in the sources we checked.

## Step by step

:::spoiler Part 1: the boat (reveals the start of the mission)
VGC says the mission starts with Jason and Cal on a fishing boat, waiting for a call. You follow Donnie's plane. He drops packages in the water. You steer the boat to them so Cal can fish them out. Slow down near each package or Cal cannot grab it (VGC). Beebom says there are three packages.
:::

:::spoiler Part 2: the island camp (reveals a location and a gang)
VGC says Donnie then heads to a meeting with the Kingfishers, a gang in GTA 6. You watch through binoculars. Beebom says to hold **L1**, pick binoculars with the right stick, then hold **L2** to look. When things go wrong, Jason parks the boat near the camp and goes in to find Donnie.

VGC says the camp is full of enemies, the high grass on the right is good for sneaking, and the front door of the building where Donnie is held is unlocked. VGC adds that there is a hammer behind that building if you want an extra melee weapon.
:::

:::spoiler Part 3: freeing Donnie (reveals a fight and a health item)
VGC says that after you untie Donnie, a large Kingfisher fights Jason. Use **Square** to hit and **X** to dodge. After you win, pick up the **Zombix** health item. VGC says you will see a lot of it.

VGC says you are then asked if you want to take cash from a nearby trailer on the way out. It is marked on the map. If you take it, an enemy jumps out and you need to fight. The cash is on a table. You do not have to beat every Kingfisher. You only have to reach the dock.
:::

:::spoiler Part 4: the plane and home (reveals the end of the mission)
VGC says you fly Donnie's plane over the Keys for a while, then land at Brian's marina and meet Brian in a cutscene. After that you drive Cal to his house, then drive to Jason's house and go in. VGC says you get a call there, which ends the playable part. Beebom says this opens up Jason's home in Key Lento and teaches the basic controls.

Beebom adds a plane tip: if the plane is hit too much, slow down with **L2** and land on the water, then taxi to the nearby dock.
:::

## FAQ

### What is the first mission in GTA 6?
The Prologue, where you play as Lucia. The first mission as Jason is Gone Fishing (VGC).

### Do I have to sneak?
No. VGC says you can go in with guns blazing and still finish, but it is much harder with only one weapon.

### How long is it?
IGN's gameplay video of this mission runs about 15 minutes.
