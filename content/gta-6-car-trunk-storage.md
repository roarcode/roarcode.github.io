# GTA 6 Car Trunk Storage: Swap Guns and Outfits Fast

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 car trunk storage lives in your **Personal Vehicle**. Open its trunk with **Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox)** (GamesRadar+).
> - **Hold L1 / LB** to open your loadout and swap guns and items with what is in the trunk (GamesRadar+).
> - You **cannot** upgrade what you carry, so the trunk is your extra space (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Trunk* is the storage box at the back of a car. *Personal Vehicle* is a car that belongs to you. *Loadout* is the list of weapons and items you have on you right now. *Sidearm* is a small gun, like a pistol. *Longarm* is a big gun, like a rifle or shotgun. *Stow* means put away.

## How to use the trunk, step by step

1. Walk to the back of your **Personal Vehicle**. GamesRadar+ says it must be your own car.
2. Press **Triangle (PS5) / Y (Xbox)** to open the trunk.
3. When the prompt shows, hold **L1 (PS5) / LB (Xbox)** to open your loadout.
4. Press **R1 / RB** to switch between the **Weapons** tab and the **Items** tab.
5. Move the **right stick** to pick a slot.
6. Press **left or right on the D-pad** to cycle through what you have stored for that slot.
7. Press **X (PS5) / A (Xbox)** to equip it.

## What the loadout holds

GamesRadar+ lists these slots:

| Tab | Slots |
|---|---|
| Weapons | 2 sidearms, 1 for your hands, 1 for equipment, 2 longarms, 1 melee weapon, 1 explosives |
| Items | Masks, Headgear, Headwear, Outfits, Bags, Gear, Eyewear, Smokes (one slot each) |

GameSpot says there is **no way to increase** what Jason and Lucia carry on their person: no bigger backpack and no extra slots. Anything extra stays in your car's trunk.

## Put a big gun away

1. Open the trunk with **Triangle / Y**.
2. Press **Square (PS5) / X (Xbox)** to stow your longarm (GamesRadar+).

## Move guns into the trunk from Ammu-Nation

1. Open the **GTA 6 Index** and go to the **Weapons** tab to find your closest Ammu-Nation (GamesRadar+).
2. Go inside and use the **weapons locker**.
3. Move the guns you own into your Personal Vehicle's trunk (GamesRadar+).

## Outfit will not change from the trunk?

One player on [r/GTA6 on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/GTA6/comments/1y05g89/cant_change_outfit_from_trunk/) says they could not change outfit from the trunk. That is a single report, not a confirmed bug. Check that you are at your own Personal Vehicle, and that you are on the **Items** tab (R1 / RB) where GamesRadar+ lists the **Outfits** slot.

## FAQ

### Can I carry more weapons in GTA 6?
Not on your person. GameSpot says there is no inventory upgrade, so store extra gear in your car's trunk.

### Does any car's trunk work?
GamesRadar+ says to use the trunk of your **Personal Vehicle**.

### How do I put a rifle in the trunk?
Open the trunk and press Square (PS5) / X (Xbox) (GamesRadar+).
