# GTA 6 Mission List: All 91 Missions by Chapter (Spoilers)

> - **Quick answer:** GTA 6 has **five chapters and 91 story missions** (PC Gamer).
> - **Counts by chapter:** 22, 11, 26, 25 and 7 (PC Gamer's list).
> - **Titles can spoil things**, so every chapter is in a closed box. PC Gamer hides three titles itself.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** mission titles can hint at the plot. Tap a chapter box only if you want to see its titles.

**Words used on this page.** *Story mission* is a mission that moves the main story forward. *Chapter* is a big part of the story, like a chapter in a book. *Checklist* is a list you tick off. *Redacted* means hidden on purpose.
