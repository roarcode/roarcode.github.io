# How to Repair Your Car in GTA 6 (and Why It Won't Fix)

> - **Quick answer:** to repair your car in GTA 6, drive into a **Pay 'n' Spray** or a **Mod Shop**. Repair is the first option, for a fee (GamesRadar+).
> - Press **down on the D-pad** while driving to see how hurt your engine is (GamesRadar+).
> - Gas stations fix **small damage only**, and cost more (GamesRadar+).

**Words used on this page.** *Engine* is the part that makes the car go. *Pay 'n' Spray* is a garage that fixes and repaints cars. *Mod Shop* is a garage that fixes and changes how cars look and drive. *GTA 6 Index* is a list in your menu of places you have found. *Tracker* is a device that tells the police where a stolen car is.

*One tip names an early mission character. It is in a closed box.*

## Step 1: Check the damage

1. Get in the car.
2. Press **down on the D-pad** (PS5 and Xbox).
3. Look at the **engine icon** above the minimap (GamesRadar+).

| Icon | What it means (GamesRadar+) |
|---|---|
| Green icon and circle | Engine is fine |
| Orange icon and circle | Moderate damage |
| Red icon and circle | Critical damage |
| Red icon and cross | Engine destroyed |

GamesRadar+ says a badly hurt engine first slows down, then makes the car swerve, and can then catch fire.

## Step 2: Find a garage

1. Open the **GTA 6 Index**.
2. Go to the **Vehicles** tab.
3. Pick **Pay 'n' Spray** or **Mod Shops**. GamesRadar+ says the ones you have found are listed by shortest drive.

![Our diagram: Find a garage. Open the GTA 6 Index. Vehicles tab. Pay 'n' Spray or Mod Shops. Not a game screenshot.](TODO: image:gta-6-how-to-repair-car-step-2 — diagram, our own art: menu path Index, Vehicles, Pay 'n' Spray)

## Step 3: Drive in and repair

1. Drive the car into the garage lot.
2. Choose **Repair**. GamesRadar+ says it is the first option.
3. Pay the fee.

A **gas station** can do small repairs, but GamesRadar+ says it will not fix critical damage and costs much more than a garage.

## Garage will not fix my car?

- **A stolen car with a red tracker.** GameSpot says a Pay 'n' Spray will not take a car with an **advanced (red) tracker**. Swap to another car. See [how to steal cars in GTA 6](/cars/gta-6-how-to-steal-cars/) for tracker icons.

## Free repairs

:::spoiler How to get free repairs early (names an early mission character)
Game Rant says an early mission sends Jason to **Joey's** mod shop, **Coastal Auto Care** in Key Lento. It says the first repair there is free. After the first body mods, Joey gives Jason his number. Game Rant says you can then press **up on the D-pad**, open **Contacts** and call Joey, and he repairs your car for free and leaves it parked next to you.
:::

## FAQ

### How do I repair my car in GTA 6?
Drive into a Pay 'n' Spray or Mod Shop and pick Repair (GamesRadar+).

### How do I see engine damage?
Press down on the D-pad while driving and check the engine icon above the minimap (GamesRadar+).

### Can gas stations fix my car?
Only small damage, and it costs more (GamesRadar+).
