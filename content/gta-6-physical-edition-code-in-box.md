# GTA 6 Code-in-Box: How to Redeem and Fix Code Problems

> - **Quick answer:** The GTA 6 physical edition **has no disc**. The box holds a **download code** only. On PS5, redeem it from your avatar menu > **Redeem Code** (PlayStation Support).
> - **Missing or unreadable code?** PlayStation says to **contact the retailer first**. Rockstar says the same for purchases outside the Rockstar Store.
> - **Open the box early.** Players report some boxes had bad or used codes. Rockstar has not confirmed a wider problem.

**Words used on this page.** *Code-in-box* is a box with a card inside that has a secret code. *Redeem* means typing the code to get the game on your account. *Retailer* is the shop you bought it from. *Region* is the country your account is set to.

## Redeem your code on PS5

These are PlayStation's own steps.

1. Sign in on your PS5 or on the PlayStation Store website.
2. Select your **avatar** at the top of the screen.
3. Select **Redeem Code** from the drop-down menu.
4. Type in your code.
5. Download the game. Pre-load is already open. See [GTA 6 pre-load](/launch-hub/preload-size/).

## Check your order first

If you ordered online, check that the order went through. PlayStation's steps:

1. Sign in and select your **avatar**.
2. Select **Order History**.
3. Check that your GTA VI order shows as successful.

## If the code is missing, unreadable or already used

1. **Look at the box and the card inside.** Take a clear photo of the code or the damage. You may need it as proof.
2. **Contact the shop you bought it from.** PlayStation: "If your code is missing or unreadable, please contact the retailer."
3. **Bought it on the Rockstar Store?** Rockstar's support article says to contact **Xsolla** at [help.xsolla.com](https://help.xsolla.com/). To cancel a physical pre-order there, see [Xsolla: cancel pre-order](https://help.xsolla.com/en/pre-orders/cancel-pre-order).
4. **Bought somewhere else?** Rockstar says purchase, refund and delivery questions go to that retailer.
5. **Code does not work on your account?** PlayStation Support lists "code issues" as something it can help with.

## In the news: an EU politician asks about boxes with no disc

On 18 Nov 2026, Maltese MEP **Alex Agius Saliba** wrote to the European Commission about games sold in a box with only a download code inside, with GTA VI as the example ([Times of Malta](https://timesofmalta.com/article/grand-theft-agius-saliba-says-discless-video-games-threaten.1137305)). An *MEP* is a member of the European Parliament.

- **What he asks for:** clear labels that say if a box has a disc or only a code, and the right to buy, sell, lend and keep playing games you paid for ([Times of Malta](https://timesofmalta.com/article/grand-theft-agius-saliba-says-discless-video-games-threaten.1137305)).
- **Why:** he says a digital purchase is only "a revocable license", so you can lose a game if servers shut down ([Times of Malta](https://timesofmalta.com/article/grand-theft-agius-saliba-says-discless-video-games-threaten.1137305)).
- **What it means for you today:** nothing changes yet. It is a letter asking for an investigation, not a new law ([MaltaToday](https://www.maltatoday.com.mt/news/europe/145804/agius_saliba_questions_whether_gamers_truly_own_the_games_they_purchase_ahead_of_gta_vi_release)). Your code still works the same way.

## FAQ

### Does the GTA 6 physical edition have a disc?
No. PlayStation says the box has a download code only and a disc is not included.

### My GTA 6 code is missing or unreadable. What now?
Contact the shop where you bought it. That is PlayStation's advice.

### Where do I redeem a GTA 6 code on PS5?
Select your avatar, then Redeem Code, then type the code.

### Who do I ask about a Rockstar Store order?
Xsolla, according to Rockstar's support article.
