# GTA 6 Best Things to Spend Money On (and What to Skip)

> - **Quick answer:** GameSpot's top buys are **weapon mods**, **garages**, the **BuckMe savings account** and **registering a car you love**.
> - **Skip:** lots of clothes and lots of guns early on (GameSpot).
> - **Best first buy:** a **suppressor** on a gun you already have beats buying three new guns (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Weapon mod* is an add-on for a gun, like a scope. *Suppressor* (or silencer) makes a gun quieter. *Optics* means a sight or scope. *Garage* is a place to store cars and gear. *Register a car* means making a stolen car your own. *BuckMe* is the bank in the game.

*Some shops and characters are named later in the story, so those bits are in closed boxes.*

## Best things to buy (GameSpot)

| Buy | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Weapon mods | Often matter more than the gun itself. A suppressor on an early assault rifle helps more than three different rifles | GameSpot |
| Garages | Store any car, add vehicle slots, let you manage weapons and loadout, boost Focus and drop off goods to sell | GameSpot |
| BuckMe savings account | Money put in has an effect on the story. Later goals give better interest and a stronger bond for the couple | GameSpot |
| Registering a car | Pay to make a stolen car your own. Can cost tens of thousands of dollars for a luxury car | GameSpot |

GameSpot's mods list is suppressors, magazines, foregrips, barrels and optics for first person.

GameSpot's tips page agrees. It says the two most important attachments are **sights** and **silencers**. A gun with a sight can snap into first person if you click the right stick. Iron sights cannot do that. The tips page says a silencer helps because police come when someone reports gunshots.

:::spoiler Which character helps with garages and registering cars? (names a Kelly County character)
GameSpot says that after you reach the Kelly County part of the story and meet **Moses**, you can pay a fee to register a vehicle as your own. At first Moses gives a discount. GameSpot says garages can be bought after speaking with Moses, and are listed on the Vice City Real Estate app.
:::

## What not to buy

GameSpot says the number one thing not to overspend on is **clothes**. Jason and Lucia get free clothes from many sources. Rich areas cost more. GameSpot says Ultimate Edition owners can also redeem free clothes.

GameSpot says buying too many guns is not wise early either, because you can keep free guns found on defeated enemies. It says there are also dozens of free legendary weapons from side content and story missions.

## Simple order to follow

1. Rob and earn first. See our [how to make money fast](/money/gta-6-how-to-make-money-fast/) page.
2. Buy a suppressor and a sight for a gun you already use (GameSpot).
3. Buy a garage or two when you can (GameSpot).
4. Put some money into the BuckMe savings account once it is set up. Keep enough for shopping (our own advice).
5. Register only a car you really want to keep (GameSpot).

## FAQ

### What should I buy first in GTA 6?
Weapon mods, especially a suppressor (GameSpot).

### Is the BuckMe savings account worth it?
GameSpot says it matters for the story and for better interest later.

### Should I buy lots of clothes?
GameSpot says no. You get many free outfits.
