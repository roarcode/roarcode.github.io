# Is GTA 6 Out? Yes: Unlock Time in Every Time Zone

> - **Quick answer:** Is GTA 6 out? **Yes, once it is 12:00 AM (midnight) on Thursday 19 November 2026 where you live.** New Zealand unlocked first, at 12:00 CET on Wed 18 Nov 2026.
> - **Slovenia and central Europe (CET):** 00:00 on Thu 19 Nov. **UK:** 00:00 GMT (01:00 CET). **US East Coast:** 12:00 AM ET (06:00 CET).
> - **Pre-loading does not unlock it early.** Your console shows a countdown. If it still says locked, wait for the countdown to end.

**Words used on this page.** *Unlock* means the game stops being locked and you can press play. *Pre-load* means you downloaded the game early. *CET* is central European time, the time in Slovenia, Germany and Italy.

Rockstar and PlayStation both say GTA 6 goes live at **midnight local time on 19 November 2026**. That is a rolling launch: each time zone unlocks at its own midnight, one after another.

## How to check on your console in 30 seconds

1. **PS5:** go to the home screen and open **Game Library**.
2. Select **Grand Theft Auto VI**.
3. If it is locked, you see a countdown. If the countdown is gone, press play.
4. **Xbox Series X|S:** press the **Xbox button**, then open **My games & apps**.
5. Select **Full library**, then select **Grand Theft Auto VI** and check the release time.

## FAQ

### Is GTA 6 out right now?
It is out in every time zone where midnight on 19 November 2026 has passed. New Zealand was first, at 12:00 CET on 18 November. Check the table above.

### Is GTA 6 out in Slovenia?
It unlocks at 00:00 CET on Thursday 19 November 2026. The PlayStation Store lists Slovenia at midnight local time (gta6everything.com, checked 20 Oct). Check your console's countdown to be sure.

### Is GTA 6 out on PC?
No. No PC version is announced. It is for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One can stream it through Xbox Cloud Gaming.

### Is GTA 6 out on Xbox One?
Only by cloud streaming, from 1:00 AM ET on 19 November (07:00 CET), at Xbox Series S graphics settings (Xbox Wire).

### Does pre-loading let me play early?
No. It only means the download is done when your time zone unlocks.
