# GTA 6 How to Scan Cars: WAiNK App in 3 Steps (PS5, Xbox)

> - **Quick answer:** to scan cars in GTA 6, **hold up on the D-pad** to open the WAiNK app, look at a car, then **hold R1** (Xbox: **RB**) (PowerPyx).
> - **What you learn:** the tools you need to steal it, what it is worth and what it costs to register it as your own (GamesRadar).
> - **Best tip:** stand next to a busy road and scan every car that drives past. You do not need to drive them (PowerPyx).

**Words used on this page.** *Scan* means pointing your phone at something so the game saves its details. *WAiNK* is the phone app in GTA 6 that scans cars. *D-pad* is the plus-shaped button pad on the controller. *Tracker* is a device in some cars that tells the police where the car is. *Register* means making a car your own personal car. *100% completion* means finishing every task on the game's big checklist.

*This page has no story spoilers. One small box says when the app unlocks.*

## How to scan a car

1. Stand near a car, or let one drive past you.
2. **Hold up on the D-pad.** This opens the WAiNK app straight away. It is faster than tapping the app on your phone (PowerPyx).
3. Point the camera at the car.
4. **Hold R1** on PS5, or **RB** on Xbox, until the scan finishes (PowerPyx).

A player on X shared the same steps: "Open WAiNK, aim, hold R1" ([@JonsUnlocked](https://x.com/JonsUnlocked/status/2123030229857792341)). We have not tested this ourselves.

## Scanning for 100% completion

- PowerPyx says the 100% checklist needs **200 of the 277** base model cars to be scanned.
- **Boats and aircraft** count too (PowerPyx).
- Different places have different cars. PowerPyx says slow pickup trucks show up more in the countryside, and supercars more in Vice City.
- Start early. You will get most of them just by playing.

## Where to see the cars you scanned

1. Open the game menu. On PS5, PowerPyx says press the **touchpad**.
2. Go to **Progress**.
3. Choose **Vehicles**.

Xbox button for this menu: **not announced** in the sources we checked.

## FAQ

### How do you scan cars in GTA 6?
Hold up on the D-pad to open WAiNK, look at the car, and hold R1 (RB on Xbox).

### What is the WAiNK app in GTA 6?
A phone app that scans cars. It shows the tool you need, the car's value and the cost to register it.

### Do I have to drive a car to scan it?
No. PowerPyx says scanning is enough.

### How many cars do I need to scan for 100%?
PowerPyx says 200 of the 277 base model cars.

### Where can I see the cars I scanned?
In the game menu under Progress > Vehicles.
