# Can You Play GTA 6 Without Killing Anyone? How It Works

> - **Quick answer:** in theory yes, GameSpot says you can play from start to finish without killing anyone. It is "just incredibly difficult".
> - **How:** aim at **joints and limbs** to injure. Head and heart shots kill (GameSpot).
> - **Why bother:** the game keeps a **criminal profile**. GameSpot says a violent Lucia acts more violently when you are not controlling her. Games.gg says it also changes how NPCs and police react.

More on the levels: [GTA 6 criminal profile explained](/beginner-tips/gta-6-criminal-profile/).

**Words used on this page.** *Non-lethal* means not killing. *Hit marker* is the little mark that shows you hit someone. *Criminal profile* is the game's record of how violent you are. *Joint* is a body part that bends, like a knee or elbow. *Pursuing* means chasing.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## How hit markers tell you what happened

GameSpot says that in GTA 6, shooting the **head or heart** kills an enemy, while shooting **joints and limbs** injures them. A **red** hit marker means your target is dead. A **yellow** one means they are injured and out of the fight for the rest of the shootout.

## Vehicles and helicopters

GameSpot says disabling a vehicle is a lot harder than shooting the driver. But if you shoot out a tyre or two, the chasing vehicle will spin out and leave you alone. GameSpot says **helicopters** retreat if you fire enough shots in their general direction, and the pilot says so on the police radio.

## Why does it matter?

GameSpot says it is tied to Jason and Lucia's **criminal profile**. If you play a violent Lucia, she acts more violently when you are not controlling her. GameSpot's team tried the non-lethal route and still killed a lot of people, but its profile rarely dropped below **professional**, mostly because it rarely killed outside missions.

Games.gg says the Criminal Profile System tracks how professionally or unnecessarily violently you commit crimes. It says this affects how hostile NPCs are, how severe the police are, story events and world reactions.

PC Gamer says the criminal profile is effectively the game's version of Red Dead Redemption 2's karma system.

## Tips for a gentle run

1. Use a silencer so gunshots do not bring the police (GameSpot).
2. Aim at arms and legs, not the head (GameSpot).
3. Shoot tyres to stop cars (GameSpot).
4. Keep your violence to what you must do in missions (GameSpot's experience).
5. Games.gg says to avoid starting trouble just because you have a weapon out.

## FAQ

### Can you finish GTA 6 without killing?
GameSpot says it is possible but very hard.

### What does a yellow hit marker mean?
The target is injured and out of the shootout (GameSpot).

### What is the criminal profile?
The game's record of how violent you are. It changes how NPCs, police and your partner react (GameSpot, Games.gg).
