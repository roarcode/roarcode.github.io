# GTA 6 Fences and Stash Bags: How to Sell Loot Safely

> - **Quick answer:** loot goes into **stash bags**. You sell it to a **fence**, someone who buys stolen goods (GameSpot).
> - **Each character can carry two bags.** If you die with bags on you, you lose them and your cash (GameSpot).
> - **Safest habit:** visit a fence after every big robbery (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Loot* is stolen goods. *Stash bag* is the bag the game puts your loot in. *Fence* is a person who buys stolen things. *Narcotics* means illegal drugs. *Marked bills* are bank notes the police can trace. *Trunk* is the boot of a car.

*Names of some fences are in a closed box because you meet them later in the story.*

## The three kinds of loot

GameSpot says valuables from robberies fit into three groups:

| Group | Notes | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Narcotics | Some fences pay more for these than others | GameSpot |
| Jewelry | Most fences buy it | GameSpot |
| Marked bills | Must be sold to a fence before you can use them | GameSpot |

GameSpot says most fences buy all three but pay different prices depending on what they specialise in.

Game8 says robberies reward you heavily with loot you can sell for high money at drop-offs, and that robberies also give you valuables such as unique weapons that come already upgraded.

## How to sell loot

1. Do a robbery. Your loot goes into a stash bag on its own (GameSpot).
2. Get away from the police first.
3. Take your bags to a fence. The first time you work with a fence, you must hand-deliver the goods (GameSpot).
4. If you keep working with a fence, they eventually offer pick-ups for a fee, and some give a bigger cut for specific items (GameSpot).

:::spoiler Who are the fences? (reveals names of early characters)
GameSpot gives two examples. **Yasmani** in Little Cuba pays a high price for jewelry and marked bills. **Tyrone** pays a high price for narcotics. GameSpot says you meet Yasmani in Chapter 3.
:::

## Stash bags: the rules

- Jason and Lucia can **each carry two bags** (GameSpot).
- Several bags can sit in a vehicle trunk. GameSpot says vans and trucks can hold up to eight, and motorcycles cannot hold any.
- GameSpot says you should expect to fill a bag or two per robbery, unless you only rob small convenience stores.
- If you rob as a pair, you can **pass a bag**: press **right on the D-pad**, then **Circle** (PS5) or **B** (Xbox) (GameSpot).

## Do not lose your loot

GameSpot says that if you die while holding stash bags, you lose the bags and the cash in your pocket. If you stash bags somewhere safe they might still be there, but it is not sure. Storing them in your car is another option, but if the police impound the car you lose them.

GameSpot's tip: split up after a job. If one character is caught, the other keeps their bags. The character you are not playing can sell loot to a fence for you. The money takes a while to reach your account, but it saves a trip.

GameSpot's robbery guide says the same about splitting up, and adds that the police then have two moving targets.

## Where to keep cash

VGC says that if you die or get arrested, you lose the cash you are carrying. Early on, VGC says there is a spot in Jason's bedroom, under some loose floorboards, where you can stash cash until you get a bank account. VGC says to use it as often as you can.

## FAQ

### What is a fence in GTA 6?
Someone who buys stolen goods (GameSpot).

### How many stash bags can I carry?
Two per character (GameSpot).

### What happens to my bags if I die?
You lose them with your cash on hand (GameSpot).

### What buttons pass a bag to my partner?
Right on the D-pad, then Circle on PS5 or B on Xbox (GameSpot).
