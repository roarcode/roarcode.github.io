# GTA 6 Crab Lab Codes: Safe, Computer and Dock (Pelican Valley)

> - **Quick answer:** the GTA 6 crab lab is a research outpost in the **southwest of the Pelican Valley swamps**, just north of the Keys ([The Big Lead](https://www.thebiglead.com/gta-6-pelican-valley-crab-research-outpost-computer-password-keypad-codes/)).
> - **You need three things:** a computer password, a safe code and a dock door code. All three are in the closed box below.
> - **The prize:** just over $1,000 in cash and a special SMG (submachine gun) you can use early.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** this is a side secret, not a story twist. The codes and the prize name are inside closed boxes, so you can still solve it yourself.

**Words used on this page.** *Outpost* is a small base far from town. *Keypad* is a box of number buttons on a door or safe. *SMG* means submachine gun, a small gun that fires fast. *Long gun* is a big gun, like a rifle or SMG, that you carry in a limited slot. *Boot* is the trunk of a car. *Focus mode* is a view that makes clues glow.

## What is the crab lab?

The Big Lead's Kirk McKeand says it is a research base with "bizarre crab experiments", a locked computer and keypads that need codes. He calls it "one of the weirdest early game surprises in GTA 6". You can reach it early in the game, so it is a good way to get a strong gun before the big shootouts.

## How to solve it, step by step

Want to try it yourself first? Turn on **Focus mode** (press **L3 and R3** together on PS5) to make clues stand out ([PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-safe-codes-combinations/) gives the PS5 buttons). The lab has a main yard, a house in the middle, an outer building full of crabs, and a locked building at the end of a dock.

:::spoiler Step 1: turn on the power
1. Walk into the main yard.
2. Go around the **side of the house in the middle**.
3. Flip the **power switch** on the outside wall.

(The Big Lead)
:::

:::spoiler Step 2: the computer password
1. Go inside the main house.
2. Find the **side room with writing on the floor**.
3. Look at the writing. It gives the password.
4. Type **CLEAVES** into the computer.

The computer gives you a hint for the safe: **"double success"** (The Big Lead).
:::

![Our diagram: The three locks. Computer: Password on the floor. Safe: Use the hint. Dock door: Code is in the safe. Not a game screenshot.](TODO: image:gta-6-crab-lab-codes-step-2 — diagram, our own art: The three locks, see the spec)
*Caption: Diagram. Each lock gives you the clue for the next one.*

:::spoiler Step 3: the safe code
1. Go to the **outer building with the dead crabs**.
2. Read the **researcher's note**. One experiment is marked as the success.
3. Its specimen number is **0036**.
4. "Double success" means double it: 36 + 36 = 72.
5. Type **0072** into the safe.

Inside: just over **$1,000** cash and the dock code (The Big Lead).
:::

:::spoiler Step 4: the dock building and the prize
1. Walk to the **building at the end of the dock**.
2. Type **0212** into the keypad.
3. Go in and take the **Crabshack VF5 SMG**.

The Big Lead says it is a long gun, so you have to **store it in your car's boot**, and calls it "a pretty decent early game choice" for big shootouts.
:::

## Is there more money? (reports)

Some players say there is more. A post from [@viceovertime on X](https://x.com/viceovertime/status/2123329309733700036), sharing a clip from TikTok creator @mislead, says the lab also has a **duffel bag with $10K** in it. The Big Lead does not mention this bag, so treat it as a **report** for now.

## FAQ

In the southwest of the Pelican Valley swamps, just north of the Keys (The Big Lead).

Just over $1,000 from the safe and a special SMG from the dock building (The Big Lead). A duffel bag with $10K is only a player report so far.
