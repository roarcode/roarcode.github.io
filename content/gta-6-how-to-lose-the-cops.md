# How to Lose the Cops in GTA 6: 6 Clues to Hide

> - **Quick answer:** the police do not magically know where you are. They follow six clues: your **look, outfit, visible weapon, vehicle, CCTV footage and your partner** (GameSpot).
> - **Hide every clue without the cops seeing you do it, and they cannot find you** (GameSpot).
> - **Fast fixes:** hat and bandana, spare outfit in the trunk, put the gun away, switch cars, destroy the CCTV evidence, split up.

**Words used on this page.** *Wanted level* is the star rating that shows how hard the police are chasing you. *Heat indicator* is a clue that tells the police who you are. *CCTV* means security cameras. *Trunk* is the boot of a car, where you can keep things. *Evidence* means proof, like camera footage.

*This page has no story spoilers.*

## The six clues and how to hide each one

GameSpot says the wanted system was reworked. The police do not know where you are when you commit a crime. They need clues. Here is GameSpot's list of the clues and the fix for each:

| Clue | The fix | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance | Wear a hat and bandana for the crime, then take them off | GameSpot |
| Outfit | Keep a spare outfit in the trunk and change at the car, or buy a new outfit in a clothing store | GameSpot |
| Visible weapon | Put your pistol away. Stow or drop long guns (dropped guns you own return to the trunk) | GameSpot |
| Vehicle | Leave the car or swap to another one | GameSpot |
| CCTV footage | Destroy the evidence in the back room | GameSpot |
| Your partner | Split up | GameSpot |

GameSpot says that as long as you deal with all of these **without the cops seeing you do it**, they cannot find you.

## Step by step after a crime

1. Get out of sight first. Put distance between you and the place.
2. Cover your face if you can, and change your outfit.
3. Put your gun away.
4. Leave the car or change it. GameSpot's robbery guide says a **Pay 'n' Spray** paint job also helps.
5. If you were in a job together, **split up**. GameSpot says the cops then have two moving targets, and if one gets caught the other keeps their loot.
6. Check that you were not spotted. The police can still see you doing the changes.

## Camera footage

GameSpot's robbery guide says that being caught on camera does not alert the cops straight away. But if you do not destroy the evidence, the police can identify you when you leave. The alarm does alert them right away.

## Time of day

VGC says police response is different at night. It says planning crimes for when the streets are empty may make it easier for you.

## Quiet guns

GameSpot says police will come if someone reports gunshots, so the quieter you are, the more time you have. It says a silencer helps with that.

## FAQ

### How do you lose the police in GTA 6?
Hide the six clues: look, outfit, visible weapon, vehicle, CCTV footage and your partner (GameSpot).

### Do the cops always know where I am?
No. GameSpot says they do not automatically know where you are after a crime.

### What if I reach a very high wanted level?
PC Gamer says at six stars you can no longer lose your wanted level. This page is about the normal levels.
