# How to Heal in GTA 6: Every Way to Get Health Back

> - **Quick answer:** use **Zombix** or **Adrenaline** items. Food and drinks can heal too (GameSpot).
> - **No items?** Stay out of trouble for a short time and your health refills slowly, up to the solid white line on the health bar (GameSpot).
> - **Watch out:** pills get weaker if you take them again and again in a fight (GameSpot).

**Words used on this page.** *Health bar* is the bar that shows how hurt you are. *Regeneration* (or regen) means your health fills up by itself. *Stamina* is the bar that shows how tired you are. *Tolerance* means something works less well each time you use it. *Cover* means hiding behind a wall or car.

## Ways to heal

| Way | What it does | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Zombix or Adrenaline items | The main healing items | GameSpot |
| Food and drink (pink heart icon) | Restores health up to the maximum | GameSpot |
| Food and drink (orange heart icon) | Gives a temporary boost above the normal maximum | GameSpot |
| Waiting | Health fills slowly if you take no damage, up to the solid white line | GameSpot |

GameSpot says it has seen no injuries that need special treatment, such as broken legs. Items and food are the answer to everything it has found so far.

## How to use a healing item

1. Press and hold **L1** on PS5.
2. While you hold it, press **Square** (Beebom). VGC also names L1 and Square for this.
3. Xbox buttons for this are **not stated** in the sources we checked.

## Slow healing and your stamina

GameSpot says that if you have no items you can just avoid damage for a while and your health bar refills slowly. It will not refill past the solid white line. For more health than that you need a recovery item.

Stamina matters too. GameSpot says low stamina makes your health come back more slowly. You can raise your stamina by working out, drinking energy drinks and sleeping enough. GameSpot's tips page says exercise such as running, biking or kayaking trains stamina.

## The tolerance trap

GameSpot says that if you take pills in the middle of a gunfight, they slowly work less well. In the end you have to use cover instead.

## FAQ

### How do you heal in GTA 6?
Use Zombix or Adrenaline items, or food and drink. You can also wait and let your health refill slowly.

### What do the heart colours mean?
A pink heart restores health up to the maximum. An orange heart gives a temporary boost above it (GameSpot).

### Do you need a hospital?
GameSpot has not found any injury that needs one.

### Why is my health not refilling?
It stops at the solid white line on the bar. Low stamina also slows it down (GameSpot).
