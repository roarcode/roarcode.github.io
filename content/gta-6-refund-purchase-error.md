# GTA 6 Refund: Pre-Order Error and Payment Failed Fixes

> - **Quick answer:** Ask the **store that took your money**. PlayStation: Order details > **Request Refund**. Xbox: Xbox Support > **Request a refund**. Rockstar Store: **Xsolla**. Other shops: that shop.
> - **PlayStation pre-order rule:** the main game must **not be downloaded** yet, and you must ask before the release date or within 14 days (PlayStation).
> - **Money questions go to the seller.** Gameplay problems, patches and bugs go to Rockstar Support (PlayStation's GTA VI page says so).

**Words used on this page.** A *refund* is when you get your money back. A *pre-order* is buying a game before it is out. *Payment failed* means the shop could not take your card. *Order history* is the list of things you bought.

## PlayStation: ask for a refund

PlayStation's steps:

1. Open your **Order details** in PlayStation Store.
2. Select the **eligible transaction**.
3. Select **Request Refund**.
4. Follow the on-screen steps.
5. Still stuck? Contact PlayStation Support.

**Rules for pre-orders (PlayStation):**
- Bought **more than 14 days before release:** ask **before the release date**, and the main game must **not be downloaded**.
- Bought **less than 14 days before release:** ask within **14 days of buying**, and the main game must **not be downloaded**.
- A pre-load may count as downloading. PlayStation's page does not say. Ask PlayStation Support before you pre-load if a refund matters to you.

## Charged but no game in your library?

PlayStation Support lists "your account", "game download issues" and "digital refunds" as things it can help with. Check **Order History** first. Open your avatar menu and select **Order History**. Check the order shows as successful.

## Xbox: ask for a refund

1. Open Xbox Support's **Request a refund for digital games** page and sign in with the account that bought the game.
2. Select the item.
3. Select **Request a refund**.
4. Give a reason and select **Next**.
5. Check and select **Submit**.

Microsoft says a request can take up to **72 hours**. If it is approved, you lose access to the game while it processes. Xbox's page says to sign in and check your **billing info** before a pre-ordered game launches.

- **Any other shop:** Rockstar says purchase, refund and delivery questions go to that shop.

## FAQ

### Where do I get a GTA 6 refund?
From the seller. PlayStation Store: Request Refund in Order details. Xbox: Request a refund on Xbox Support. Rockstar Store: Xsolla. Other shops: that shop.

### My payment failed. Was I charged?
Check Order History (PlayStation) or your Microsoft account billing. If you were charged and there is no game, contact the store's support.

### Can I get a refund after I pre-load?
PlayStation requires that the main game is not downloaded. It does not say if a pre-load counts. Ask PlayStation Support.
