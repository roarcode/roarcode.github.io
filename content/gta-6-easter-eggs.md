# GTA 6 Easter Eggs: 5 Secrets Found So Far (Spoilers)

> - **Quick answer:** GameSpot's list has **5 easter eggs** so far.
> - **GameSpot says there are many more.** It is adding to its list as players find them.
> - **Every egg is in a closed box.** Open only the ones you want.

⚠️ **SPOILER WARNING:** these are small secrets, not story twists. Tap a box only if you want to know where an egg is.

**Words used on this page.** *Easter egg* is a hidden nod or surprise in a game. *Reference* means a small mention of something else, like another game. *Island* here means one area of the map. *Footage credit* means who recorded the video.

We keep this page short and only list eggs that a reputable outlet has described. We do not use single posts from fans. Rumours stay out.

## The list (GameSpot)

:::spoiler 1. A nod to another Rockstar game
GameSpot says the world has several reminders that Red Dead Redemption and Grand Theft Auto share one story universe. One of the early ones is a Western novel about a cowboy in New Austin, a place from the Red Dead series. GameSpot says the example it pictured is in Jacob's Tavern in Millport, and that you can read excerpts from books and magazines in the game.
:::

:::spoiler 2. A book in Jason's home
GameSpot says that early in Chapter 1, when you first play as Jason, there is a memoir on his small table near the back door. It is by a former member of Liberty City's organised crime. GameSpot says the book mentions events from 2008, which seems to be a nod to GTA 4, set in 2008.
:::

:::spoiler 3. A TV character's flat
GameSpot says Dexter Morgan's apartment is on Washington Island, a part of Vice City, near the Vice Beach Top Fun Drones shop. You cannot go inside. GameSpot says some players think a boat docked nearby looks like his boat.
:::

:::spoiler 4. A very tall climb
GameSpot says a real egg sits atop the tallest tower in Vice City. The player who found it said the climb took about seven minutes. GameSpot says you can take the egg and throw it, and it does not say what happens next.
:::

:::spoiler 5. Bigfoot
GameSpot says Bigfoot is at Gulley Marsh and is unmissable because of a particular mission. GameRant separately says he can be found south of Cottonmouth Lake as soon as the first mission is done.
:::

## How to hunt for easter eggs

1. Read books, magazines and posters you find in homes. GameSpot says you can read excerpts.
2. Look at shop items and place names. GameSpot says eggs appear in books, store items, dialogue and places of interest.
3. Use the map to see which buildings can be entered (VGC says darker blue buildings have an inside).
4. Take your time. GameSpot says it has only just begun to gather them.
